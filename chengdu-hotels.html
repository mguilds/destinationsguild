Chengdu’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Chengdu — Best Areas & Hotels

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Sichuan’s laid-back capital is all teahouses, hotpot and easy days — and where you stay sets the pace. Do you want the shopping buzz of Chunxi Road, the lantern-lit lanes of Kuanzhai Alley, or a quiet base for a morning with the pandas? We’ve grouped Chengdu’s most enduring, well-run hotels by style and character, each with an honest note and a Book Me link that opens that exact property on Trip.com so you can just choose your dates.

Luxury

Chengdu’s luxury hotels rise over the central shopping and business districts, pairing five-star polish and superb dining with easy access to Chunxi Road and the metro. Book here when you want the hotel to be a highlight of the trip.

$$$$ LuxuryCity centreRooftop poolFine dining

A polished, central flagship near Tianfu Square with large, elegant rooms, a beautiful rooftop pool and bar, an excellent spa and some of the city’s finest restaurants. Service is warm and assured, and the location puts the metro and Chunxi Road within reach. The definitive luxury choice for travellers who want a refined base in the heart of Chengdu.

Boutique

Chengdu’s boutique hotels put design and neighbourhood soul first — a courtyard set among restored Qing lanes, a jewel-box hideaway near the old town. Ideal for travellers who want atmosphere, style and a walkable base near the teahouses.

Budget & Value

Chengdu is easy on the wallet: a clean, central room near the old town or a transport hub costs little, and leaves more for hotpot, teahouses and the pandas. Trade frills for a good location and reliable comfort.
