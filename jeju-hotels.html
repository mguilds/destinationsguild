Jeju’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Jeju — Best Areas & Hotels

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Jeju is Korea’s holiday island, and where you stay sets the mood. The resort strip of Jungmun on the south coast has the beaches and the big five-star names; the town of Seogwipo nearby adds waterfalls and markets; and Jeju City in the north puts you by the airport and the ferries. We’ve grouped the island’s best-run hotels by the kind of stay you’re after — including beachfront, resort and spa options — each with an honest note and a Book Me link that opens that exact property on Trip.com.

Luxury

Jeju’s finest hotels sit on the resort coast at Jungmun or in the new integrated-resort towers, trading on ocean views, big grounds and destination dining. Book here when the resort itself is the holiday.

The Shilla Jeju

$$$$ LuxuryJungmunCliffside gardensOcean views

A perennial favourite among Korea’s grand resorts, The Shilla commands a cliff-top spot in Jungmun with sub-tropical gardens, sweeping ocean views, an outdoor pool and impeccable service honed over decades. It’s walking distance to Jungmun Beach and the resort attractions, and a serene, polished base for a special-occasion stay. The benchmark for old-guard luxury on the island.

Grand Hyatt Jeju

$$$$ LuxuryJeju CityDream TowerInfinity pools

The centrepiece of the Jeju Dream Tower integrated resort in Jeju City, this soaring twin-tower hotel brings big-city glamour to the island — huge rooms with sea or mountain views, a spectacular indoor-outdoor infinity pool, and one of Korea’s largest collections of restaurants and bars under one roof. Handy for the airport and the north coast. A modern, high-energy luxury choice.

Boutique

Jeju has a real design streak, and its boutique stays play to the island’s nature — architect-designed retreats and small resorts that trade big-hotel bustle for calm and character.

The Seaes Hotel & Resort

$$$ BoutiqueJungmunThatched cottagesOcean-front

A charming low-rise retreat in Jungmun built around traditional Jeju stone-and-thatch cottages set in gardens above the sea, blending island heritage with boutique comfort and an ocean-front pool. Rooms feel private and individual rather than cookie-cutter, and the setting is quiet and romantic. A distinctive, character-filled alternative to the big resort towers.

Budget & Value

Jeju City, near the airport, is where the value sits — comfortable ocean-front and business hotels at a fraction of the Jungmun resort rates, handy for a first or last island night. We list the verified pick we can stand behind; browse the link for more.

Ramada Plaza by Wyndham Jeju Ocean Front

$$ ValueJeju CitySeafrontNear the airport

A large, well-priced ocean-front hotel on the Jeju City waterfront, a short hop from the airport, with sea-view rooms, a pool and easy access to the northern coast, the port and the city’s restaurants. It won’t match the Jungmun resorts for grounds, but for comfort, a sea view and value near the transport hub it’s a sensible choice. Good for an arrival or departure night, or a city-side base.

Family-friendly

Spa & pool

Jeju’s clean air and volcanic water make it a natural for wellness, and a few hotels put the spa and the pool at the very centre of the stay.

WE Hotel Jeju

$$$$ WellnessSeogwipoMedical spaHallasan foothills

An architect-designed wellness hotel in the foothills below Hallasan, WE Hotel is built around water — a serious medical-and-hydro spa, thermal and swimming pools fed by Jeju’s famed volcanic water, and calm, nature-facing rooms. It’s a retreat for genuine rest and rejuvenation rather than beach buzz. The pick for a health-and-spa focused escape.
