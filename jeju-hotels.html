Where to Stay in Jeju — Best Areas & Hotels

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Jeju is a volcanic island the size of a small country, so the kind of stay you choose matters as much as the address. Do you want a skyline suite in Jeju City near the airport, a cliff-top resort above Jungmun’s beaches, a famous spa hotel in Seogwipo, or a quiet boutique retreat out east near the Sunrise Peak? Luxury

Jeju’s finest hotels split between the glossy new towers of Jeju City and the long-established landmark resorts above Jungmun’s coast. Book here when you want polished service, a memorable view and the hotel to feel like part of the holiday.

$$$$ LuxuryJeju CityDream TowerSea & city views

Jeju Hotels — Frequently Asked

What is the best area to stay in Jeju?

It depends on your trip. Jeju City in the north is closest to the airport and has the seafront, markets, casinos and easy dining, so it suits short stays and first arrivals. The south coast around Jungmun and Seogwipo is the resort belt — warmer, greener and home to the big spa hotels, waterfalls and the finest beaches — and is better for a relaxed multi-night stay. The east, near Seongsan Sunrise Peak, suits travelers chasing scenery and a quieter pace.

Is Jeju walkable, or do you need a car?

Jeju is a large volcanic island and its sights are spread out, so it is not walkable end to end. Most visitors rent a car, and Jeju has plenty of rental desks at the airport; taxis and an extensive local bus network are good alternatives if you would rather not drive. Within a single base such as Jungmun resort or downtown Jeju City you can happily walk to restaurants, beaches and shops.

When is the best time to visit Jeju?

Spring, roughly April and May, is the classic choice for canola-flower fields and cherry blossoms in mild weather, and autumn from September to October brings clear skies and comfortable temperatures. Summer is warm, green and busy with Korean holidaymakers, especially around the beaches, while winter stays fairly mild by Korean standards and is quiet, with camellias and the occasional dusting of snow on Hallasan.
