Where to Stay in Langkawi

Where to Stay in Langkawi — Best Hotels & Areas

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Langkawi is Malaysia’s duty-free island escape — rainforest-clad hills tumbling to white-sand bays on the Andaman Sea. The lively Pantai Cenang strip has the buzz, the restaurants and the water sports; the quieter northern and western coves hold some of Asia’s finest resorts. Below are Langkawi’s standout stays by style, each with an honest note and a Book Me link straight to Trip.com.

Luxury

Langkawi is one of Asia’s great resort islands, with rainforest and beachfront retreats to match anywhere.

The Datai Langkawi

$$$$ LuxuryRainforestPrivate beachIconic

Set deep in ancient rainforest above a secluded bay in the island’s north-west, The Datai is a byword for barefoot luxury — villas among the treetops, a beautiful beach club and superb nature guiding. Serene, sophisticated and utterly private. The island’s signature special-occasion stay.
