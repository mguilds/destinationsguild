Mediterranean Cruises

A Mediterranean cruise is a moving history lesson — Rome, Barcelona and Athens in one trip, with a new ancient city at breakfast most mornings. The Western loop favors Spain, France and Italy; the Eastern loop leans into the Greek Isles, the Adriatic and Turkey.

Best time to cruise

April to October, with the shoulders — May and September — the connoisseur’s pick: warm seas, thinner crowds and gentler prices. July and August are hot and busy but deliver the longest beach days.
