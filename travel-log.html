Frequently Asked Questions

Three straight answers about what the Log is and is not.

What is the Travel Log, and how is it different from the Guild Travel Library?

The Guild Travel Library holds the books: long, researched country and city guides that take weeks to put together and are meant to be read before you go. The Travel Log holds everything in between. A dispatch is short, it is dated, and it is written by whoever was actually standing in the place — usually about one thing rather than a whole country: which Tokyo neighbourhood to sleep in, how the Cyclades ferries really connect, which month puts you alongside the migration in the Serengeti. Think of the Library as the shelf and the Log as the postcards tucked into the back of the books.

How many dispatches are there so far, and where do the “Read more” links go?

Three opening entries, and we would rather say so plainly than dress it up as an archive. The Log launched with three cards written by the Guild: A First-Timer’s Guide to Tokyo’s Neighborhoods dated June 2, 2026, Greek Island Hopping: Santorini to Milos dated May 28, 2026, and Safari Season: When to Visit the Serengeti dated May 20, 2026 — each marked Polly’s Pick. Their Read more links currently open live listings on Trip.com for the destination in question rather than a finished article, because the full write-ups are still being filed. As each dispatch lands, its card will point to the piece itself.

Can I write a dispatch for the Travel Log?

Yes. The Log exists to carry first-hand notes from people who were there, and we take submissions from travellers who are not on staff. What we look for, how long a dispatch should run and how to send one are set out on Become a Guild Reporter. Short and specific beats long and general: one neighbourhood, one ferry route, one month of the year, written the way you would tell a friend who is about to make the same trip.
