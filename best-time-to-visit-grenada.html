Grenada, month by month

Best Time to Visit Grenada

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated November 2026

Grenada has two seasons and a sharp price ladder laid over them. The dry season runs from about January to May; the rains and the Atlantic hurricane season run from June to November. The weather is only half the question, though, because the island’s own calendar — Independence Day in February, Spicemas in August, the regattas — decides whether a room exists at all. Below is the year as we would actually plan it, and where a festival date comes from an organiser we say which one. Where nobody has published a date yet, we say that too.

The short answer

Best weather

February, with January and March just behind it. Dry, breezy and dependable — and the dearest and busiest weeks of the year.

Best value

Late April into May, and the first half of December. Dry-season weather at shoulder-season prices, with room on the beach.

Cheapest

September, which is also the peak of the hurricane season and the month when some small places close. Travel insured and flexible.

For the carnival

The second week of August. Spicemas Corporation puts J’Ouvert on Monday 9 August 2027 and Fancy Mas on Tuesday 10 August 2027.

Grenada month by month

We have deliberately not printed an average temperature for each month. We have no Grenadian weather station figures we can stand behind, and an invented number is worse than none — so each card says what the month is actually like to travel in.

January

Dry season · peak rates

The most reliable month of the year and one of the busiest. The trade wind blows steadily, showers are brief, and the island is full of visitors out of the northern winter. Rooms are at or near their highest and the small hotels want booking well ahead. Grenada Sailing Week usually falls at the end of the month.

February

Dry season · peak rates

Much like January and the single busiest month on the main island, with Independence Day on the seventh adding flags, parades and school events across the parishes. Dry, breezy and expensive. If February is the only month you can travel, book early and expect no bargains.

March

Dry season · high rates

Still firmly in the dry season and a little hotter as the month goes on. The sea is calm on the south and west coasts, visibility for divers is good and the hillsides are at their driest. Rates begin to ease towards the end of the month once the northern school holidays are past.

April

Shoulder · good value

One of the two best weeks-to-go windows in our view: the weather is still dry season weather, the crowds have thinned and rooms have come off their winter prices. Late April is warm without being heavy, and the island feels like itself again rather than like a high-season resort.

May

Shoulder · good value

The last of the dry season and often lovely, with the first proper showers arriving towards the end of the month. Prices are low, the gardens are greening up and the sea is warm. Our other pick, alongside early December, for a visitor who wants good weather without paying February money.

June

Rainy season begins · low rates

The rains set in and the hurricane season opens on the first of the month. Showers are heavy and short rather than all-day, mornings are often clear, and the island is quiet and cheap. Carriacou travellers should note that Hurricane Beryl struck on 1 July 2024, early in the season rather than at its peak.

July

Rainy · low rates · festival build-up

Hot, humid and green, with real downpours. Prices stay low and the island begins to fill with the sound of Spicemas rehearsals and panyard practice. The Carriacou Regatta Festival traditionally falls at the end of the month, though no organiser has published a 2027 date.

August

Carnival · rooms scarce

Spicemas week is the island’s own holiday and the one time of year when a room is genuinely hard to find at any price. Spicemas Corporation publishes J’Ouvert and Monday Night Mas on Monday 9 August 2027 and Fancy Mas on Tuesday 10 August 2027. Come for the carnival or come in a different month, but do not arrive in carnival week expecting a quiet beach.

September

Peak hurricane month · cheapest

Statistically the most active part of the Atlantic season and the quietest month for visitors. Some small hotels and restaurants close for a few weeks. If you travel now, take insurance that covers weather disruption and book something flexible. Hurricane Ivan crossed the island in September 2004.

October

Still wet · cruise season opens

The rains ease towards the end of the month and the cruise season begins — the Grenada Ports Authority describes the Caribbean season as running from October to April. Rooms are still cheap, the island is at its greenest, and the risk of a storm is falling but not gone.

November

Season turning · fair value

The hurricane season closes on the thirtieth and the weather settles week by week. A good month for walking in the interior while everything is still green. The Grenada Tourism Authority lists the Grenada Flower and Garden Festival on 8 to 15 November 2026.

December

First half is the sweet spot

The first two weeks are our favourite time to be here: dry-season weather arriving, the island unhurried and rates still at shoulder-season level. From about the twentieth that reverses sharply and Christmas and New Year are the dearest nights of the year. The Tourism Authority lists Carols by Candlelight on 6 December 2026, with the date to be confirmed.

Rain, wind and hurricanes

The honest version, because this is the part of the year people get wrong in both directions. Grenada sits at about twelve degrees north, towards the southern end of the hurricane belt, and most seasons pass with the big storms tracking well to the north of it. That is a real advantage over the islands further up the chain and it is why the brochures say “below the hurricane belt”. It is not the same as being outside it. Hurricane Ivan crossed the main island in September 2004 and did enormous damage to buildings and to the nutmeg crop, and Hurricane Beryl struck Carriacou and Petite Martinique on 1 July 2024 — at the very start of a season rather than at its peak. The Grenada Tourism Authority still carries a notice on its own front page saying that Carriacou and Petite Martinique were affected by Beryl and have started welcoming visitors once again.

Practically: the season runs 1 June to 30 November and September is usually its most active month. Rain in the wet half of the year mostly arrives as a heavy shower that passes, and the mornings are often clear. The trade wind blows hardest from December to April, which keeps the heat honest and makes the Atlantic side of the island choppy. If you travel between June and November, take travel insurance that covers weather disruption, book a room with some flexibility, and watch the forecast in the week before you fly.

Events worth timing a trip around

Every date below is either taken from the organiser’s own site or marked as unpublished. We would rather leave a gap than print a date that sends you to the wrong week.

Grenada Sailing Week

31 Jan – 5 Feb 2027

Racing out of Port Louis Marina, and a good week to be in St George’s even if you never set foot on a boat. The date is from the Grenada Sailing Club’s own event calendar; the regatta’s own website still showed January 2025 when we checked and had published nothing for 2027, so confirm with the organisers if your trip depends on it.

Independence Day

7 February

Grenada became independent on 7 February 1974, and the anniversary is the national holiday: parades, school events and flags across the parishes in the week leading up to it. It falls in the busiest fortnight of the year, so book ahead.

Spicemas

5 – 10 August 2027

The carnival, and the island’s own week. Spicemas Corporation publishes the National Carnival Queen Pageant on Thursday 5 August 2027, Soca Monarch on Friday the 6th, Panorama on Saturday the 7th, Dimanche Gras on Sunday the 8th, J’Ouvert and Monday Night Mas on Monday the 9th and Fancy Mas on Tuesday the 10th. Several other events on its calendar are still marked to be confirmed.

Carriacou Regatta Festival

No 2027 date published

The sloop racing and the sister island’s big weekend, traditionally at the end of July or the start of August. The Tourism Authority’s own page for it gives 31 July to 2 August with no year attached and the note “date to be confirmed”, so we are not going to pretend we know the 2027 dates.

Grenada Flower and Garden Festival

8 – 15 November 2026

Listed with those dates on the Grenada Tourism Authority’s own events page. A gentle, local sort of week at a good time of year for the interior of the island.

Carols by Candlelight

6 December 2026, to be confirmed

Also from the Tourism Authority’s events page, which marks the date as to be confirmed. It falls in our favourite fortnight of the year to be here.

Maroon and String Band Festival

No official date found

Carriacou’s drumming, big-drum and string-band festival, usually spoken of as an April event. We could find no date published by an organiser or by the Tourism Authority when this page was written. Ask locally before booking around it.

Grenada Chocolate Fest

No official date found

A week of estate tours and tastings built on the island’s cocoa, generally held in the spring. Again, no organiser date was published where we could read it, so treat any date you see on a booking site as unconfirmed.

Prices and what is open

Grenada’s price year has four steps rather than two. Christmas and New Year are the dearest nights of all and want booking months out. January to mid-April is high season: everything open, everything busy, rates near the top. Mid-April to the end of May and the first half of December are the shoulder, and this is where the value sits — the weather is still good and the number on the invoice is noticeably smaller. June to November is low season: the cheapest rooms of the year, the greenest island, and a handful of small hotels and restaurants closed for a few weeks, usually around September. Spicemas week in August is its own category, when demand has nothing to do with the weather.

Two practical notes. A government tax and a service charge are normally added to hotel and restaurant bills in Grenada, so ask what is included rather than reading the headline rate. And on Carriacou, where the stock is guest houses and cottages rather than resorts, ring or email the property directly before you commit — it is a small island, Beryl changed what is open, and the owner will tell you straight.

Timing it in your later years

Three thoughts, from people who travel this way themselves. First, avoid the heat and humidity of August and September if you feel them: the comfortable months are the dry-season ones, December through to May, when the trade wind does the work of air conditioning. Second, the shoulder weeks in late April, May and early December are not just cheaper, they are emptier, which means shorter queues at immigration, a quieter beach and taxi drivers with time to talk. Third, if a storm would be more than an inconvenience — a cancelled flight, a change of hotel, a few days indoors — then simply do not book between June and November, and spend the saving on a better room in April. Best time to visit Grenada — questions

What is the single best month to visit Grenada?

If you want the most reliable weather and do not mind the price, February. If you want the best balance of weather, price and space on the beach, late April into May, or the first half of December. Those two windows sit just outside the peak season, which is why they work: the dry-season trade wind is still blowing, the rain has not arrived, and the rooms have not yet gone to their winter rates.

When is Grenada's rainy season, and is it a reason not to go?

Roughly June to November, and no, not on its own. Grenadian rain in those months tends to arrive as a heavy shower that clears rather than a grey day, and mornings are often bright. The island is at its greenest, the waterfalls are full, and the hotels are at their cheapest. The real reason to think twice about those months is the hurricane season rather than the rain itself.

Does Grenada get hurricanes?

Yes. Grenada lies at about twelve degrees north, towards the southern edge of the belt, so in most years the storms track well to the north. But the island has been hit hard twice in living memory: Hurricane Ivan crossed the main island in September 2004, and Hurricane Beryl struck Carriacou and Petite Martinique on 1 July 2024. The Grenada Tourism Authority still carries a notice saying those two islands were affected by Beryl and have started welcoming visitors once again. The season runs from 1 June to 30 November, with September usually the most active month.

When is Spicemas, and should I plan a trip around it?

Spicemas is Grenada's carnival and it runs over the second weekend of August into Carnival Monday and Tuesday. For 2027 the organiser, Spicemas Corporation, publishes the National Carnival Queen Pageant on Thursday 5 August, Soca Monarch on Friday 6 August, Panorama on Saturday 7 August, Dimanche Gras on Sunday 8 August, J'Ouvert and Monday Night Mas on Monday 9 August, and Fancy Mas on Tuesday 10 August. If carnival is what you have come for, plan it like a sporting fixture: flights and rooms go early and prices do not soften. If it is not, pick another month, because the whole island is pointed at the parade that week.

Are there festival dates nobody has published yet?

Several, and we would rather say so than print a guess. When this page was written the Grenada Tourism Authority's own events listing carried only two dated entries, both for 2026: the Flower and Garden Festival on 8 to 15 November and Carols by Candlelight on 6 December, the latter marked date to be confirmed. Its Carriacou Regatta Festival page gives 31 July to 2 August with no year attached and the note date to be confirmed. We found no official published dates for the Carriacou Maroon and String Band Music Festival or for the Grenada Chocolate Fest. Check with the organisers before you book flights around any of them.

When is Grenada Sailing Week?

The Grenada Sailing Club's own event calendar lists Grenada Sailing Week for 31 January to 5 February 2027, based at Port Louis Marina. That is a club listing rather than the regatta's own announcement: the regatta's website still showed its January 2025 dates when we checked and had published nothing for 2027. If your trip depends on the racing, confirm it with the organisers.

When are rooms cheapest in Grenada?

September is the cheapest month, and the quietest, for the reasons above. After that the low rates run from about June through to the middle of November. The genuinely good value, as opposed to the lowest number, sits in late April and May and in the first two weeks of December, when you are paying shoulder-season prices for dry-season weather. Christmas, New Year and February are the dearest, and Spicemas week is the hardest to book at all.
