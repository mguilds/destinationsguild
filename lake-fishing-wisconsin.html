Lake Fishing in Wisconsin — Six Great Fishing Lakes and the Towns Beside Them

Lake Winnebago, Green Bay, Geneva Lake, the Chippewa Flowage, Lac Vieux Desert and Big Green Lake: what swims in each, how people fish them in summer and through the ice, the 2026–27 rules, and where to sleep.

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Checked against the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources, 7 November 2026

Wisconsin has thousands of lakes, and its anglers take them seriously — this is the state of the muskie, of February sturgeon spearing on Lake Winnebago, and of the Friday fish fry. We have chosen six waters that each show a different side of it: a vast walleye lake in the farm country, a bay of Lake Michigan, a deep, clear lake an easy drive from Chicago, a wild Northwoods flowage, a lake on the Michigan border, and the lake its own chamber of commerce calls the deepest natural inland lake in the state.

This guide belongs to The Guild Fishing Library. It is written for families with a spinning rod, boat anglers, ice anglers and anyone who wants a lake, a cabin and perhaps a perch for supper. Wisconsin’s rules change from one lake to the next more than almost any other state’s, so we have quoted the Wisconsin DNR wherever a number is involved. Our neighbouring guides cover lake fishing in Michigan and lake fishing in Minnesota.

Lake Winnebago — Wisconsin’s largest lake

A huge, shallow lake between Oshkosh, Fond du Lac and Neenah, joined by the Fox and Wolf rivers to the “upriver lakes” — Butte des Morts, Winneconne and Poygan. Together the DNR calls them the Winnebago System, and they keep their own rules.

Lake Winnebago, from Oshkosh, Fond du Lac and Neenah

What swims there. Walleye, sauger, white bass, yellow perch, bass and catfish — and lake sturgeon, the ancient fish the lake is famous for. Wisconsin’s record yellow perch, 3 lb 4 oz, came from Lake Winnebago in 1954.

How people fish it. Trolling and jigging for walleye from boats (motor trolling is allowed with three lines per person), casting the reefs, and a very large ice-fishing community in winter when the ice allows. It is a wide, shallow lake that builds big waves quickly in wind — watch the forecast in a small boat.

Keeping a fish. Walleye and sauger are open all year here: three a day in total, only one of them a sauger or hybrid, with no minimum length. Bass: open all year, five, at least 14 inches. Panfish: 25 together. Northern pike: two, at least 26 inches, 2 May 2026 to 7 March 2027. Muskellunge: one, at least 50 inches, 2 May to 31 December 2026. Lake sturgeon may not be fished with hook and line on the Winnebago System at any time.

Confirmed with the Wisconsin DNR, 7 November 2026 — Guide to Wisconsin Hook and Line Fishing Regulations, 2026–2027, Winnebago System Waters (pages 94–95): “WALLEYE and SAUGER (INCLUDES HYBRIDS) — Open all year — 3 in total; only 1 may be a sauger or hybrid — None”; “LARGEMOUTH and SMALLMOUTH BASS — Open all year — 5 in total — 14″”; “MUSKELLUNGE — May 2, 2026 — Dec. 31, 2026 — 1 — 50″”; “NORTHERN PIKE — May 2, 2026 — March 7, 2027 — 2 — 26″”; “LAKE STURGEON … Hook and line fishing closed all year”; “Motor trolling is allowed on all Winnebago System waters with three hooks, baits or lures per person.” State record list: “Perch, Yellow 3 lbs. 4 oz. 1954 Lake Winnebago, Winnebago Co.”

Sturgeon spearing. Each February the DNR opens a short spearing season through the ice. In 2026 it ran from 14 February to 1 March on Lake Winnebago, 7 a.m. to 1 p.m. each day, or until a harvest cap was reached. A Lake Winnebago spearing licence costs $20 for residents and $65 for visitors, spearers must be 12 or older, and the licence must be bought by 31 October before the season. The upriver lakes are a separate draw, with applications due 1 August.

Confirmed with the Wisconsin DNR, 7 November 2026 — Winnebago System Sturgeon Spearing Regulations, 2026: “Feb. 14 through March 1, 2026 on Lake Winnebago, or until any of the pre-set harvest caps for Lake Winnebago are reached … from 7 a.m. to 1 p.m. each day”; “residents must purchase a sturgeon spearing license for $20 and nonresidents must purchase a sturgeon spearing license for $65”; “Only persons 12 years of age and older are eligible to spear sturgeon.” DNR licence page: “Sturgeon Spearing Lake Winnebago (deadline to purchase October 31)”.

Boat launches. High Cliff State Park, on the north-east shore, is the only state-owned recreation area on the lake and has a marina with more than 100 slips and fuel; a state park vehicle sticker is needed. Oshkosh, Fond du Lac and the shoreline towns also run public launches.

Nearest towns: Oshkosh on the west shore, Fond du Lac at the south end and Neenah at the north end. Appleton is close to High Cliff as well.

Green Bay — walleye and perch on the big water

The long bay of Lake Michigan between the Door County peninsula and the city of Green Bay. For fishing purposes the DNR counts it as Lake Michigan water, with its own seasons.

Green Bay, from the city of Green Bay and Sturgeon Bay

What swims there. Walleye, yellow perch, smallmouth bass, northern pike and Great Lakes muskellunge — the fish the DNR’s Green Bay rules are written for.

How people fish it. Trolling and casting for walleye from boats, perch fishing over the flats, casting for smallmouth around the rocks of Sturgeon Bay and Door County, and ice fishing on the bay in a hard winter. Open-bay ice shifts with wind and current; go with someone who knows that day’s ice.

Keeping a fish. Walleye: five a day, at least 15 inches, from 2 May 2026 to 7 March 2027; outside those dates only one, at least 15 inches. Yellow perch: 15 a day, 20 May 2026 to 15 March 2027. Smallmouth bass may be kept from 1 July (five with largemouth, at least 14 inches). Muskellunge: one, at least 54 inches, 23 May to 31 December 2026. Northern pike: five, no minimum, all year. Lake sturgeon: closed. Trout and salmon need a Great Lakes Trout and Salmon stamp.

Confirmed with the Wisconsin DNR, 7 November 2026 — Hook and Line Guide 2026–2027, Lake Michigan (“Includes the Bay of Green Bay”), pages 88–90: walleye, “Lake Michigan Waters North of Waldo Boulevard, Manitowoc — March 2, 2026 – May 1, 2026 — 1 in total — 15 inches; May 2, 2026 – March 7, 2027 — 5 in total — 15 inches; March 8, 2027 – April 30, 2027 — 1 in total — 15 inches”; “YELLOW PERCH in Green Bay and the Fox River — Open: May 20, 2026 – March 15, 2027 — 15”; “SMALLMOUTH BASS HARVEST — Green Bay and waters within 5 miles of the Door County shoreline — July 1, 2026 – March 7, 2027 — 5 in total — 14 inches”; muskellunge north of Waldo Boulevard “May 23, 2026 – Dec. 31, 2026 (on open water only) — 1 — 54 inches”; northern pike north of Waldo Boulevard “Open all year — 5 — None”; “Anglers must carry a paper copy of their license/stamps while fishing on Lake Michigan.”

Carry your licence on paper. On Lake Michigan and Green Bay the DNR requires a paper copy of your licence and stamps — a phone screen is not enough. Ice shelters on the bay must be taken off each day after the first Sunday following 12 March.

Boat launches. Brown County Parks runs four launches, including Bay Shore Park on the east shore and Suamico on the west; a launch fee is charged. In Door County, Potawatomi State Park sits on the shore of Sturgeon Bay.

Nearest towns: Green Bay at the foot of the bay, and Sturgeon Bay, the gateway to Door County, for the eastern shore.

Geneva Lake — deep, clear and close to Chicago

A deep, clear lake in Walworth County in the far south of the state, ringed by the towns of Lake Geneva, Fontana-on-Geneva Lake and Williams Bay. The DNR’s rule book calls it “Geneva Lake” in one place and “Lake Geneva” in another; it is the same water.

Geneva Lake, from Lake Geneva, Fontana and Williams Bay

What swims there. Lake trout and other trout in the deep cold water, smallmouth and largemouth bass, northern pike, walleye, muskellunge and panfish. Wisconsin’s inland record brown trout, 18 lb 6 oz, was caught here in 1984.

How people fish it. Trolling and jigging deep water for lake trout in winter and spring, casting the weed edges for bass and pike in summer, and panfish from the piers. It is a very busy lake on summer weekends — fish early in the morning.

Keeping a fish. Lake trout: two a day, at least 17 inches, in a season that ran from 3 January to 30 September in 2026. Other trout: three in total, at least 8 inches, 2 May 2026 to 7 March 2027. Walleye anywhere in Walworth County must be at least 18 inches. Northern pike: five of any length on this lake. Muskellunge: at least 50 inches. Bass: five, at least 14 inches, 2 May 2026 to 7 March 2027 (catch-and-release the rest of the year). To fish for trout you need an Inland Trout stamp ($10) on top of your licence; Wisconsin residents can instead buy a two-day inland lake trout licence ($14).

Confirmed with the Wisconsin DNR, 7 November 2026 — Hook and Line Guide 2026–2027, Walworth County: “Countywide: Walleye, sauger or their hybrids must be at least 18″”; “Geneva Lake: Muskellunge must be at least 50″. Five northern pike of any length may be kept.” Guide to Wisconsin Trout Fishing Regulations, 2026–2027, Walworth County: “LAKE GENEVA — Daily bag limit and length limit: 2 lake trout over 17″, other trout 3 in total and only fish 8″ or greater may be kept. Season: Lake trout — Jan. 3, 2026 – Sept. 30, 2026; other trout — May 2, 2026 at 5 a.m. – March 7, 2027”. State record list: “Trout, Brown (inland) 18 lbs. 6 oz. 05/07/1984 Geneva Lake, Walworth Co.”

Boat hire and the shore. Big Foot Beach State Park is on the lake’s shore at the town of Lake Geneva. Boats and tritoons can be hired on Wrigley Drive in Lake Geneva (see Resorts & launches); boating experience is required to rent.

Nearest towns: Lake Geneva at the east end, Fontana-on-Geneva Lake at the west end and Williams Bay on the north shore.

The Chippewa Flowage — muskie water in the Northwoods

A wild, island-studded flowage east of Hayward in Sawyer County, made, its resort association says, when the Chippewa River was dammed in 1923. It lies in the Ceded Territory, where many lakes carry their own walleye rules.

The Chippewa Flowage (“the Big Chip”), from Hayward

What swims there. Muskellunge — Wisconsin’s record muskie, 69 lb 11 oz, was caught here on 20 October 1949 — with walleye, northern pike, smallmouth and largemouth bass, crappie and other panfish. Lake sturgeon swim here too, for catch-and-release only.

How people fish it. From boats among the islands, bays and old river channels: casting and trolling for muskie in late summer and autumn, jigging for walleye, and ice fishing in winter. Its resort association counts more than 15,000 acres of water and over 140 islands. Stumps and shallow bars lurk away from the channels — go slowly and carry a lake map.

Keeping a fish. Walleye: open 2 May to 30 November, and only three a day may be kept from the Flowage and the Chippewa River down to the Arpin Dam combined; the Ceded Territory’s standard size rule (keep fish of 15 but less than 20 inches, with one over 24) applies to most of its waters. Northern pike: ten of any length. Muskellunge: at least 50 inches, 2 May to 31 December 2026. Largemouth bass of any length; smallmouth bass at least 14 inches. Panfish: only ten a day here. Lake sturgeon: catch-and-release only, 6 June 2026 to 7 March 2027.

Confirmed with the Wisconsin DNR, 7 November 2026 — Hook and Line Guide 2026–2027, Sawyer County: “Sawyer County is in the ceded territory”; “Chippewa Flowage (includes Lake Chippewa, West Fork of Chippewa River upstream to the Moose Lake Dam, James Slough and Scott, Tyner, Crane, James, Crystal and Moonshine lakes): Ten northern pike of any length may be kept. Muskellunge must be at least 50″. The open season on walleye is May 2 to Nov. 30 for the Chippewa Flowage. Only three walleye may be kept per day from the Chippewa Flowage and the Chippewa River downstream to the Arpin Dam combined. Largemouth bass of any length may be kept, but smallmouth bass must be at least 14″. Only 10 panfish may be kept.” General Inland Waters, lake sturgeon: “… West Fork Chippewa River from Moose Lake Dam (Sawyer Co.) including Chippewa Flowage … June 6, 2026 – March 7, 2027 — 0 (catch and release only)”. State record list: “Muskellunge 69 lbs. 11 oz. 10/20/1949 Chippewa Flowage, Sawyer Co.”

Boat launches. The Lake Chippewa Flowage Resort Association publishes a list of the area’s public boat landings and a free lake map; most resorts on the Flowage have their own docks and boats.

Nearest town: Hayward, to the west, with the most choice; many visitors stay in a resort cabin on the Flowage itself.

Lac Vieux Desert — the lake on the Michigan line

A large Northwoods lake split between Vilas County, Wisconsin, and Gogebic County, Michigan. It is a Wisconsin–Michigan boundary water, with its own set of rules.

Lac Vieux Desert, from Land O’ Lakes, Eagle River and Watersmeet

What swims there. Muskellunge, walleye, northern pike, smallmouth and largemouth bass, yellow perch and panfish. Wisconsin’s record tiger muskie, 51 lb 3 oz, was taken here in 1919.

How people fish it. From boats — drifting and jigging for walleye, casting for muskie and bass along the weed beds and islands, and ice fishing in winter. Trolling is allowed, but on the Vilas County boundary waters only with one lure per person and three lines per boat.

Keeping a fish. Walleye: three a day, each at least 18 inches, from 2 May 2026 to 1 March 2027. Muskellunge: one, at least 50 inches, 1 June to 31 December 2026, open water only. Bass: five, at least 14 inches, 20 June to 31 December 2026, catch-and-release the rest of the year. Northern pike: five, no minimum, 2 May 2026 to 7 March 2027. Perch, crappie, bluegill and other panfish: 25 together.

Which licence? Wisconsin residents need a Wisconsin licence and Michigan residents a Michigan one. Visitors from other states may buy a non-resident licence from either state — and must follow the rules of the state whose water they are on. Carry a paper copy of your licence. Goldfish, alewife and live crayfish may not be used as bait here.

Confirmed with the Wisconsin DNR, 7 November 2026 — Hook and Line Guide 2026–2027, Vilas County: “Lac Vieux Desert: Is part of the Wisconsin-Michigan boundary waters. See those regulations on page 74. Three walleye may be kept and they must be at least 18″.” Wisconsin–Michigan Boundary Waters (pages 73–76): “Anglers must carry a paper copy of their license while fishing the Wisconsin-Michigan boundary waters”; “Vilas and Iron County boundary waters, where trolling is allowed with one hook, bait or lure per person and three lines maximum per boat”; “Residents of other states need a nonresident license from Wisconsin or Michigan. Regulations on these waters differ between the two states. You must obey the regulation of the state in which you are fishing.”; bass harvest “June 20, 2026 – Dec. 31, 2026 — 5 in total — 14 inches”; muskellunge “June 1, 2026 – Dec. 31, 2026 (on open water only) — 1 — 50 inches”; northern pike “May 2, 2026 – March 7, 2027 — 5 — None”; walleye in lakes “May 2, 2026 – March 1, 2027”. State record list: “Muskellunge, Tiger 51 lbs. 3 oz. 07/16/1919 Lake Vieux Desert, Vilas Co.”

The Michigan side. We checked Wisconsin’s rules only. If you fish the Michigan half of the lake, read the Michigan DNR’s rules for it first.

Nearest towns: Land O’ Lakes, Wisconsin, the nearest town on the Wisconsin side; Eagle River, the larger Northwoods town to the south; and Watersmeet, Michigan, to the north-west.

Big Green Lake — Wisconsin’s deepest inland lake

A long, deep lake in Green Lake County, west of Fond du Lac, with the old resort town of Green Lake on its shore. The Green Lake Area Chamber of Commerce calls it “Wisconsin’s deepest natural inland lake”. Locals call it “Big Green” to tell it from Little Green Lake nearby.

Big Green Lake, from Green Lake and Ripon

What swims there. Lake trout in the deep, cold water — Wisconsin’s inland record lake trout, 35 lb 4 oz, came from Green Lake in 1957 — with other trout, walleye, smallmouth and largemouth bass, northern pike and panfish.

How people fish it. Trolling deep for lake trout, jigging, and casting the shallower bays for bass and panfish; ice fishing in winter when the lake freezes safely.

Keeping a fish. Lake trout: two a day, at least 17 inches; the 2026 season ran to 30 September. Other trout: three, at least 14 inches, 2 May 2026 to 7 March 2027. Panfish: only ten a day on this lake. Bass: five, at least 14 inches, 2 May 2026 to 7 March 2027. Northern pike: two, at least 26 inches (Southern Zone). An Inland Trout stamp is needed for trout.

Confirmed with the Wisconsin DNR, 7 November 2026 — Guide to Wisconsin Trout Fishing Regulations, 2026–2027, Green Lake County: “BIG GREEN LAKE and tributaries upstream to the first dam — Daily bag limit and length limit: 2 lake trout over 17″, 3 other trout over 14″. Season: Lake trout — Jan. 4, 2026 – Sept. 30, 2026; other trout — May 2, 2026 at 5 a.m. – March 7, 2027” (the same guide’s summary box says 3 January). Hook and Line Guide 2026–2027, Green Lake County: “Green Lake, Big: Only 10 panfish may be kept.” State record list: “Trout, Lake (inland) 35 lbs. 4 oz. 06/01/1957 Green Lake, Green Lake Co.”

Boat hire. Green Lake Marina, in the town of Green Lake, rents boats and pontoons and slips. The Heidel House hotel on the lake has public boat slips.

Nearest towns: Green Lake on the north-east shore, and Ripon, to the east, for more choice.

On the ice

The Wisconsin DNR says plainly that no ice is ever 100% safe, and that it does not monitor ice conditions itself — local fishing clubs, outfitters and bait shops are the best source for today’s ice. Check the thickness as you go with a spud bar, wear or carry a flotation device, tell someone where you are going, keep a phone in a waterproof bag and take dry clothes. Hidden currents and springs thin the ice in places, especially on the big waters and flowages.

Confirmed with the Wisconsin DNR, 7 November 2026 — news release, 19 February 2026, “No Ice Is 100% Safe”: “The DNR does not monitor ice conditions, but they are monitored locally. Local fishing clubs, outfitters and bait shops are the best sources for local current ice conditions.”

Questions

Do I need a fishing licence to fish a lake in Wisconsin, and what does it cost?

Yes, from age 16; children 15 and under fish free every day. The Wisconsin DNR lists a resident annual licence at $20 and a non-resident annual at $55, with non-resident one-day ($15), four-day ($29) and 15-day ($33) licences, and a $70 non-resident family annual. Trout need an extra $10 stamp. The 2026 annual licences are valid to 31 March 2027.

How many walleye can I keep on Lake Winnebago?

Three a day in total, of which only one may be a sauger or hybrid, with no minimum length, and the season is open all year on the Winnebago System waters, under the DNR’s 2026-2027 Hook and Line regulations.

When is sturgeon spearing on Lake Winnebago, and how do I get a licence?

The DNR opens a short spearing season through the ice each February; in 2026 it ran from 14 February to 1 March, 7 a.m. to 1 p.m., or until a harvest cap was reached. A Lake Winnebago spearing licence costs $20 for residents and $65 for non-residents, spearers must be at least 12, and the licence must be bought by 31 October before the season. Sturgeon may not be fished with hook and line on the Winnebago System.

Which state’s licence do I need on Lac Vieux Desert?

Lac Vieux Desert is a Wisconsin-Michigan boundary water. Wisconsin residents need a Wisconsin licence and Michigan residents a Michigan one; visitors from other states may buy a non-resident licence from either state, and must follow the rules of the state whose water they are fishing. Carry a paper copy of the licence. On the Wisconsin side three walleye a day may be kept, each at least 18 inches.

When can I fish for lake trout on Geneva Lake and Big Green Lake?

Both are among the few Wisconsin inland lakes with a lake trout season. In 2026 it ran from early January to 30 September, with two lake trout a day of at least 17 inches. You need an Inland Trout stamp ($10) as well as a fishing licence, or, for Wisconsin residents, a two-day inland lake trout licence ($14). Check the DNR for the 2027 dates.
