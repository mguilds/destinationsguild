Johannesburg’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Johannesburg — Best Areas & Hotels

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Most visitors base themselves in the leafy northern suburbs rather than the old downtown: Sandton is the polished business and shopping hub with the Gautrain to the airport, Rosebank is walkable and arty, and Westcliff and Hyde Park are green and grand. We’ve grouped Joburg’s most dependable hotels by the way you like to travel — luxury, boutique, value, family, adults-only and pet-friendly — each with an honest note and a Book Me link that opens that exact property on Trip.com.

Luxury

Johannesburg’s finest hotels sit in the green northern suburbs, pairing serious service and spas with garden calm a short drive from Sandton’s buzz.

The Saxon Hotel, Villas and Spa

$$$$ LuxurySandhurstGardensRenowned spa

Set in ten acres of gardens in leafy Sandhurst, the Saxon is Johannesburg’s most celebrated hotel, all-suite, with a vast spa, sweeping pools and a legendary reputation for discreet service. It is a serene, cocooning retreat minutes from Sandton. The definitive choice when you want privacy, space and pampering in the heart of the northern suburbs.

$$$$ LuxuryWestcliffHillside viewsPools & spa

Cascading down a wooded ridge above the zoo and botanical gardens, this terraced hotel offers big, contemporary rooms, hillside pools and views over the city’s green canopy. Its village-like layout and polished Four Seasons service make it a favourite for a stylish city stay. Best for travellers who want luxury with a leafy outlook and a sense of calm.

Boutique

Smaller, design-led hotels with a personal welcome — the pick for travellers who want character and calm over a big corporate tower.

Budget & Value

Reliable, well-located rooms that keep costs down — ideal for a night either side of a safari or a flight, with the Gautrain close for the airport.

$$ Mid-rangeMorningsidePool & gymReliable

A dependable, well-kept mid-range hotel in Morningside, minutes from Sandton City and the Gautrain, with tidy modern rooms, a pool and a gym. It won’t dazzle, but the consistency, safety and location make it a sensible value pick. A solid, no-surprises base for business or a short city stop before heading to the bush.
