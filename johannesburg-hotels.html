Johannesburg’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Johannesburg — Best Hotels by Type

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Johannesburg is a city of neighborhoods rather than one grand center, and the northern suburbs — Sandton, Rosebank, Melrose and leafy Dunkeld — hold nearly all of its best places to stay. Rather than sort by area alone, we’ve grouped Johannesburg’s most dependable hotels by the kind of trip you have in mind — a grand landmark, a boutique hideaway, honest value, a family base, an adults-only calm, or a room your dog can share. Budget & Value

You trade marble lobbies for tidy comfort — and spend the savings on tours to Soweto, the Apartheid Museum and the Cradle of Humankind.

Where to Stay in Johannesburg — FAQ

What is the best area to stay in Johannesburg?

For most visitors the northern suburbs are the easy answer: Sandton is the safe, modern business hub with the best hotels, malls and the Gautrain to the airport, while nearby Rosebank is a little more relaxed, walkable and arty. Melrose and Dunkeld sit between them with leafy boutique stays. Base yourself in this northern corridor for comfort and convenient day trips to Soweto, the Apartheid Museum and the Cradle of Humankind.

Is Johannesburg a walkable city?

Not really — Johannesburg is a sprawling, car-oriented city, and most travelers get around by ride-hailing apps, hotel transfers or the Gautrain train. Rosebank and the Maboneng and Braamfontein pockets are pleasant on foot by day, and Sandton’s malls and squares are easy to stroll, but you should not plan to walk between neighborhoods. Arrange transport in advance and stick to well-trafficked areas after dark.

When is the best time to visit Johannesburg?

Johannesburg is a year-round high-altitude city with a mild climate. The dry, sunny autumn-to-spring months from April to September bring crisp blue-sky days and cool evenings, ideal for sightseeing, though winter nights can be genuinely cold. The summer months of October to March are warmer and greener but bring dramatic afternoon thunderstorms. Pack layers whenever you come.
