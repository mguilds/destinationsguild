Cape Town’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Cape Town — Best Areas & Hotels

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Cape Town rewards a little planning: the V&A Waterfront and City Bowl put restaurants and Table Mountain on your doorstep, while Camps Bay and Clifton trade convenience for beach and sunset. We’ve grouped the Mother City’s most enduring, well-run hotels by the way you like to travel — luxury, boutique, value, family, adults-only, pet-friendly, beachfront, resort and spa — each with an honest note and a Book Me link that opens that exact property on Trip.com.

Luxury

Cape Town’s finest hotels are destinations in themselves — harbour and mountain views, celebrated restaurants and service that makes the stay part of the story.

One&Only Cape Town

$$$$ LuxuryV&A WaterfrontTable Mountain viewsSpa island

A glamorous waterfront resort on its own marina channel, with large, warm rooms, a spa on a private island and headline restaurants, all framed by Table Mountain. It pairs big-city polish with a relaxed, family-welcoming feel that few five-stars manage. The benchmark luxury address at the Waterfront when you want space and a mountain view.

Mount Nelson, A Belmond Hotel

$$$$ LuxuryGardensHistoricAfternoon tea

The pink grande dame of Cape Town, set in nine acres of gardens below the mountain, with a storied afternoon tea, heated pools and generations of history. It is more classic and residential than the Waterfront glamour hotels, and a short stroll from the City Bowl. The choice for old-world elegance and leafy calm in the heart of the city.

Boutique

Smaller, characterful hotels with a personal welcome — the pick for travellers who prize style and calm over a big-hotel lobby.

The Cape Cadogan

$$$ BoutiqueGardens districtHeritage housePersonal service

A restored Georgian-Victorian national monument on the edge of the City Bowl, with individually styled rooms, a small pool and an intimate, house-party feel. Its Gardens location is quiet yet walkable to Kloof Street’s cafés and the mountain. Ideal for couples who want boutique character and attentive service near the centre.

Four Rosmead

$$$ BoutiqueOranjezichtMountain viewsGuesthouse feel

A restored Cape heritage villa in leafy Oranjezicht, with a handful of elegant rooms, a saltwater pool and mountain views from the terrace. Warm, discreet hosting and a residential-street calm make it a long-standing favourite. Best for travellers who want a refined, small-scale base a short drive or walk from the City Bowl.

Budget & Value

Cape Town rewards careful budgets — a well-placed guesthouse or a central three-star costs a fraction of the grand hotels and leaves more for wine, whales and the cableway.

The Backpack

$ BudgetTamboerskloofLong-runningPrivate rooms too

One of Cape Town’s original and best-loved guesthouses-cum-hostels, a friendly, well-run spot near Kloof Street with dorms and tidy private rooms, a pool deck and a strong responsible-travel ethos. It is sociable without being a party house. A superb value base for independent and younger-at-heart travellers who want help planning day trips.

Cape Town Lodge Hotel

$$ Mid-rangeCity BowlRooftop poolReliable

A comfortable, well-located four-star on Buitengracht at the edge of the City Bowl, with straightforward modern rooms, a rooftop pool and easy reach of the Waterfront and Long Street. It won’t dazzle, but the location and price are hard to beat for exploring the centre. A sensible mid-range choice for sightseeing-first travellers.

Family-friendly

Space, pools and a location that keeps everyone happy — the Waterfront is a winner for families, with restaurants, the aquarium and boat trips on the doorstep.

Radisson Blu Hotel Waterfront

$$$ UpscaleSea point edgeOceanfront poolFamily rooms

Perched right on the Atlantic between the Waterfront and Sea Point, this modern hotel has an oceanfront pool where you can watch the waves, spacious family-friendly rooms and easy access to the promenade for evening strolls. The setting and space make it a reliable, comfortable pick for families who want the sea without leaving the city.

Its car-free surroundings and easy pace suit families with children well. A dependable, well-priced base right where the action is.

Pet-friendly

$$$$ LuxuryConstantiaGarden estatePet-friendly

It suits travellers who want green calm and vineyards over city bustle, a short drive from the beaches. A refined, pet-friendly retreat on the city’s leafy southern side.

Beachfront

Cape Town’s Atlantic seaboard is all about the sunset — Camps Bay puts the beach, palms and the Twelve Apostles mountains right outside the door.

The Bay Hotel

$$$$ LuxuryCamps Bay beachSea & mountain viewsPools

The grande dame of Camps Bay, set directly across the palm-lined strip from the beach, with breezy rooms facing the ocean or the Twelve Apostles, sun-deck pools and a buzzy champagne bar. Its position is unbeatable for beach days and sundowners. The pick when you want to walk from your room onto Camps Bay’s sand.
