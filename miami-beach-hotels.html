Where to Stay in Miami Beach — South Beach, Mid Beach & North Beach

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated November 2026

Miami Beach is its own city, with its own mayor and its own free trolley, and the Greater Miami Convention & Visitors Bureau describes seven miles of white sand beaches. The choice that matters is which stretch of it you wake up on. South Beach is the Art Deco District, Ocean Drive and the busiest evenings. Mid Beach is the line of big oceanfront hotels further up Collins Avenue. North Beach is quieter still. This page sorts the three and names nine hotels we checked on their own websites today.

Choosing Your Area in Miami Beach

Three stretches of one beach, each suited to a different trip.

South Beach — Ocean Drive and the Art Deco District

The bureau describes South Beach as an eclectic mix of dining, shopping and galleries among iconic Art Deco buildings. Lummus Park runs ten blocks between the Atlantic and Ocean Drive, with the Beach Walk winding through it, and Lincoln Road is the open-air street for shopping and dinner. The bureau also says South Beach goes beyond nightlife and works as a family destination.

Stay here for a first visit, for walking everywhere, and for the classic Miami Beach picture. Expect crowds and noise on the busiest blocks of Ocean Drive; the streets a little north, around Collins Park, are calmer.

Mid Beach — the big hotels on Collins Avenue

The bureau describes Mid Beach as the place where five-star hotels, dining, shopping, art and the shoreline come together, and lists its shores as the relaxed ones, between lively South Beach and scenic North Beach.

Stay here for a resort-style stay with the beach outside the door and South Beach a short ride away. The hotels here tend to be large resorts.

North Beach

The bureau calls North Beach serene, with a boardwalk, historic hotels, distinctive MiMo architecture and good restaurants, and says it suits visitors who want a low-key, small-beach-town feel. The Miami Beach Bandshell, an open-air amphitheater in the North Shore Historic District, holds regular concerts.

Stay here for peace and space on the sand. We have not listed a hotel here yet; the free trolley's North Beach Loop and Collins Express connect it with the rest of the city.

Getting There & When to Go

The handful of Miami Beach details that change how a trip goes.

Getting to Miami Beach

The main airport is Miami International (MIA), on the mainland. Freehand’s own website points its guests to the Miami-Dade Airport Flyer, bus 150; otherwise a taxi or rideshare is the simple way to your hotel. Cruising? Our Miami cruise port guide covers PortMiami.

Getting around

The City of Miami Beach runs a free citywide trolley. Its own page says it operates from 8 a.m. to 11 p.m., seven days a week, about every 20 minutes on each route, on four routes: the South Beach Loop, the Middle Beach Loop, the Collins Express and the North Beach Loop (read 27 November 2026). The bureau also lists a free weekday water taxi between Maurice Gibb Memorial Park in Miami Beach and Downtown Miami. If you bring a car, ask your hotel about parking before you book.

When to go

The bureau says you will use your beach sandals at any time of year, even in midwinter, and that summer brings light showers almost daily, usually over in about ten minutes. The Atlantic hurricane season runs from 1 June to 30 November; the National Hurricane Center puts most activity between mid-August and mid-October (both read 27 November 2026). If you travel in those months, choose a refundable rate and watch the forecast.

Big weeks to plan around

The bureau lists Art Basel Miami Beach for 4 to 6 December 2026, at the Miami Beach Convention Center. During big events, rooms fill and prices rise, and some hotels close parts of their beach service, so book early and read your hotel’s own notices.

Miami Beach Hotels FAQ

The questions travellers ask most before booking a room here.

Where is the best area to stay in Miami Beach?

South Beach for a first visit, walking and the Art Deco District. Mid Beach for a resort stay on the sand with South Beach a short ride away. North Beach for a quieter, small-beach-town feel.

Is Miami Beach the same as Miami?

No. Miami Beach is its own city, with its own mayor and city government, and the bureau says the free water taxi links it with Downtown Miami in about 20 minutes. Many visitors stay on the beach and visit the mainland by day.

Do I need a car in Miami Beach?

Usually not. The city runs a free trolley from 8 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day on four routes, and South Beach is easy on foot. If you bring a car, ask your hotel about parking before you book.

Is there an adults-only hotel in Miami Beach?

Yes. The National Hotel, listed above, describes itself on its own website as an adults-only oceanfront resort in South Beach (read 27 November 2026).

When is hurricane season in Miami Beach?

The Atlantic hurricane season runs from 1 June to 30 November, with most activity between mid-August and mid-October, according to the National Hurricane Center. Choose a refundable rate if you travel then.
