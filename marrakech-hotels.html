Marrakech’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Marrakech — Best Areas & Riads

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

In Marrakech, the choice is as much about style as location: a courtyard riad hidden in the Medina puts you in the heart of the storybook old city, while the wide boulevards of Hivernage and the palm groves of the Palmeraie offer pools, space and calm. We’ve grouped Marrakech’s most enduring, well-loved places to stay by area and character, each with an honest note and a Book Me link that opens that exact property on Trip.com so you can just choose your dates.

The Medina & Riads

Staying in a riad — a traditional house built around a plant-filled courtyard — inside the walled Medina is the quintessential Marrakech experience. You step from the noise of the souks into sudden calm, with rooftop breakfasts over the rooftops. Lanes are car-free, so you walk (or a porter carts your bags) the last stretch to the door.

El Fenn

$$$$ LuxuryBoutique riadRooftop & poolsArt-filled

A ravishing warren of connected riads near the Jemaa el-Fna, packed with contemporary art, hidden courtyards, plunge pools and one of the city’s best rooftop bars. Each room is individual and richly styled. The definitive design-led Medina splurge for travelers who want character, colour and a scene without leaving the old city.

La Maison Arabe

$$$ UpscaleHistoric riadCooking schoolPiano bar

One of Marrakech’s original riad-hotels, elegant and grown-up, with a famous cooking school, a clubby piano bar and a shuttle to its own garden pool outside town. Service is polished and the mood refined rather than boho. A gracious choice for travelers who want Medina atmosphere with hotel-level comforts.

Riad Kniza

$$$ UpscaleFamily-run riadAntiquesWarm service

A beautifully restored 18th-century riad run by a well-known antique-dealer family, celebrated for genuinely warm, personal hospitality and a fine in-house restaurant. Rooms are traditional and characterful. A long-time favorite for those who want an authentic, lovingly kept riad with service that remembers your name.

The small riad behind one of the most photographed courtyard pools on the internet — intimate, green and impossibly pretty. It has only a handful of rooms, so it books out far ahead. Best for couples and photographers who want that dreamy Medina-riad look without a grand-hotel price.

Grand Hotels & Hivernage

Just outside the Medina walls, Hivernage is the district of palm-lined avenues, grand hotels, gardens and nightlife. It suits travelers who want big rooms, real pools, spas and easy taxis, with the old city a short ride or walk away. This is also where Marrakech’s most legendary hotels stand.

La Mamounia

$$$$ LuxuryLegendaryGardens & spaIconic

Arguably the most famous hotel in Morocco — a palatial landmark set in historic gardens, with a storied guest list, a vast spa and several celebrated restaurants. Grand, glamorous and impeccably run, it is a destination in itself. The definitive Marrakech splurge when the hotel is meant to be the occasion.

The Palmeraie & Resorts

A short drive north of the centre, the Palmeraie is a palm-grove oasis of walled resorts, golf and big gardens. It trades the buzz of the Medina for space, quiet and serious poolside relaxation — ideal for a spa-and-swim holiday, honeymoon or a restful few days bookending a busier city or desert trip.

Mandarin Oriental, Marrakech

$$$$ LuxuryPrivate villasOlive grovesSuperb spa

A serene garden estate of private pool villas and suites set among olive groves and rose gardens, with a magnificent spa and refined dining, yet only minutes from the Medina. Impeccable service and vast, tranquil grounds. Perfect for honeymooners and travelers who want flawless luxury with total privacy.

Boutique & Mid-range

Between the grand palaces and the budget riads sits a sweet spot of stylish, well-run hotels that deliver pools, spas and comfort without a luxury bill. These are dependable choices for first-timers who want a bit of polish, a good pool and an easy location.

La Sultana Marrakech

$$$ UpscaleMedina luxuryRooftop poolNear Saadian Tombs

An intimate, opulent boutique hotel tucked in the Kasbah quarter of the Medina, near the Saadian Tombs, with richly decorated rooms, a spa and a rooftop pool with Atlas views. It blends riad character with hotel facilities. A romantic pick for couples who want luxury and old-city atmosphere together.

Budget & Value

Marrakech rewards budget travelers handsomely — a simple riad or a design hostel puts you in the thick of the Medina for a fraction of the grand-hotel rate, often with a courtyard and rooftop of its own. You trade marble and pools for character and location, and spend the savings on the souks and the desert.

Equity Point Marrakech

$ BudgetRiad hostelCourtyard poolSocial

A sociable hostel set in a converted riad near the Jemaa el-Fna, with a courtyard plunge pool, a rooftop and both dorms and private rooms. Clean, friendly and remarkably central for the price. Ideal for solo travelers, younger visitors and anyone who wants a Medina address and easy company on a budget.

A stylish riad-hostel in the Medina with a lovely tiled courtyard, a small pool and a rooftop terrace, offering dorms and neat private rooms. It punches well above its price for looks and location. A smart pick for budget-minded couples and solo travelers who still want that classic riad feel.

It’s a short taxi or walk from the Medina and handy for cafés and the train. A dependable value base for travelers who plan to be out all day.
