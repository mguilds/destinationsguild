Marrakech’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Marrakech — Best Areas & Hotels

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Marrakech rewards a little planning. Do you want to sleep inside the ancient medina, in a hidden riad a few steps from Jemaa el-Fna, out among the palms of the Palmeraie, or in the leafy, modern comfort of Hivernage and Gueliz? We’ve grouped the city’s most enduring, well-run places to stay by style and budget, each with an honest note and a Book Me link that opens that exact property on Trip.com so you can just choose your dates.

Luxury

Marrakech does grand luxury like nowhere else in Morocco — palace hotels with rose gardens, hammams and courtyards, where the stay is as memorable as the sightseeing. Book here when you want to be pampered between forays into the souks.

La Mamounia

$$$$ LuxuryLegendaryMedina gardensGrand spa

The most famous hotel in Morocco, and deservedly so: a 1920s palace set in twelve acres of walled gardens just inside the ramparts, with sumptuous Moorish-Deco interiors, an acclaimed spa and several destination restaurants. Service is polished and discreet, and the gardens alone are worth the stay. The definitive Marrakech splurge for a once-in-a-lifetime trip.

Royal Mansour Marrakech

$$$$ Ultra-luxuryPrivate riadsRooftop diningFlawless service

Commissioned by the king and built by master craftsmen, the Royal Mansour is a private medina of its own: guests stay in individual multi-storey riads with plunge pools and rooftop terraces, served through hidden passageways. The spa is a soaring white confection and the restaurants are among the country’s finest. Peerless, and priced to match.

Boutique

The riad — a traditional courtyard house turned intimate guesthouse — is Marrakech’s signature stay. Tilework, a plunge pool, a rooftop for breakfast and a dozen or so rooms make for a personal, atmospheric base deep in the medina.

El Fenn

$$$ BoutiqueMedinaRooftop barArt-filled

A rambling, ravishing riad complex near Jemaa el-Fna, El Fenn is a warren of courtyards, plunge pools and jewel-toned rooms hung with contemporary art. Its rooftop bar and restaurant, with Atlas views, is a destination in itself. Effortlessly stylish and full of character, it is the medina boutique so many others try to copy. Book well ahead.

Riad Yasmine

$$ BoutiqueMedinaFamous poolInstagram favourite

A small, beautifully styled riad whose green-tiled courtyard pool became one of the most photographed corners of Marrakech. Behind the fame is a genuinely charming, well-run guesthouse with warm service, a pretty rooftop and a handful of characterful rooms. Intimate and excellent value for the look; book early, as it is tiny and much in demand.

Budget & Value

Marrakech is kind to careful budgets: a simple medina riad or a tidy hotel near the square costs a fraction of the palaces and puts you in the thick of it. You trade grand lobbies for character and location — and spend the savings on souks, tours and the desert.

Jnane Mogador

$ BudgetMedinaGreat value riadRooftop & hammam

A long-loved budget riad a short walk from Jemaa el-Fna, with a pretty tiled courtyard, a little hammam and a rooftop for breakfast, all at prices that seem impossible for the setting. Rooms are simple but spotless and the welcome is warm. A perennial favourite for travellers who want authentic medina character without the boutique price tag.

Hotel Toulousain

$ BudgetGuelizLong-runningCourtyard & pool

A friendly, family-run stalwart in the modern Gueliz district, the Toulousain has offered honest, no-frills rooms around a plant-filled courtyard for decades. It is walkable to Gueliz’s cafés and restaurants and a short taxi from the medina, with a small pool for cooling off. Dependable value for independent travellers who just want a comfortable, well-placed base.

Family-friendly

Families do best with space, a big pool and a little distance from the medina crush. These larger resort-style hotels on the city’s edge give children room to swim and play while keeping the sights an easy taxi ride away.

Es Saadi Marrakech Resort

$$$ ResortHivernageGardens & poolsKids’ club

A long-established, family-owned resort set in eight hectares of gardens in Hivernage, close to the medina yet wonderfully green and calm. Large pools, a kids’ club, a casino, a spa and several restaurants mean everyone is catered for, and the rooms are generously sized. A reliable, all-ages base that balances resort comfort with easy sightseeing.

Mövenpick Hotel Mansour Eddahbi Marrakech

$$$ ResortHivernageBig poolsFamily rooms

A large, well-run hotel beside the convention centre in Hivernage, with expansive gardens, several pools, generous family rooms and a good breakfast spread. Its scale and reliable amenities make it an easy pick for families and groups who want space and a pool day between excursions. Comfortable and dependable rather than boutique, and priced sensibly for what you get.

Adults-only

For a grown-up, tranquil escape — couples, spa-goers and anyone wanting calm — a handful of Marrakech hotels keep to an adults-only or over-16 policy. Expect quiet pools, spa focus and unhurried service.

2Ciels Luxury Boutique Hotel & Spa

$$$ BoutiqueAdults-onlySpa & hammamRooftop pool

A refined adults-only boutique between the medina and Hivernage, built around a serene spa and hammam with a rooftop pool and Atlas views. The mood is calm and grown-up, service is attentive, and the design blends Moroccan craft with contemporary comfort. A polished pick for couples and spa-lovers who want peace within easy reach of the sights.

Pet-friendly

Travelling with a dog is easier in Marrakech’s garden hotels and villas on the city’s green edge, where there is room to walk and lawns rather than a tight medina lane. Always confirm the pet policy, size limits and any fee when you book.

Les Deux Tours

$$$ BoutiquePalmeraieGarden villasPet-friendly

A tranquil cluster of pisé villas in the palm groves of the Palmeraie, designed by the architect Charles Boccara, with private gardens, pools and a spa among the palms. The green, low-rise setting and generous grounds make it one of the more welcoming Marrakech addresses for travellers with a pet. Peaceful and characterful, a short drive from the medina.
