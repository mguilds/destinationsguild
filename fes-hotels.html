Fes’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Fes — Best Areas & Hotels

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Fes is the spiritual and craft capital of Morocco, and where you stay shapes the whole visit. Sleep in a riad deep in Fes el-Bali, the world’s largest living medieval city, and the souks, madrasas and tanneries are on your doorstep. Prefer a pool, parking and a view? A hilltop hotel just outside the walls looks out over the whole medina, while the modern ville nouvelle is calmer and cheaper. Fes rewards careful budgets: a pretty riad or a comfortable garden hotel costs far less than the palace names, and still puts you within reach of the medina. You trade grandeur for good value and a warm welcome — and spend the savings on guides and crafts.

Fes Hotels FAQ

The questions travellers ask most when choosing where to stay in Fes.

What is the best area to stay in Fes?

For most visitors, a riad inside Fes el-Bali, the old walled medina, is the best choice — you sleep in the heart of the medieval city, steps from the souks and monuments. Hilltop hotels just outside the walls trade the maze for sweeping medina views and easy parking. The modern ville nouvelle is quieter and cheaper but a taxi ride from the sights.

Should I stay in a riad or a hotel in Fes?

A riad in the medina gives you atmosphere, quiet courtyards and personal service in the thick of the old city, though you reach it on foot through the lanes and a porter helps with bags. A hotel outside the walls or in the ville nouvelle offers pools, parking and easy taxis. Many travellers pick a medina riad for the experience and accept the short walk in.

Is the Fes medina hard to navigate?

Fes el-Bali is the largest car-free urban area in the world, with thousands of lanes, so yes, it is a maze — but a wonderful one. Note your riad’s nearest landmark gate, keep the address and a phone number, and don’t be shy about hiring an official guide for your first day. Most riads will send someone to meet you at the nearest gate with a luggage cart.

How far is Fes from the airport and the train station?

Fès–Saïss airport (FEZ) is about 15 km south of the city, roughly 20 to 30 minutes by taxi. The train station is in the ville nouvelle, with fast connections to Meknes, Rabat and Casablanca and onward to Marrakech and Tangier. From either, it is a short taxi to the medina gates, where your riad can meet you.
