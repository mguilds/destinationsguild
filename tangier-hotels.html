Tangier’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Tangier — Best Areas & Hotels

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Tangier looks two ways at once — across the strait to Spain, and back over its own storied past of writers, spies and artists. Sleep in a boutique guesthouse up in the historic Kasbah and medina for atmosphere and views, along the corniche and beach for sea air, or in the city centre for cafés and the fast train. We’ve grouped the best-run places to stay by style and budget, each with an honest note and a Book Me link that opens that exact property on Trip.com.

Luxury

Tangier’s grandest hotels trade on history and the view across the strait — a restored palace on the hill, or a legendary address that once hosted writers and royalty. Book here when you want glamour with a sense of the city’s golden age.

Fairmont Tazi Palace Tangier

$$$$ LuxuryHilltop palaceSpa & poolsPanoramic views

A 1920s pasha’s palace transformed into a lavish hilltop resort in landscaped gardens above the city, with a superb spa, indoor and outdoor pools, and several restaurants. Grand public spaces and sweeping views over Tangier and the bay make it the city’s standout luxury stay. The definitive choice when you want to be pampered and to survey the strait from on high.

El Minzah Hotel

$$$ Historic luxuryCity centre1930s landmarkGarden & pool

The grande dame of Tangier, a 1930s Hispano-Moorish landmark that has hosted film stars and writers, with an elegant courtyard, a garden pool overlooking the bay and old-world service. Centrally placed above the medina and the port, it wraps genuine history in comfortable five-star surrounds. A characterful, atmospheric base for travellers who love a hotel with a story.

Boutique

Tangier’s boutique stays are small guesthouses up in the Kasbah and medina, with roof terraces that catch the sea breeze and views to Spain. Intimate, arty and full of character — you’ll climb some steps, but the atmosphere is the reward.

La Tangerina

$$ BoutiqueKasbahRooftop terraceStrait views

A charming guesthouse tucked into the ramparts of the Kasbah, with individually styled rooms, a hammam and a glorious rooftop terrace looking over the medina roofs to the Strait of Gibraltar. Warm, personal hosting and a peaceful perch above the city make it a favourite. An atmospheric boutique base for travellers who want the old town on their doorstep.

Dar Nour

$$ BoutiqueKasbahBohemianBook-lined salons

A bohemian guesthouse of interconnected houses in the Kasbah, full of books, art and cosy salons, with a much-loved rooftop for breakfast and sundowners over the strait. The mood is creative and relaxed, the hosting genuine, and the whole place feels like staying with a well-travelled friend. Perfect for independent travellers who prize character over polish.

Budget & Value

Tangier is friendly to careful budgets, with historic medina hotels and tidy city-centre addresses at honest prices. You trade a pool for character and location — and save your dirhams for the cafés, the beach and a day trip to Chefchaouen.

Hotel Continental

$ BudgetMedinaHistoricHarbour views

A gloriously atmospheric old hotel perched on the edge of the medina above the port, the oldest in Tangier and a favourite of writers and film-makers. Rooms are simple and creaky, but the terrace views over the harbour and the faded grandeur are unbeatable value. A characterful budget pick for travellers who love history and don’t need modern gloss.

Rembrandt Hotel

$$ Mid-rangeCity centreGarden poolRetro classic

A retro city-centre classic on Boulevard Pasteur, once a haunt of the international set, with comfortable updated rooms, a garden pool and a handy central location near the cafés and the medina. It offers pool-and-parking convenience at a mid-range price, with a dash of mid-century Tangier character. A solid, well-placed value base for exploring the city on foot.

Family-friendly

Families do best along the corniche and beach, where larger seafront hotels offer pools, space and easy access to the sand, with the medina a short taxi away. Look for a pool for downtime and rooms with room to spread out.

Kenzi Solazur

$$ Mid-rangeCorniche & beachPoolSea views

A large seafront hotel on the corniche overlooking Tangier’s beach, with a pool, sea-view rooms and a straightforward, family-friendly operation right by the sand. Its scale, location and pool make it an easy base for families who want beach time with the city close by. Comfortable and practical rather than stylish, and well priced for the seafront setting.

$$$ UpscaleCity centrePool & gymFamily rooms

A modern, reliable chain hotel in the city centre near the marina and shops, with bright rooms, a pool, a gym and the predictable comforts families value after a busy day. Air conditioning, lifts, parking and easy taxis make it a fuss-free base for all ages. Contemporary and convenient, with the beach and medina a short ride away.

Adults-only

Tangier has few hotels with a formal adults-only policy, but its small Kasbah guesthouses and the hilltop palace hotels deliver much the same grown-up calm — quiet terraces, spa time and unhurried dinners with a view.
