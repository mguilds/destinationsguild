Tangier’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Tangier — Best Areas & Hotels

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Tangier stands where the Mediterranean meets the Atlantic, Africa looking across the Strait to Spain — a fabled port of writers and painters, smartly reborn. Stay in or beside the hilltop kasbah and medina for atmosphere and grand old names, along the seafront and city centre for easy access to the port and train, or at a beach resort a short drive out of town. Luxury

Tangier’s finest hotels carry its cosmopolitan history — grand old dames of the international era and a lavish new palace hotel in the hills — all views, gardens and old-world glamour. Book here when you want the stay to be part of the story.

Generations of writers, stars and diplomats have passed through its doors, and the old-world glamour endures. The classic choice for history and central elegance in the heart of town.

Budget & Value

Tangier is kind to budgets: a historic medina hotel or a central mid-range stay costs a fraction of the grand names and puts you close to the action. You trade luxury for character and location — and spend the savings on the cafés and a trip to Chefchaouen.

Tangier Hotels FAQ

The questions travellers ask most when choosing where to stay in Tangier.

What is the best area to stay in Tangier?

For atmosphere, stay in or beside the medina and the hilltop kasbah, where boutique hotels and grand old names look out over the Strait of Gibraltar. For seafront hotels, restaurants and an easy walk to the port and train, base yourself in the city centre near the bay. Families and beach-lovers often prefer the resorts a short drive west along the coast.

Is Tangier worth staying in overnight?

Yes. Once known mainly as a ferry port, Tangier has been smartly revived, with a spruced-up medina, a long seafront promenade, and a storied literary and artistic history. A night or two lets you enjoy the kasbah, the cafés and the sunset over the Strait, and makes a relaxed start or finish to a trip, or a gateway to Chefchaouen and the north.

How do I get to Tangier, and is it close to Spain?

Tangier has its own airport (TNG) and is the northern hub of Morocco’s high-speed train, about 2 hours 10 minutes to Casablanca. It is also the main ferry gateway from Spain: fast boats cross from Tarifa in about an hour, while others run from Algeciras. Note that most passenger ferries use Tanger Med port, some 40 km east of the city.

Is Tangier safe and walkable for visitors?

The main tourist areas — the medina, kasbah and seafront — are generally safe and very walkable, with a visible police presence. Take normal city precautions, and expect the occasional would-be guide offering help in the medina; a polite, firm no is fine. The medina is steep and cobbled in places, so wear sturdy shoes, and use taxis after dark or for the beach resorts.
