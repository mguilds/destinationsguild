Pretoria’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Pretoria — Best Areas & Hotels

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated September 2026

Greener and calmer than neighbouring Johannesburg, Pretoria is South Africa’s administrative capital, famous for its jacaranda-lined streets and the Union Buildings. Most visitors stay in the modern eastern suburbs: Menlyn for shopping and business, and leafy Hatfield near the embassies, universities and the Gautrain. We’ve grouped the city’s most dependable hotels by the way you like to travel — luxury, boutique, value, family, adults-only and pet-friendly — each with an honest note and a Book Me link that opens that exact property on Trip.com.

Luxury

Pretoria’s smartest hotels sit near the government precinct and the Menlyn business hub, pairing polished service with easy access to the sights.

A note on the Sheraton Pretoria. For years the obvious luxury answer in Pretoria was the Sheraton, directly opposite the Union Buildings. It is now listed as permanently closed — Marriott no longer carries a page for it and it cannot be booked through Trip.com — so we have taken it off this list rather than send you to a hotel that cannot take your reservation. If you see it advertised elsewhere, confirm directly with the property before paying.

$$$ UpscaleMenlyn MaineAll-suiteRooftop pool

A sleek all-suite hotel in the modern Menlyn Maine precinct, with spacious apartment-style rooms, a rooftop pool and restaurants and the Menlyn shopping centre on the doorstep. Its contemporary suites and self-catering options suit longer stays and families alike. A polished, well-located choice in Pretoria’s liveliest business-and-shopping district.
