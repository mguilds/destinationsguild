Good to know before you book

Which area is the best place to stay in Pretoria?

The leafy eastern suburbs are the most pleasant and secure bases. Brooklyn, Hatfield and Waterkloof put you near good restaurants, the university and Loftus stadium, with easy driving reach of the city. Arcadia sits close to the Union Buildings and the government precinct, while Menlyn is best if you want the big eastern shopping malls on your doorstep.

Is Pretoria walkable?

Not as a whole city. Pretoria is spread out and car-dependent, and most locals rely on driving or e-hailing apps like Uber and Bolt. Within a single suburb such as Hatfield or Brooklyn you can comfortably stroll to cafes and shops, but for sightseeing across the city you should plan on a car or ride-hailing.

When is the best time to visit Pretoria?

Pretoria is famous for its jacaranda trees, which turn the streets purple in October and early November, the prettiest time to come. Autumn from March to May and spring bring mild, dry days; summer from November to February is warm with afternoon thunderstorms; and winter days are bright and sunny though the nights get cold.
