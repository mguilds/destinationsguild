Seoul hotels: quick questions

What is the best area to stay in Seoul?

For first-timers, Myeongdong and the Jongno palace district are the easiest, most central bases — walkable, packed with food and shops, and on the main subway lines. Gangnam suits travelers who want modern high-rises, shopping and business convenience, while Hongdae and Itaewon draw younger visitors for nightlife, cafes and a creative, international scene.

Is Seoul walkable, and is the subway easy for visitors?

Central neighborhoods like Myeongdong, Insadong and Jongno are very walkable, and Seoul’s subway is clean, cheap and fully signed in English with a rechargeable T-money card. The city is large and hilly in places, so most travelers walk their own district and ride the metro between areas.

When is the best time to visit Seoul?

Spring, roughly April to May, brings cherry blossoms and mild days, while autumn, from late September to November, offers crisp weather and brilliant foliage — these are the two most rewarding seasons. Summer is hot, humid and rainy, and winter is cold but bright, so pack accordingly if you travel then.
