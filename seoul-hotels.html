Seoul’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Seoul — Best Areas & Hotels

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Seoul is huge and superbly connected, so the hotel you choose matters more than the district. Do you want a landmark tower with a skyline view, a design-led boutique in Hongdae, a value base in the shopping streets of Myeongdong, or a family hotel beside a theme park? We’ve grouped Seoul’s most enduring, well-run hotels by the kind of stay you’re after, each with an honest note and a Book Me link that opens that exact property on Trip.com so you can just choose your dates.

Luxury

Seoul’s finest hotels cluster around the palaces and the business core of Jung-gu and Gangnam — soaring towers, destination restaurants and some of Asia’s best service. Book here when you want the hotel to be part of the story.

Signiel Seoul

$$$$ LuxuryLotte World TowerSky-high viewsJamsil / Gangnam

Occupying the top floors of the 555-metre Lotte World Tower, Signiel is Seoul’s ultimate view hotel, with floor-to-ceiling windows over the city and the Han River, a Michelin-starred restaurant and polished, discreet service. It sits above a vast mall, an aquarium and Lotte World, so families and skyline lovers alike are covered. Ask for a high floor facing the river for the full effect.

Four Seasons Hotel Seoul

$$$$ LuxuryGwanghwamunWalk to the palaceSpa & pool

A sleek modern flagship in Gwanghwamun, moments from Gyeongbokgung Palace and the old city, with large, beautifully finished rooms, a serious spa and swimming pool, and a celebrated cluster of bars and restaurants. Its central location puts the palaces, Bukchon and Myeongdong all within reach on foot or one subway stop. The benchmark for luxury in historic Seoul.

Boutique

For character over scale, Seoul’s design hotels put you in the creative districts — Hongdae’s art-and-music energy or the shopping heart of Myeongdong — with style, rooftops and a younger crowd.

RYSE, Autograph Collection

$$$ DesignHongdaeArt & rooftopCreative crowd

A genuinely design-led hotel in the heart of Hongdae, RYSE was built with artists and creatives in mind — commissioned artwork throughout, a buzzy lobby, a rooftop bar and rooms with real personality. It puts the indie shops, cafés and nightlife of Hongdae on the doorstep and the airport train a few stops away. Best for style-minded travelers who want to be where the city’s energy is.

L7 Myeongdong by LOTTE

$$ Mid-rangeMyeongdongRooftop barPlayful design

Lotte’s lifestyle brand brings colour and fun to the middle of Myeongdong — bright, compact designer rooms, a popular rooftop bar with city views, and shopping and street food the moment you step outside. It’s stylish, well-priced and central, with the airport bus and two subway lines close by. A smart pick for younger couples and first-timers who want to be in the thick of it.

Budget & Value

Seoul rewards a careful budget: clean, modern business hotels in Myeongdong put you in the shopping and street-food core, steps from the subway, for a fraction of the tower prices — spend the savings on barbecue and day trips.

Nine Tree by Parnas Seoul Myeongdong

$$ ValueMyeongdongSpotless & modernGreat location

A reliably excellent value hotel in the heart of Myeongdong, run by the same group behind the grand Parnas hotels, with crisp, modern rooms, friendly staff and an unbeatable location for shopping, food and the subway. It won’t dazzle with facilities, but for a comfortable, spotless base in the middle of everything it’s hard to beat. Ideal for first-timers who plan to be out all day.

Aloft Seoul Myeongdong

$$ ValueMyeongdongMarriott brandBuzzy bar

A bright, upbeat Marriott lifestyle hotel a short walk from Myeongdong’s shopping streets and Namsan, with modern loft-style rooms, a lively lobby bar and dependable service at a friendly price. The 24-hour gym, easy subway access and consistent standards make it a comfortable, no-surprises base for couples and solo travelers. Good value for a recognised international brand in a central spot.

Family-friendly

For families, the winning move in Seoul is a hotel attached to the fun — a theme park downstairs or big rooms near a subway hub — so tired children and heavy shopping bags are never far from a lift.

Lotte Hotel World

$$$ UpscaleJamsilTheme park downstairsFamily suites

Directly connected to Lotte World — the giant indoor-outdoor theme park, aquarium and mall — this large, well-run hotel is a family favourite, with spacious rooms, an indoor pool and character-themed floors that delight children. You can ride the rides, escape the weather and be back in your room in minutes. Right on the Jamsil subway hub, with the Lotte World Tower next door.

Grand InterContinental Seoul Parnas

$$$$ LuxuryGangnam / COEXIndoor poolMall & aquarium

A polished Gangnam landmark connected to the vast COEX mall, aquarium and subway, with generous rooms, an indoor pool and enough dining to keep everyone happy without leaving the building. It’s a smooth, self-contained base for families in the modern half of the city, with easy links to the airport and the KTX. Comfortable, reliable and endlessly convenient for a rainy day.

Adults-only

Dedicated adults-only hotels are uncommon in Korea — most upscale hotels simply keep their spas and executive lounges grown-up in feel. The one clearly adults-only address we can point to is below; otherwise, browse Seoul’s quieter luxury and boutique stays for a couples’ escape.

Seoul Riviera Hotel (Adults Only)

$$ Adults onlyCheongdam / GangnamQuiet & residentialCouples

A long-established hotel in the upscale Cheongdam district of Gangnam that is run as an adults-only property, giving couples a calm, grown-up base among the boutiques, galleries and restaurants of the neighbourhood. Rooms are comfortable and traditional rather than cutting-edge, and the setting is quiet and residential. A straightforward choice for a couples’ stay on the smart south side of the river.

Pet-friendly

Korea’s pet-friendly travel scene is growing fast, and a handful of Seoul’s international hotels welcome dogs with beds, bowls and treats. Always confirm the size limit, fee and room type when you book.

Grand Hyatt Seoul

$$$$ LuxuryNamsan hillsideDogs welcomePool & gardens

Set on the wooded slopes of Namsan above Itaewon, the Grand Hyatt is one of Seoul’s most established luxury hotels and a genuinely pet-friendly one, welcoming dogs under its Hyatt pet programme (confirm the weight limit and fee). Big rooms, leafy grounds, a fine outdoor pool in summer and an ice rink in winter round it out. A calm, green retreat with the city at its feet.

Novotel Ambassador Seoul Dongdaemun

$$$ UpscaleDongdaemunPets welcomeRooftop pool

A modern Accor hotel in the round-the-clock shopping district of Dongdaemun, with a stylish rooftop pool and bar, and a pet-friendly policy that makes it a handy choice for travelers bringing a small dog (check the fee and room rules). It’s well placed for the night markets, the DDP design plaza and the city walls. A contemporary, well-connected base on the eastern side of the centre.
