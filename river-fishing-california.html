River and Stream Fishing in California — Twelve Waters, Month by Month

The Shasta rivers, the steelhead and salmon of the north coast, and the trout streams of the Eastern Sierra: when each one fishes, which stretch has which rule, who to call, and where to sleep.

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Checked against the California Department of Fish and Wildlife and the shops that fish these waters, 4 November 2026

California’s rivers are its finest fishing and its most complicated. In the space of a morning’s drive around Mount Shasta you can stand in a freestone river, a canyon of emerald pools and a spring creek so clear the trout see you first. The north coast has wild steelhead and salmon. The Eastern Sierra has trout streams with the snow still on the peaks above them.

The price of all that is the rulebook: this state regulates its rivers stretch by stretch, and a fish legal at one bridge may not be at the next. So here are twelve waters, each with its rule as printed for 2026, and a calendar for eight of them. This is the river volume of our California fishing guide, and the companion to our fly fishing book. Where we could not confirm something, we say so.

Where these facts come from. The rules on this page were read on 4 November 2026 from the California Department of Fish and Wildlife’s 2026 Freshwater Sport Fishing Regulations (updated 10 July 2026), its 2026 salmon notices and its low-flow page; and from the Forest Service and the National Park Service. Hatches and months come from the shops that fish these waters, read the same day. In-season changes after 10 July are not in the booklet. Rules are set stretch by stretch — always check the booklet or the CDFW regulations map before you fish.

Where Are They Biting

Choose a month and a river. Or choose a month alone and we will rank them, best first. The score out of ten is our own reading of what the shops say.

The one rule to learn first

It takes effect on 16 November, so it matters now.

Streams in winter: open, but nothing to keep

From the last Saturday in April to 15 November, streams without a special rule are open with 5 trout a day and any legal gear. From 16 November, those same streams stay open but with zero trout and artificial lures with barbless hooks only, for every kind of fish. Lakes are open all year.

Any water on the special lists has its own dates and takes priority, and that includes most of the rivers below. Coastal rivers where steelhead run have a further list of their own.

Confirmed with CDFW, 4 November 2026 — 2026 regulations, section 5.85: streams are “open to fishing from the last Saturday in April through November 15 with a five-trout daily bag limit and ten-trout possession limit, with no gear restrictions.” “From November 16 through the Friday preceding the last Saturday in April, a zero-trout bag limit applies, and only artificial lures with barbless hooks may be used for all species of fish.” A steelhead card rule changes on 1 January 2027: from then it is needed for steelhead or rainbow trout in anadromous waters from 1 August to 30 April; a short 2027 card goes on sale on 15 November 2026. Read the CDFW licence page.

The Shasta rivers — five waters within reach of Redding

Base yourself in Redding, Dunsmuir or Burney and fish a different one each day.

Upper Sacramento, at Dunsmuir

The water. About 40 miles of freestone riffles, pockets and pools from Lake Siskiyou to Shasta Lake, with wild rainbows and easy access from more than fifteen freeway exits off Interstate 5.

Signature. The October Caddis, from mid-October above Dunsmuir. On 27 October 2026 the shop report had them coming off.

Rule, CDFW 2026, section 7.50(b)(126) — Box Canyon Dam to Scarlett Way bridge, Dunsmuir: all year, artificial lures with barbless hooks, 0 trout. Scarlett Way to Sweetbriar bridge: Saturday before Memorial Day to 30 September, 5 trout; 1 October to Friday before Memorial Day, artificial lures only, 2 trout. Sweetbriar to Shasta Lake: all year, artificial lures only, 2 trout.

Shops: Ted Fay Fly Shop, Dunsmuir, 530-235-2969; Jack Trout Fly Fishing, Mount Shasta, (530) 926-4540.

Lower Sacramento, at Redding

The water. A big, cold tailwater below Keswick Dam, about 56°F in the summer heat, with rainbows the shop says average about 16 inches, plus Chinook salmon and steelhead. Mostly drift-boat water, though public areas wade well below about 7,500 cfs.

Signature. The egg bite behind spawning salmon, October to February, and spring caddis. On 4 November 2026 the kings were finishing at about 4,900 cfs.

Rule, CDFW 2026, section 7.40(b)(80) — Keswick to 650 feet below: closed all year. 650 feet below Keswick to Highway 44 bridge: “Closed to all fishing from Apr. 1 through Jul. 31.” Otherwise barbless hooks only, 5 brown trout, 2 hatchery trout or steelhead, 4 in possession. Highway 44 to Deschutes Road: all year, barbless, same limits. For salmon, see below.

Shop: The Fly Shop, Redding, 800-669-3474, which also publishes the stream report we relied on for all the Shasta waters.

McCloud River

The water. A dam-released canyon river of boulders and emerald pools, with famous rainbows and brown trout that run up from Shasta in autumn. It stays fishable through runoff but can colour up: on 1 November 2026 releases were high and the water murky.

Signature. Salmonflies from late April to June, and the October Caddis.

Access. Ah-Di-Na, with a rough road that a four-wheel drive suits, snow-bound in winter. The Nature Conservancy’s preserve below takes 10 rods a day; confirm the booking with the Conservancy.

Rule, CDFW 2026, section 7.50(b)(90) — McCloud Dam to Ladybug Creek: Saturday before Memorial Day to 30 September, artificial lures only, 2 trout; 1 October to Friday before Memorial Day, artificial lures with barbless hooks, 0 trout. Ladybug Creek to Shasta Lake: all year, artificial lures with barbless hooks, 0 trout.

Hat Creek

The water. A spring creek, slow, weedy and technical at Carbon Flats below Powerhouse 2, and quicker below Highway 299. Wild rainbows and browns, and shy ones. The Wild Trout Section is only the lower 3.2 miles, from Powerhouse 2 to Lake Britton; the upper creek is over 30 miles, mostly private, with stocked sections and family camps.

Signature. Salmonflies in May, Green Drakes in June, summer Tricos. Browns spawn on the Powerhouse riffle in autumn, so keep off bare gravel.

Rule, CDFW 2026, section 7.50(b)(55) — Lake Britton up to Baum Lake, excluding the Hat No. 2 intake canal: all year, artificial lures with barbless hooks, 0 trout.

Fall River

The water. A huge spring creek of about 15 miles, seven feet deep on average and too deep to wade, with wild rainbows and no runoff.

Signature. PMD spinner falls in May and June; the Hex at dusk from mid-June through July, when the biggest trout come up.

Access. This is boat water, and the banks are almost entirely private. The public way in is the CalTrout site by Island Road: free, hand-launch only, no gas motors. Most anglers fish with a guide or stay at a lodge with a dock; ask The Fly Shop.

Rule, CDFW 2026, section 7.50(b)(47) — Pit River confluence up to Thousand Springs: all year, artificial lures with barbless hooks, 0 trout.

Trinity, Klamath and Smith — the steelhead rivers

The rules here come with quotas, flows and report cards. Barbless hooks throughout the Klamath-Trinity basin.

Trinity River, Lewiston to Junction City

The water. A dam-controlled river, with its upper 40 miles the main fishery; the Fly Shop calls it arguably one of the finest steelhead streams in the West. Drift boats and rafts, and wading on public land along Highway 299. Expect high flows and little fishing in May and June.

Signature. Autumn steelhead in October and November, wild winter fish from December to March. On 27 October 2026 adult steelhead were arriving daily near Junction City.

Rule, CDFW 2026, section 7.40(b)(50) — 250 feet below Lewiston Dam to Old Lewiston Bridge: 1 April to 15 September, “Only artificial flies with barbless hooks may be used.” Old Lewiston Bridge to Cedar Flat: all year, 2 hatchery trout or steelhead, 10 brown trout. Cedar Flat to the Denny Road bridge at Hawkins Bar: closed to all fishing 1 September to 31 December. Barbless hooks basin-wide; report cards required.

Shops: Trinity Fly Shop, Lewiston, 530-623-6757, Tuesday to Sunday; The Fly Shop, Redding.

Klamath River

The water. A big freestone river from the Oregon line to the Pacific, with Highway 96 along the middle river through Happy Camp and Orleans. Chinook, steelhead, the small steelhead called half-pounders, and protected coho.

The dams. Four dams came out: Copco 1 and 2, Iron Gate and JC Boyle. Fish passage opened on 26 September 2024, and Chinook spawned above the old Iron Gate site that October, the first recorded there since 1961. CDFW counts 420 miles of newly accessible habitat. For anglers it is not new water yet: the reach below is still closed.

Rule, CDFW 2026, section 7.40(b)(50) — State line to Copco Road bridge: 1 November to 28 February and fourth Saturday in May to 31 August, artificial lures with barbless hooks, no Chinook, 0 hatchery trout or steelhead. Copco Road bridge to Lakeview Road bridge: closed all year. Lakeview to I-5: 2 hatchery fish; no Chinook from 15 August. I-5 to the mouth: Chinook by quota. No fishing within 500 feet of the Salmon, Shasta, Scott, Blue, Bogus, Fall and Shovel creek mouths from 15 September to 31 December. Ishi Pishi Road bridge to the falls closed 15 August to 31 December. Quota hotline 800-564-6479.

Smith River

The water. California’s undammed coastal river, very clear, through the redwoods. It rises and clears quickly, so timing is everything.

Signature. Chinook after the first big rains, about October to December, and winter steelhead from late December to April.

Rule, CDFW 2026, section 7.40(b)(105) and section 8.00 — Main stem: fourth Saturday in May to 30 April; artificial lures with barbless hooks to 31 August; barbless hooks from 1 September. 2 hatchery trout or steelhead, 2 cutthroat at 10 inches, 1 Chinook. Upper forks and North Fork have shorter seasons, and part of the South Fork is closed: read the booklet. Low flow: 1 September to 30 April, closed below 600 cfs at Jedediah Smith Redwoods State Park. Closed on 4 November 2026. The whole North Coast table was closed that day: check the CDFW low-flow page, updated Monday, Wednesday and Friday by 1 p.m.

Guides: Jack Trout, for fly anglers, and Steve Huber Guide Service, for conventional tackle. We found no fly shop on the river itself.

The Truckee and the Eastern Sierra

One drive down Highway 395 takes in the lot.

Truckee River

The water. Freestone with dam-fed flows, from Tahoe to Nevada, with rainbows and browns. Signature: Skwala stoneflies in March and April, then PMDs, caddis and sallies into June. On 29 October 2026 the shop had streamer fishing good.

Rule, CDFW 2026, section 7.50(b)(154) — 1,000 feet below the Tahoe outlet dam: closed. Trout Creek to Prosser Creek: all year, artificial flies with barbless hooks only, 0 trout. Prosser Creek to the Nevada line: last Saturday in April to 15 November, artificial lures, 2 trout; winter, artificial barbless, 0 trout.

Shop: Trout Creek Outfitters, Truckee, (530) 563-5119.

Owens River

The water. Three rivers in one: meadow water above Crowley Lake, the Gorge, and a Bishop tailwater below Pleasant Valley Dam. Browns and rainbows, with lake-run fish in autumn. Signature: midday Baetis on the Lower Owens in winter, when flows are low. The Bishop Visitor Center warns wading is unsafe above 300 cfs.

Rule, CDFW 2026, section 7.50(b)(104) — Benton Bridge up to Big Springs: artificial barbless, 0 trout. Benton Bridge to Crowley: last Saturday in April to 31 July, 5 trout; 1 August to 15 November, artificial lures, 18-inch minimum, 2 trout. Pleasant Valley Dam to the campground footbridge: last Saturday in April to 15 November, 2 trout; winter barbless, 0. Footbridge to 5 Bridges Road: all year, artificial barbless, 0 trout.

Shops: Sierra Trout Magnet, Bishop, 760-873-0010; Sierra Drifters, Mammoth Lakes, (760) 935-4250.

Hot Creek

The water. A small spring-fed meadow creek and canyon near Mammoth, weedy by autumn, with wild trout. Signature: summer Tricos and caddis, winter midges.

Read this before you go. Inyo National Forest lists the Hot Creek Geologic Site as no fishing, and a forest order bars anyone from entering the creek along a quarter-mile stretch through 31 December 2026. Yet its fishing page still lists the creek as open, and shops report fishing the canyon. These do not fit together, and we will not guess at the line. Telephone the Mammoth Ranger District.

Rule, CDFW 2026, section 7.50(b)(61) — State hatchery property line to the Owens River: “All year. Only artificial flies with barbless hooks may be used.” 0 trout.

Shops: Kittredge Sports, Mammoth Lakes, (760) 934-7566, and Sierra Drifters.

East Walker River, Bridgeport

The water. A tailwater from Bridgeport Reservoir, about seven miles in California, known for big browns. Highway 182 follows it. Fish on across the Nevada line and you need a Nevada licence. Signature: caddis in May and June, autumn browns on streamers.

Rule, CDFW 2026, section 7.50(b)(164) — Bridgeport Dam to the Nevada line: last Saturday in April to 15 November, artificial lures only, 18-inch minimum, 2 trout. Closed the rest of the year. Ignore one Forest Service page that says it is open all year.

Shop: Ken’s Sporting Goods, Bridgeport, 760-932-7707.

Other waters, small streams and the south

One line each. We have not researched these to the full standard yet.

Worth knowing

Merced and Tuolumne in Yosemite. New in 2026, fishing is year-round in the park. On the Merced, its South Fork and the Tuolumne, only artificial lures or flies with barbless hooks may be used; rainbows must be released; you may keep 5 brown and brook trout a day. Read the Park Service page.

Delta striped bass. Limit 2, 18-inch minimum, no maximum. Sturgeon there are catch and release only. See the state’s advice before you eat anything from the Delta.

Pit River. Slippery, and flows change without notice; a wading staff is wise. Kern River. All year, 5 trout, from Lake Isabella up to the Johnsondale bridge. Russian River. Barbless rules, and a low-flow closure below 300 cfs. Eel River. Most of the drainage is closed; closed for low flow on 4 November. Lower Yuba. Artificial lures with barbless hooks.

Southern California. The West Fork San Gabriel is open weekends and federal holidays only at the Forest Service’s fishing access, $5 a day, and catch and release from the second bridge to Cogswell Dam. Piru Creek below Pyramid Lake is barbless, 0 trout, past a closed stretch at the dam. Most other southern coastal streams are closed to protect steelhead.

With children. Look for stocked waters on CDFW’s fishing guide. Upper Hat Creek has stocked stretches and family camps, and the Truckee beside the Tahoe City bike path is easy to reach. Check the stretch first.

Questions

When is trout season on California streams?

From the last Saturday in April through 15 November, with 5 trout a day. From 16 November until the next opener, most streams stay open but with a zero-trout limit and artificial lures with barbless hooks only. Many special waters have their own rules, so check the stretch.

Is the Smith River open?

It depends on the water. From 1 September to 30 April the Smith closes whenever the flow is under 600 cubic feet per second at Jedediah Smith State Park. On 4 November 2026 it was closed, like the rest of the North Coast rivers. CDFW updates the list on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays.

Did the Klamath dam removal open new fishing?

Not yet. Four dams came out and salmon reached the old reservoir reach in autumn 2024. But the 2026 regulations still close the Klamath from Copco Road bridge to Lakeview Road bridge all year. A summer season has been proposed for 2027; we could not confirm it was adopted.

Can you keep salmon from California rivers in 2026?

In 2026 yes, in places, after three closed years. The Sacramento is open to 16 December below Woodson Bridge, and to 31 December from Deschutes Road to Woodson Bridge, with 2 Chinook a day. Klamath and Trinity seasons run on quotas that close by stretch, so call 800-564-6479. Seasons are set again each May.

Can you fish Hot Creek?

Hot Creek is flies only, barbless, zero trout, all year under state rules. But the Forest Service lists the Hot Creek Geologic Site as no fishing, and a closure order covers part of the creek through 31 December 2026. Telephone the Mammoth Ranger District before you go.

Can a child fish these rivers?

Many are hard wading water. Family fishing is easier on stocked streams and at the Tahoe City bike path on the Truckee, but check the stretch. Under 16 needs no licence, though a report card is needed for steelhead and for salmon on the Klamath, Trinity and Smith.

The part of a fishing page most sites leave out.

Five honest gaps

Quotas and flows on the day. The Klamath above Weitchpec and the upper Trinity may have closed to adult salmon since we looked. Low-flow status changes three times a week.

The 2027 rules. CDFW proposed changes for 1 January 2027, including a summer catch-and-release season on the closed Klamath reach and the Steelhead Card change. We confirmed the card, but not the rest.

Hot Creek and access gates. Forest pages conflict on Hot Creek. We could not confirm the current position of utility gates around Hat Creek, or float tubes at the Fall River site.

Calendars. Shops publish hatches, not scores, so the marks out of ten are ours. Winter months for Hot Creek come from a general chart, and a few Fall River and Klamath months have no shop data.

Not listed. We found no fly shop on the Smith or the Klamath, and no fly guide on the Delta, that met our standard.
