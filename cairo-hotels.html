Cairo’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Cairo — Best Areas & Hotels

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Cairo is vast, so the area you pick shapes the whole trip. Do you want the Pyramids from your balcony at Giza, a leafy island calm in Zamalek, or a Nile-view room in Garden City within reach of the great museums? We’ve grouped Cairo’s most enduring, well-run hotels by area and character, each with an honest note and a Book Me link that opens that exact property on Trip.com so you can just choose your dates.

Giza & the Pyramids

Base yourself in Giza and the Pyramids are your backdrop — some hotels put the Great Pyramid squarely in the window. It’s further from Downtown and the Nile museums, and traffic can be slow, but for many travelers waking up to that view is the whole point of coming to Egypt.

Marriott Mena House

$$$$ LuxuryFoot of the PyramidsHistoricGardens & pool

A legendary 19th-century palace hotel set in green gardens right at the foot of the Great Pyramid, with pyramid-view rooms that are among the most famous in the world. Grand public rooms, a big outdoor pool and generations of history make it Cairo’s signature splurge. Best for a once-in-a-lifetime stay where the view is everything.

Le Méridien Pyramids Hotel & Spa

$$$ UpscalePyramids RoadBig poolFamily-friendly

A large, full-service resort-style hotel on the Pyramids road, with expansive pools, several restaurants and many rooms angled toward the plateau. Its scale and amenities make it an easy, comfortable base for families and groups who want space and a reliable operation near Giza. Dependable rather than boutique, and priced accordingly.

Zamalek

On an island in the Nile, Zamalek is Cairo’s most gracious quarter — tree-lined streets, embassies, galleries, cafés and a slower pace, yet still central. It’s the pick for travelers who want a calmer, more residential base with good restaurants on the doorstep and easy reach to both banks.

Cairo Marriott Hotel & Omar Khayyam Casino

$$$ UpscaleHistoric palaceGardensCentral Zamalek

Built around a genuine 19th-century khedival palace, this large hotel wraps modern towers around ornate historic halls and leafy garden courtyards in the heart of Zamalek. It blends grand character with full resort amenities and a lively terrace. A characterful, well-located choice for those who want history without leaving the island.

Sofitel Cairo Nile El Gezirah

$$$$ LuxuryIsland tip360° Nile viewsRooftop bar

Occupying the southern tip of Gezira island, this circular tower gives nearly every room a wraparound Nile view, with a waterfront promenade and a well-known rooftop bar. Polished service and a dramatic setting make it a favorite for couples and skyline lovers. Ask for a higher floor to make the most of the river panorama.

Hotel Longchamps

$$ Mid-rangeBoutiqueLeafy terracesGreat service

A beloved family-run boutique hotel on an upper floor of a Zamalek apartment building, with plant-filled terraces, a warm welcome and a loyal repeat clientele. Rooms are comfortable and quiet, and the value is excellent for the address. Ideal for independent travelers who prize character, calm and personal service over a big-hotel lobby.

Garden City & the Nile Corniche

Garden City and the central corniche put you on the river, walking distance to Tahrir Square, the Egyptian Museum and Downtown. This is the most convenient base for first-timers who want Nile views and the great museums close at hand, with the Pyramids a manageable drive away.

Kempinski Nile Hotel Cairo

$$$$ LuxuryGarden CityBoutique feelRooftop pool

A smaller, more intimate luxury hotel on the Garden City corniche, with plush Nile-view rooms, a rooftop pool and a refined restaurant lineup. Its boutique scale makes it feel calmer than the big convention hotels while keeping you right on the river. A polished pick for couples who want central Nile luxury without the crowds.

Luxury Landmarks — the grand Nile hotels

Cairo’s great riverfront hotels are destinations in themselves — sweeping Nile views, celebrated restaurants and the kind of service that makes the stay part of the story. Book here when you want the hotel to be as memorable as the sightseeing.

$$$$ LuxuryGarden CityNile viewsSpa & pools

Consistently rated among Cairo’s finest, this Garden City flagship pairs large, elegant Nile-view rooms with a superb spa, multiple pools and some of the city’s best restaurants. Impeccable service and a prime riverside setting make it the benchmark for luxury in the capital. The definitive choice when nothing but the best will do.

Budget & Value

Cairo rewards careful budgets: a historic Downtown room or a simple pyramid-view guesthouse costs a fraction of the grand hotels, and puts you close to the action. You trade marble lobbies for character and location — and spend the savings on guides, tombs and the Nile.

The Windsor Hotel

$ BudgetDowntownHistoricOld-world bar

A time-capsule Downtown institution, once a khedival bathhouse and British officers’ club, with creaking woodwork, antique lifts and a famously atmospheric barrel-lounge bar. Rooms are simple and old-fashioned, but the history and value are unbeatable. A characterful budget pick for travelers who love faded grandeur and a central location.

Pyramids View Inn

$ BudgetGiza villageRooftop pyramid viewGuesthouse

A small, friendly guesthouse in the village right beside the Giza plateau, whose rooftop terrace looks straight at the Pyramids — a sound-and-light show with your dinner. Rooms are basic and it’s a walk from the Nile, but the view and the welcome are the draw. Perfect for budget travelers who want the Pyramids as close as it gets.

Horus House Hotel Zamalek

$ BudgetZamalekSimple & cleanGreat location

A long-running, no-frills hotel on a leafy Zamalek street, popular for its tidy rooms, honest prices and an old-school restaurant. It lets you stay in Cairo’s nicest quarter without the boutique price tag. A dependable value base for travelers who plan to be out sightseeing all day and just want a comfortable, well-placed room.
