Caribbean Cruises

The Caribbean is the world’s most popular cruise region — and the easiest way to sample a dozen sun‑drenched islands in a single week. Ships sail year‑round from Florida and beyond, and itineraries split three ways: Eastern (beaches and duty‑free shopping), Western (Maya ruins, reefs and Mexico), and Southern (the deep, less‑crowded islands).

Best time to cruise

The sweet spot is December through April — warm, dry and calm. Hurricane season runs June through November; sailings still go (and prices drop), but pack flexibility with the weather. Cruise lines shift ships south in winter, so that’s when choice is widest.
