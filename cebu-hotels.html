Where to Stay in Cebu

Where to Stay in Cebu — Best Hotels & Areas

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Cebu asks you one question before it asks you anything else: beach or city. The resorts are on Mactan, the island the airport sits on; the restaurants, the history, the malls and the ferry pier are across the bridge in Cebu City. They are twenty minutes apart on a good morning and well over an hour apart at five in the afternoon, and choosing the wrong one for the trip you actually want is the commonest mistake visitors make here. Choosing Your Area

Four places hold nearly every hotel worth the trip. Here is the honest difference between them.

Mactan, Cebu Business Park, IT Park or Nivel Hills?

Read this firstCebu & Lapu-LapuThe bridge matters

Mactan — officially Lapu-Lapu City — is the resort island, and the Punta Engaño headland at its northern end is where the big beachfront properties sit, with private sand, large pools, dive shops and the airport fifteen to twenty-five minutes away. It is a holiday rather than a base: dining outside your resort takes a taxi. Cebu Business Park, in the city around Ayala Center Cebu, is the safe answer for a first visit — central, walkable by Philippine standards, and surrounded by restaurants and shops. Cebu IT Park in Lahug is the younger sibling a few minutes north: newer buildings, a good deal of nightlife, and noticeably better value for the same standard of room. Nivel Hills, on the slope above Lahug, buys you the finest view in the city, over the rooftops to the sea and Mactan beyond, and costs you a car journey for every meal. One point overrides all of it: ask what the drive will be at the hour you plan to move, not what the map says. The bridges between Mactan and the mainland queue badly from about four in the afternoon, and the Cebu–Cordova link road helps the south of the island rather than the airport run.

Luxury & the landmark resorts

Cebu’s top end is mostly on the water at Mactan, with two large city addresses to set against it.

Shangri-La Mactan, Cebu

$$$$ LuxuryPunta Engaño, MactanPrivate beachMarine sanctuary

The resort most people mean when they say they are going to Cebu: a very large, mature, beautifully planted estate on the Punta Engaño headland, with its own protected reef, a genuine stretch of sand and gardens old enough to have proper shade in them. It is the strongest all-round choice on the island for a family or a couple who want everything on site — several restaurants, a serious spa, a dive centre and a children’s club. It is not the newest resort on Mactan and it does not pretend to be; what it has is the space and the trees, which the newer places will not have for another twenty years.

Family-friendly

Honest noteCebu & MactanBook the quiet, not the label

Mactan’s resorts are family resorts, and that is the plain truth of the island — Trip.com carries no genuine adults-only property in Cebu or Lapu-Lapu that we would put our name to. If a child-free beach holiday is the whole point of the trip, the format genuinely exists on Boracay and in Palawan, and it is worth moving the trip rather than settling here. Honest noteAsk before you bookApartments work best

We could not verify a Cebu or Mactan hotel that Trip.com sells as reliably pet-friendly, and we will not invent one — a family turned away at a resort gate with a dog in the car is the worst outcome this page could produce. Two practical notes instead. Serviced apartments such as Citadines Cebu City, listed above, are the likeliest to make an arrangement, as apartment-style properties generally are, and they give an animal room to be comfortable rather than confined. And whatever you book, agree it in writing with the property itself before you travel, and confirm the size limit and any fee: pet policies in the Philippines change far more often than room rates, and a booking site’s badge is not a promise the front desk has to keep. Cebu Hotel FAQ

Should I stay on Mactan or in Cebu City?

Mactan if you want a beach holiday, Cebu City if you want a base. They are two different trips joined by a bridge. Mactan, in Lapu-Lapu City, is where the resorts are: private beaches, big pools, dive shops, and the airport about twenty minutes away. Cebu City is where the restaurants, the malls, the history and the ferry pier are, and it is where you begin any journey to Bohol, Moalboal or Malapascua. Many visitors do both, and the sensible order is city first and resort last, so the beach is what you remember. Allow real time for the crossing at rush hour: the bridges between Mactan and the mainland can take well over an hour in the late afternoon.

Which part of Cebu City should I stay in?

Cebu Business Park or Cebu IT Park for a first visit. Cebu Business Park, around Ayala Center Cebu, is the most comfortable and central choice, with restaurants and shopping within a short walk. Cebu IT Park in Lahug is younger, busier at night and very good value. Nivel Hills, above Lahug, buys you the view over the city and the sea at the cost of needing a car for everything. Downtown, around Colon Street and the Basilica, is where the history is and where most visitors go by day rather than sleep.

How long does it take to get from Mactan-Cebu airport to my hotel?

Fifteen to twenty-five minutes to most Mactan resorts, and anywhere from forty minutes to an hour and a half into Cebu City depending entirely on the hour. The airport sits on Mactan Island, so the resorts are close; reaching the mainland means one of the bridges, and those queue badly in the late afternoon and early evening. If you land after dark the journey is usually easy. If you land at four in the afternoon, assume the long version and do not book anything for that evening.

When is Cebu busiest and dearest?

The Sinulog Festival in the third week of January, and the Christmas to New Year fortnight. Sinulog is one of the great festivals of the Philippines and fills every hotel in the city; rooms are booked months ahead and rates rise sharply. Holy Week, the week before Easter, is the other pinch point, when the whole country travels. Outside those weeks the dry months of February to May are the pick of the year for the beaches and the diving, and June to November is quieter and cheaper, with heavy afternoon rain rather than all-day grey.

Is Cebu a good base for diving and island trips?

It is one of the best in Asia, but not all of it is a day trip. Mactan has easy house-reef and boat diving on the doorstep. The famous sites are further away: the sardine run at Moalboal is about three hours down the west coast, the thresher sharks of Malapascua need a drive to the northern tip and a boat, and Bohol is a two-hour fast ferry from the Cebu City pier. Any of these is better as an overnight than as a very long day. Note also that the whale-shark encounters at Oslob involve feeding the animals from boats, a practice many marine biologists and responsible operators object to; it is worth reading about before you book it.
