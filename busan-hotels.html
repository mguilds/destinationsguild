Busan’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Busan — Best Areas & Hotels

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Busan is Korea by the sea, and most visitors want to wake up near it. The beach district of Haeundae has the big-name towers and the sand; neighbouring Gwangalli glows under its lit-up bridge; and downtown Nampo keeps you by the fish market and the old city. We’ve grouped Busan’s best-run hotels by the kind of stay you’re after — including beachfront, resort and spa options for a seaside break — each with an honest note and a Book Me link that opens that exact property on Trip.com.

Luxury

Busan’s top hotels line the waterfront at Haeundae and the Marine City towers, trading on sea views, fine dining and glossy spas. Book here when the view and the service are the point of the trip.

Park Hyatt Busan

$$$$ LuxuryMarine CityYacht-harbour viewsRooftop pool

A serene, understated luxury tower on the Marine City waterfront, with floor-to-ceiling windows over the yacht harbour and the Gwangan Bridge, a top-floor pool and spa, and the kind of quiet, precise service Park Hyatt is known for. It’s a short stroll to Haeundae Beach yet feels like a calm retreat above the city. The pick for couples who want polish and a knockout view.

Signiel Busan

$$$$ LuxuryHaeundae LCTOceanfrontSky-high rooms

Lotte’s luxury flagship occupies the upper floors of the landmark LCT tower right on Haeundae Beach, giving nearly every room a soaring ocean view, along with a sleek spa, indoor pool and destination dining. It’s the newest of Busan’s grand hotels and among the most dramatic, steps from the sand and the beach nightlife below. Ask for a sea-facing room high up for the full sweep.

Boutique

For style at a friendlier price, Busan’s smart mid-size hotels put you in the heart of the beach or downtown scene with a design edge and, often, a rooftop or sea view.

Hotel Foret Premier Nampo

$$ Mid-rangeNampo / downtownHarbour viewsDesign rooms

A sharp, contemporary hotel in the middle of downtown Nampo, walking distance to the Jagalchi fish market, Gukje market and the Gamcheon village bus, with stylish rooms and upper floors that look over the harbour and the port cranes. It’s well priced for its polish and puts the old city’s food and shopping on the doorstep. A characterful base for exploring downtown Busan on foot.

It offers a stylish stay near the action without the flagship price tag. A neat choice for design-minded travelers who want the beach nearby.

Budget & Value

Busan is easy on a budget: reliable international-brand business hotels near the beach or downtown give you a clean, modern room and a great location for far less than the towers.

Ibis Ambassador Busan Haeundae

$ BudgetHaeundaeSteps from the sandReliable brand

A dependable, well-kept Ibis just off Haeundae Beach, with compact modern rooms, friendly service and a location that is hard to beat for the price — the sand, the market and the subway are all a short walk away. It won’t win design awards, but it’s spotless, central and easy on the wallet. Ideal for beach-focused travelers who’d rather spend on seafood than on the room.

Family-friendly

For families, Busan pairs beaches with big, well-equipped hotels — pools, roomy suites and easy access to the sand, the aquarium and the theme parks.

Lotte Hotel Busan

$$$ UpscaleSeomyeonPools & mallFamily suites

A large, long-established hotel above the Lotte department store and mall in central Seomyeon, with indoor and outdoor pools, spacious rooms and family-sized options, and shopping, cinemas and food courts under the same roof. Its central-hub location makes day trips to the beaches, the aquarium and the markets simple. A comfortable, everything-on-hand base for families.

Pet-friendly

Korea’s pet-travel scene is booming, and Busan has some genuinely dog-welcoming stays — most notably the pet-friendly resort at Busan Cove. Always confirm the size limit, fee and designated rooms when you book.

Ananti at Busan Cove

$$$$ ResortGijang coastPets welcomeSeaside spa

A striking cliff-top resort on the coast north of the city (the former Hilton site, now part of the Ananti group), known for welcoming pets alongside its dramatic ocean-front pools, seawater spa and celebrated Eyagi bookstore-and-shops complex. Rooms are large and design-led, with the sea on your doorstep and walking trails all around. A memorable base for travelers who won’t leave the dog behind.

Beachfront

For toes-in-the-sand mornings, nothing beats a room right on Haeundae Beach — step from the lobby straight onto Korea’s most famous stretch of sand.

The Westin Josun Busan

$$$$ LuxuryHaeundae BeachOn the sandDongbaek headland

A grande-dame beachfront hotel at the quieter Dongbaek end of Haeundae, with direct access to the sand, sea-view rooms, a headland walking path and the polished comforts of the Westin brand. Long a Busan favourite, it pairs a prime beach location with easy reach of the aquarium and the Nurimaru pavilion. A classic choice for a proper beach-holiday base.

All-inclusive & Resort

For a stay-put holiday where the hotel is the destination, Busan’s resort-style properties wrap pools, spas, dining and the sea into one self-contained escape.

Ananti at Busan Cove

$$$$ ResortGijang coastOcean-front poolsShops & dining

Ananti’s flagship coastal resort north of Busan is a world of its own — cliffside infinity pools over the sea, a seawater spa, and the buzzing Eyagi complex of restaurants, bakeries and the vast Cloud & Bookstore. With spacious residence-style rooms and trails along the shore, you can happily settle in for days without leaving. The pick when you want a resort escape rather than a city hotel.

Spa & pool

Busan is a spa city — hot springs run beneath it — and its best hotels put serious wellness front and centre, from rooftop pools to famous seawater spas.

Park Hyatt Busan

$$$$ LuxuryMarine CityTop-floor poolSerene spa

The Park Hyatt’s upper-floor pool and spa are among the most soothing in the city, gazing out over the yacht harbour and the bridge, with treatments and a hushed, grown-up calm to match. Pair a swim above the skyline with the hotel’s refined dining for a genuine wellness escape. Best for couples who want quiet indulgence with a view.

Paradise Hotel Busan

$$$$ LuxuryHaeundae BeachCimer seawater spaTerraced pools

Home to Cimer, one of Korea’s best-known open-air seawater spas, Paradise turns wellness into the main event — hot pools, saunas and terraces set right above Haeundae Beach, with the sound of the surf below. It’s a splash-and-soak destination as much as a hotel, and a favourite for a pampering seaside weekend. The choice when the spa is the reason you came.
