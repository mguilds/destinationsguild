Busan’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Busan — Best Hotels by Category

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Busan is South Korea’s great seaside city, and where you sleep sets the tone of the trip. Do you want a high-floor sea view over Haeundae Beach, a design hotel in the old-town lanes of Nampo, or a beachfront resort with a hot-spring spa? Luxury

Busan’s five-star scene clusters in Haeundae and Marine City, where glass towers rise straight off the sand and look out over the sea. This is where you pay for a view — book a high floor and let the water be the show.

Busan hotels: quick questions

What is the best area to stay in Busan?

For most first-time visitors, Haeundae is the best base: it has Busan's most famous beach, the widest range of hotels, good restaurants and an easy subway link. Marine City and Gwangalli, both a short ride away, offer similar sea views with a slightly more local feel, while Seomyeon and Nampo suit travelers who want shopping, markets and lower prices over a beach.

Is Busan walkable, or do I need public transport?

Individual neighborhoods like Haeundae, Nampo and Seomyeon are very walkable, but Busan is a large, hilly city spread along the coast, so you will use the subway to move between areas. The metro is clean, cheap and easy to navigate in English, and a rechargeable transit card also works on buses and in taxis. Plan to walk within a district and ride between them.

When is the best time to visit Busan?

Late spring (April to June) and autumn (September to November) are ideal, with mild, dry weather and clear coastal views. Summer is warm and lively but busy and humid, and it overlaps with the July rainy season and occasional typhoons. Winters are cold but sunny and quiet, with the lowest hotel prices of the year.
