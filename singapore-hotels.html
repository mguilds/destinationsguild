Singapore’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Singapore

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Singapore is compact and superbly connected, so almost anywhere central puts you a short MRT ride from the sights — which means you can choose your hotel by the kind of stay you want rather than by neighbourhood alone. Craving a rooftop pool over Marina Bay, a shophouse boutique in Chinatown, a family resort on Sentosa, or somewhere your dog is welcome? We’ve grouped Singapore’s most reliable, well-run hotels by category, each with an honest note and a Book Me link that opens that exact property on Trip.com so you can just choose your dates.

Luxury

Singapore does grand luxury as well as any city on earth, from a colonial legend of white verandahs and ceiling fans to the boat-topped icon that reshaped its skyline. Book here when you want the hotel to be a headline of the trip, not just a place to sleep.

Marina Bay Sands

$$$$ LuxuryMarina BayInfinity SkyPark poolIconic

The three-tower landmark crowned by a 150-metre rooftop infinity pool is as much a destination as a hotel, with vast rooms, a huge casino, a designer mall and a nightly light show over the bay. It is busy and unashamedly large-scale, but no other stay puts you so squarely inside Singapore’s postcard. Best for first-timers who want the view and the wow, and don’t mind sharing it with the crowds.

Boutique

Singapore’s boutique scene lives mostly in its restored shophouses — narrow heritage buildings turned into design-led little hotels with real neighbourhood character. Choose these when you want personality, a good bar and a sense of place over a big-brand lobby.

Good to know before you book

What is the best area to stay in Singapore?

For first-time visitors, Marina Bay and the City Hall / Civic District put you within walking distance of the waterfront, Gardens by the Bay, museums and the MRT, which makes them the easiest bases. Chinatown, Tanjong Pagar and Bugis / Kampong Glam are more atmospheric shophouse neighbourhoods with great food and good value. For a resort holiday with beaches, theme parks and pools, stay on Sentosa island instead.

Is Singapore walkable, and do I need to rent a car?

Singapore is very walkable in its central districts, and you do not need a car. The MRT metro is clean, cheap, air-conditioned and reaches almost everywhere a visitor wants to go, and taxis and ride-hailing are inexpensive by Western standards. The main thing to plan around is the heat and humidity, so build in indoor breaks and carry water.

When is the best time to visit Singapore?

Singapore sits almost on the equator, so it is warm and humid all year with no real seasons. February to April tends to be slightly drier and is generally considered the most comfortable window, while November to January is the wettest monsoon stretch. Rain usually comes as short heavy downpours rather than all-day washouts, so any month works if you pace yourself around the showers.
