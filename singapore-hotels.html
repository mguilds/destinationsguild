Singapore’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Singapore — Best Hotels by Style

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Singapore is one compact, superbly connected city-state, so instead of choosing a distant region you choose a style of stay. Do you want a Marina Bay landmark with a rooftop pool, a Chinatown shophouse boutique, a family resort on Sentosa, or a pet-friendly bolthole? We’ve grouped Singapore’s best-run hotels by category and character — with districts like Marina Bay, Orchard Road, Chinatown and Sentosa noted in each — every one with an honest note and a Book Me link that opens that exact property on Trip.com so you can just choose your dates.

Luxury

Singapore does grand hotels as well as anywhere on earth — soaring Marina Bay landmarks and heritage icons with flawless service, celebrated restaurants and the kind of pool that makes the stay part of the story.

Marina Bay Sands

$$$$ LuxuryMarina BayRooftop infinity poolIconic

The three-tower landmark crowned by a ship-shaped SkyPark and the world’s most famous infinity pool, reserved for hotel guests high above the bay. Below are a vast casino, luxury mall, celebrity-chef restaurants and the ArtScience Museum, with Gardens by the Bay next door. Rooms are sleek and the whole complex is an attraction in itself — the definitive Singapore splurge.

Raffles Hotel Singapore

$$$$ LuxuryCivic DistrictAll-suite colonialBirthplace of the Sling

The grand colonial dame of Singapore, a gleaming white 1887 landmark of shuttered verandahs, palm courtyards and butlered suites, beautifully restored a few years ago. The Long Bar still serves the original Singapore Sling, and the address near the Civic District puts the museums and Marina Bay within a short stroll. Timeless, romantic and utterly iconic.

Boutique

Singapore’s heritage shophouses have become some of Asia’s most characterful small hotels — design-led, personal and tucked into the historic lanes of Chinatown, Ann Siang and Robertson Quay, a world away from the glass towers.

The Warehouse Hotel

$$$ BoutiqueRobertson Quay1895 godownDesign icon

A multi-award-winning boutique set in a restored 1895 riverside warehouse on Robertson Quay, all exposed trusses, industrial-chic rooms and a moody pool. Its acclaimed restaurant and cocktail bar draw locals, and the Singapore River location is walkable to Clarke Quay and Chinatown. A stylish, atmospheric pick for design-minded travellers who want character over a big-hotel lobby.

Ann Siang House

$$$ BoutiqueChinatown / Ann SiangHeritage shophouseIntimate

An intimate design hotel woven through restored shophouses on lovely Ann Siang Hill, in the heart of Chinatown’s cafe-and-cocktail quarter. Rooms mix heritage bones with contemporary comfort, and the buzzy bars and restaurants of Club Street and Amoy Street are on the doorstep. Ideal for couples and independent travellers who want boutique charm in Singapore’s most walkable historic district.

Budget & Value

Singapore isn’t cheap, but smart, well-run value hotels — clever compact rooms and no-frills style near the MRT — let you sleep centrally and spend the savings on hawker feasts and attractions.

YOTEL Singapore Orchard Road

$$ ValueOrchard RoadSmart cabinsSky pool

A slick, tech-forward hotel on Orchard Road with efficient “cabin” rooms, adjustable SmartBeds and self-check-in kiosks, plus a surprising sky-high pool and gym. The prime shopping-street location sits right on the MRT, minutes from the malls and a short ride from Marina Bay. Great value for travellers who want a central, modern base without paying five-star rates.

Hotel G Singapore

$$ ValueBugis / BencoolenDesign-ledNear the MRT

A design-conscious value hotel on Middle Road between Bugis and the Civic District, with compact but stylish rooms, a lively ground-floor bar and bistro, and an unbeatable central location near two MRT lines. It punches above its price for style and position, walkable to Kampong Glam, Little India and the museums. A sharp, affordable base for city explorers.

Family-friendly

For families, Sentosa is the winning base — resorts within walking distance of Universal Studios, the aquarium and the beaches, with big pools and kids’ activities that turn the hotel into part of the holiday.

Village Hotel Sentosa by Far East Hospitality

$$$ FamilySentosa / ImbiahFour pools & waterslidesNear attractions

A cheerful, well-priced family resort on Sentosa with four themed pools, waterslides and a games arcade, moments from the Sentosa attractions and the beach trams. Interconnecting rooms and family suites suit groups, and the island setting means the theme parks and sand are on your doorstep. A practical, fun-first choice for parents who want value and non-stop things for the kids to do.

$$$ FamilyResorts World SentosaSteps to Universal StudiosBig free-form pool

Aquarium and Adventure Cove waterpark just steps away. Family rooms and buzzy dining make it a favourite for a theme-park-focused stay where you can walk between everything. Ideal for families who want the attractions on their doorstep.

Adults-only & grown-up retreats

Singapore has few strictly “adults-only” properties, but these grown-up, design-led hotels are aimed squarely at couples and solo travellers — no waterslides, just style, rooftop bars and calm. Confirm any age or occupancy policy with the property when you book.

The Clan Hotel Singapore by Far East Hospitality

$$$$ LuxuryChinatown / Telok AyerRooftop pool & barGrown-up calm

A sleek, adults-oriented luxury tower rising above Telok Ayer on the edge of Chinatown, with an intimate rooftop infinity pool, a stylish bar and personalised “clan” service. Its polished, grown-up feel and prime business-district-meets-heritage location suit couples and solo travellers over families. A sophisticated, calm hideaway in the thick of Singapore’s best dining and nightlife.

$$$$ LuxuryDuxton Hill / ChinatownRooftop barDesign & nightlife

A fashion-forward lifestyle hotel on heritage Duxton Hill, wrapping a striking tower around restored shophouses in Singapore’s most stylish nightlife enclave. Expect bold design, a rooftop bar and pool, and a scene-y crowd — very much a grown-ups’ address for couples who want cocktails and restaurants at the door. Chic and adult in spirit, in the heart of Chinatown’s Duxton.

Pet-friendly

Pet-friendly hotels are still uncommon in Singapore, and policies vary by room type, so always confirm the pet policy, size limits and any fee with the hotel before booking. These two Sentosa properties are among the most reliably welcoming.

W Singapore — Sentosa Cove

$$$$ LuxurySentosa CovePets welcomeLagoon pool

A bold, design-led Marriott resort in the marina enclave of Sentosa Cove, with a tropical lagoon pool, the buzzy WooBar and a well-known pet-friendly welcome for smaller dogs (confirm the current policy and fee when you book). Its stylish rooms and lively weekend scene suit couples and pet owners alike. A playful, upscale island base with a warm welcome for four-legged guests.

$$$$ LuxurySentosa / PalawanPet-friendly roomsColonial heritage

An elegant colonial-heritage hotel near Palawan Beach, converted from 1904 British army barracks, with a heated pool, complimentary afternoon tea and designated pet-friendly rooms (confirm the policy and any fee at booking). Refined, quiet and adults-in-spirit, it pairs history with resort calm on Sentosa. A gracious, dog-welcoming retreat for travellers who want heritage and hush by the beach.

Beachfront

Sentosa is Singapore’s beach escape, and a short causeway or cable-car ride from the city. These resorts put you right on or beside the island’s sands, trading skyline for sea breeze.

Shangri-La Rasa Sentosa, Singapore

$$$$ ResortSiloso BeachSingapore’s only beachfront resortFamily & spa

Singapore’s only true beachfront resort, set directly on Siloso Beach with sea-view rooms, a big free-form pool, a spa and a superb children’s club and activities. It manages to feel like a proper tropical getaway while the city skyline is 15 minutes away. The pick when you want sand underfoot, resort calm and family facilities all in one — book a sea-view room for the full effect.

All-inclusive & Resort

Singapore doesn’t really do all-inclusive, but its integrated resorts come closest — self-contained worlds on Sentosa where dining, pools, attractions and spa are all a stroll from your room.

Equarius Hotel, Resorts World Sentosa

$$$$ ResortResorts World SentosaRainforest settingAttractions on site

Aquarium, Universal Studios and Adventure Cove within the resort. It balances resort seclusion with everything-on-your-doorstep convenience, ideal for a stay where you barely need to leave. Great for families and couples who want an integrated-resort escape.

Spa & pool

When the day’s sightseeing is a wellness ritual, these hotels lead with the spa and the water — destination treatments and standout pools to soothe away the tropical heat.

Capella Singapore

$$$$ LuxurySentosaAuriga SpaThree cascading pools

A serene, low-rise sanctuary set in rainforest and heritage colonial buildings on Sentosa, with three cascading pools, private garden villas and the acclaimed Auriga Spa built around lunar-cycle rituals. Impeccable, discreet service and peacocks on the lawns make it a favourite for honeymooners and anyone craving calm. The island’s benchmark for a spa-and-pool retreat within reach of the city.

$$$$ LuxuryMarina BayThe SpaInfinity-edge pool

Freshly reborn after a top-to-toe renovation, this fan-shaped Marina Bay landmark pairs sweeping bay and skyline views with a sleek infinity-edge pool and a refined new spa. Central to the Marina Bay attractions and Suntec, it delivers city convenience with a resort-style wellness escape upstairs. A polished choice for travellers who want a spa and a great pool in the heart of the action.
