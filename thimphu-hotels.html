Where to Stay in Thimphu — Town, River and Ridge

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated November 2026

Thimphu is the only city in Bhutan and it is still a valley town: one long main road along the Wang Chhu, the Tashichho Dzong at the north end, the Clock Tower Square in the middle and the Buddha Dordenma looking down from the southern ridge. Almost every Bhutan trip spends two nights here, usually at the start. The choice divides cleanly: a handful of international names on the slopes, a group of Bhutanese-built hotels by the river, and a solid middle of town-centre places within a walk of the square. Every hotel below was opened on Trip.com’s own listing for this city and its street address read off the page before we wrote a word about it.

Thimphu questions, answered

Where is the best area to stay in Thimphu?

Within a few streets of Clock Tower Square, for a first visit. Thimphu is small and almost everything a visitor wants - the square, the craft shops, the weekend market, the restaurants and the walk up to the Memorial Chorten - is in that one pocket of town. The riverside and ridge hotels south of the centre are quieter and greener, but you will be driven to and from them. Since you will have a guide and a vehicle in Bhutan anyway, that is less of a penalty here than it would be in most countries.

How many nights do you need in Thimphu?

Two is the usual answer, and it is a good one. One day covers the Tashichho Dzong, the Buddha Dordenma on the ridge, the Memorial Chorten and the craft and textile museums at a civilised pace, with the weekend market added if your days line up. A second night is worth having because Thimphu sits at around 2,300 metres and arriving travellers often feel the altitude a little; it is a kinder place to adjust in than Paro.

Is Thimphu walkable?

The centre is, yes. The main road and the streets either side of it are flat, short and easy to cover on foot, and the town has no traffic lights at all - a policeman directs the main junction by hand, which tells you most of what you need to know about the scale of the place. What is not walkable is the Buddha Dordenma on the southern ridge and the Tashichho Dzong at the far north end; both are short drives.

Can you book Thimphu hotels directly, without a tour operator?

Yes. Since Bhutan reopened in September 2022 visitors may book their own hotels and flights, which is what the booking links on this page are for. What you cannot skip is the guide: the Department of Tourism requires a certified guide to accompany visitors at all times and to take you inside the dzongs and monuments. You must also stay in accommodation the Department has approved, which is why the list here is ordinary licensed hotels rather than private rentals.

Does the Sustainable Development Fee change which hotel you should pick?

It changes the arithmetic more than people expect. The fee is USD 100 per adult per night on top of everything else, so the gap between a plain town hotel and a smart one is a smaller share of your nightly total in Bhutan than almost anywhere else. If you were going to trade down to save money, you will save less than you think; if you were going to trade up for one night, it costs you proportionally less than it would elsewhere.
