Lake Fishing in Patagonia — Nahuel Huapi, Traful, Los Alerces and Lago Strobel

The big trout lakes of Argentine Patagonia: Nahuel Huapi at Bariloche, Lago Traful, Lago Lácar at San Martín de los Andes, Futalaufquen in Los Alerces National Park, Lago Fontana in Chubut, and Lago Strobel — the “Jurassic Lake” — on the Santa Cruz steppe. What swims in each, the 2026/27 rules from the bodies that set them, the two licences a visitor may need, and the towns to sleep in.

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Checked against the Patagonian fishing regulation 2026/2027 and the Province of Santa Cruz, 28 November 2026

This is the lake-fishing companion to our Fly Fishing in Argentine Patagonia book. That book follows the rivers — the Limay, the Chubut streams, the Río Grande. This one stays on the lakes, and it is for a wider crowd: a family who wants a morning in a boat out of Bariloche, a troller with a rod-holder, a shore angler at a quiet beach in a national park, and the traveller who has seen the photographs from Lago Strobel and wants to know whether they are real.

Patagonia’s trout are not native. Rainbow, brown and brook trout were brought in more than a century ago and took to the cold, clear lakes along the Andes. The native fish — the perca and the Patagonian pejerrey among them — are protected inside the national parks, where every one goes back.

Every rule, limit and price below was read on 28 November 2026 from the regulation itself, and is dated. Four things to know before you plan: this is the southern hemisphere, so the season opens on 1 November and a northern angler comes in our winter; one shared licence covers Neuquén, Río Negro, Chubut and the national parks, but Santa Cruz — and Lago Strobel — needs its own; felt-soled wading boots are banned on every lake under the shared regulation; and inside the national parks carburetted two-stroke outboards may not even be brought in. Where we could not confirm something, we say so.

Lago Nahuel Huapi — Bariloche’s lake

The great lake of the northern Andes, inside Nahuel Huapi National Park, with Bariloche on its southern shore and Villa La Angostura on its north-west shore. Fishing rules: the Administración de Parques Nacionales, in the shared Patagonian regulation.

Lago Nahuel Huapi

What swims there. Introduced rainbow, brown and brook trout, and native fish such as the perca. In a national park every native fish goes back, and so does any Atlantic (landlocked) salmon, anywhere in Patagonia.

Keeping a fish. From 1 November 2026 to 1 May 2027, a daily limit of 2 salmonids (trout or salmon). From 2 May to 31 May 2027 and through October 2027, the limit drops to 1 fish under 40 cm, and you may not fish within 200 metres of a river or stream mouth.

How people fish it. Spinning and fly from the shore and from boats, and trolling from a boat — which needs the extra trolling permit on top of your licence. Artificial lures only, one hook to a lure, no battery-powered lures, and no fishing from a boat within 200 metres of a river mouth. Daylight hours only.

Closed water and no-landing shores. The western part of the Brazo Campanario is closed to fishing from 1 November to 31 December 2026 and in October 2027. All season, no motor boats within 300 metres of the cliffs at the north and south ends of Isla Victoria and the west shore of Isla Fray Menéndez. No landing on the south shore of the Brazo Blest, the north shore of the Brazo Tristeza, or the western and southern De La Guardia islands.

Boats. Only four-stroke outboards, or two-strokes with factory ultra-low-emission certification, may come into the national parks — carry the engine manual, because a ranger may ask for it. No dogs or other pets on board. We did not confirm public boat ramps or boat hire for this page.

Nearest towns: Bariloche on the south shore, and Villa La Angostura on the north-west shore. Our Bariloche hotel guide has more on the town.

Two smaller neighbours. Lago Gutiérrez, just south of Bariloche, allows 2 salmonids and trolling from 1 November 2026 to 1 May 2027. Lago Mascardi, further along the road to El Bolsón, allows 2 salmonids over the same dates; in May and October 2027 it is shore fishing only, from the Ruta 40 side, and everything goes back.

Confirmed with the Patagonian regulation, 28 November 2026 — Reglamento de Pesca Deportiva Continental Patagónico 2026/2027, General Rules and the National Parks annex (entries for Nahuel Huapi, Gutiérrez and Mascardi). The regulation flags the invasive alga didymo in all three lakes.

Lago Traful — one village, one long lake

A long, narrow lake in the north of Nahuel Huapi National Park, with the small village of Villa Traful on its southern shore. Rules: the Administración de Parques Nacionales.

Lago Traful

Keeping a fish. The rules here are written around the rainbow trout. From 1 to 30 November 2026: single barbless hooks in every method, and a limit of 1 rainbow under 40 cm. From 1 December 2026 to 31 March 2027: 2 rainbows under 40 cm. Anything bigger, and any other fish, goes back.

How people fish it. Spinning, fly and trolling (with the trolling permit), from the shore and from boats. No fishing of any kind within 100 metres of the Bosque Sumergido, the drowned forest. The Río Traful, which leaves the lake, is fly fishing only with every fish released, and boats are banned on it.

Nearest town: Villa Traful, on the lake. It is small; book ahead in summer.

Confirmed with the Patagonian regulation, 28 November 2026 — National Parks annex, entries for Lago Traful and Río Traful. Didymo is present: clean your gear before and after.

Lago Lácar — San Martín de los Andes

A lake in Lanín National Park, with the town of San Martín de los Andes at its eastern end. Rules: the Administración de Parques Nacionales.

Lago Lácar

Season and limit. Open 1 November 2026 to 31 May 2027, and again through October 2027. 2 salmonids a day all season. Felt-soled boots banned.

How people fish it. Spinning and fly from the beaches, and trolling from a boat with the trolling permit. The national-park engine and pet rules apply here too.

Nearest town: San Martín de los Andes, on the shore — a good base, with the rivers of the Junín de los Andes country in our fly book an easy drive north.

Confirmed with the Patagonian regulation, 28 November 2026 — National Parks annex, entry for Lago Lácar.

Los Alerces — Futalaufquen and its neighbours

A chain of lakes in Los Alerces National Park, in the west of Chubut. The park’s small village and offices are at Villa Futalaufquen; the nearest full town is Esquel. Rules: the Administración de Parques Nacionales.

Lago Futalaufquen

Season and limit. Open all year, with a limit of 2 salmonids a day.

Where you may not go. No boats and no fishing in the Bahía Anacleto, between the Arroyo Condesa and the north end of the bay. No landing on the lake’s west shore except at Playa Blanca. Didymo is present: treat your gear when you leave.

How people fish it. From the beaches and from boats; the general rules on lures, engines and felt soles apply.

Two neighbours with stricter rules

Lago Verde is fly only (or a spinning rod with a float and a fly), every fish released, no trolling and no fishing with the engine running. Lago Menéndez may be fished only with licensed guides and the boats kept on the lake; ask the rangers or the park office.

Staying in the park. The park’s own services page lists campgrounds, cabins and hosterías around the lakes, with phone numbers. Our booking button is for Esquel, outside the park, where Trip.com holds hotel rooms. Trevelin, the other nearby town, has no button: we could not confirm a working town number for it.

Confirmed with the Patagonian regulation, 28 November 2026 — National Parks annex, entries for Futalaufquen, Verde and Menéndez; and the Los Alerces services page on argentina.gob.ar, read the same day.

Lago Fontana — a provincial lake in Chubut

A large lake in the Andean foothills of south-west Chubut, outside the national parks. Rules: the Province of Chubut’s Dirección de Pesca Continental, in the same shared regulation.

Lago Fontana

Season and limit. Fishing opens on 1 October with every fish released until the general season starts on 1 November; trolling is allowed; the limit is 3 salmonids a day. Every stream that flows into the lake closes to fishing from 1 March 2027, and the Arroyo Fontana is fly only, catch and release.

Chubut’s own rules. In Chubut, barbs are banned on every hook, in every method. Lead is banned — sinkers, lead-headed lures and lead anchors. Felt-soled boots are banned, and the province requires you to wash all your fishing gear.

Nearest town — and why there is no button. The nearest town is Alto Río Senguer, to the east. Our partner lists the town, but we could not show that it holds rooms there, so this lake has no booking link. Plan it as a day or a camping trip from a bigger base, and confirm a bed before you go.

Confirmed with the Patagonian regulation, 28 November 2026 — Chubut annex, general rules and the entries for Lago Fontana and Arroyo Fontana.

Lago Strobel — the “Jurassic Lake”

A big lake on the high, windy steppe of Santa Cruz, a long way from any town, famous for rainbow trout of a size that seems unlikely until you see one. Rules: the Province of Santa Cruz — not the shared regulation above.

Lago Strobel

The rules. Open all year. Catch and release only — every fish goes back. Single hook, with or without a barb. No boats with an engine or any mechanical drive, at all. Clean and disinfect your gear at the end of every day, because of didymo.

The river at the lodges’ door. The Río Barrancoso, which flows into the lake, is catch and release over its whole length, and anglers must carry a daily disinfection certificate there.

How people fish it. On foot, from the shore and at the inlets, almost always with a fly rod and nearly always in wind. Most visiting anglers fish it from a lodge, which arranges the transfer. Jurassic Lake Lodge says it holds more than ten miles of private land on the lake’s south-western shore, within walking distance of the Barrancoso mouth, and runs a charter flight of about an hour from El Calafate.

Nearest towns: Gobernador Gregores is the nearest town; El Calafate, with its airport, is the usual gateway for lodge guests flying in.

Confirmed with the Province of Santa Cruz, 28 November 2026 — the 2026/2027 Reglamento de Pesca and its list of waters (Ambientes) on pesca.santacruz.gob.ar, entries for Lago Strobel and Río Barrancoso; and the Ministry of Production’s notice of 1 October 2026 opening the season (Disposición N.º 241/26).

The licence and the limits, plainly

Two licences cover this page. Prices are in Argentine pesos and are reset every season; Argentina’s exchange rate moves fast, so check what they come to on the day.

Nahuel Huapi, Traful, Lácar, Los Alerces and Fontana — the shared Patagonian licence

What it covers. One licence, sold by the provinces of Neuquén, Río Negro and Chubut and by the National Parks, is valid in all four jurisdictions. It does not cover Santa Cruz.

Prices for 2026/2027, for visitors who do not live in Argentina: one day $50,000; ten days $140,000; the whole season $280,000; a ten-day licence for residents of a neighbouring country $50,000. For residents of Argentina: one day $12,500; ten days $25,000; season $50,000; ages 13–17 season $25,000; over-65s, retirees, children up to 12 and people with an official disability certificate free.

Trolling costs extra. To troll from a boat you need a trolling permit on top of the licence: one day $25,000; ten days $50,000; season $100,000.

Dates and where to buy. The season licence is valid from the day you buy it to 31 October 2027. The Government of Río Negro sends anglers to permisosdepesca.com.ar; Neuquén sells online at cazaypesca.neuquen.gob.ar. Carry it with your passport or ID.

The rules everywhere. Season 1 November 2026 to 1 May 2027 unless a lake says otherwise. Daylight only. Artificial lures only, with one hook. In rivers and streams — and within 200 metres of where they meet a lake — every fish goes back. On a lake with no special rule, you may keep one fish a day from 1 December 2026 to 31 March 2027. No felt soles. No lead.

Confirmed with the Patagonian regulation, 28 November 2026 — Reglamento de Pesca Deportiva Continental Patagónico 2026/2027 (Neuquén, Río Negro, Chubut and the Administración de Parques Nacionales), articles 2–23; Government of Río Negro season notice; Neuquén permit sales notice of 16 October 2026.

Lago Strobel — the Santa Cruz licence

What it covers. Santa Cruz only. The permit is personal and is managed through the province’s SIPESQ system at pesca.santacruz.gob.ar.

Prices for 2026/2027. Visitors who do not live in Argentina: one day $90,000; one week $300,000; the whole season $450,000. Residents of Argentina: one day $15,000; a Saturday–Sunday weekend $20,000; one week $25,000; season $40,000. Trolling is an extra permit, though not on Lago Strobel, where engines are banned.

Dates. The Santa Cruz season runs 1 October 2026 to 30 September 2027. Your lodge will normally tell you how to get the permit; ask.

Confirmed with the Province of Santa Cruz, 28 November 2026 — Ministry of Production notice of 1 October 2026 and the 2026/2027 Reglamento on pesca.santacruz.gob.ar.

Clean your gear — didymo

The regulation warns of didymo, an invasive alga, in many of these lakes, and a new invader, a freshwater jellyfish. Its cleaning method: before you leave the water, remove every scrap of weed and mud; drain the boat; then scrub the gear for at least a minute in water hotter than 60°C, or a 5% salt solution, or 5% biodegradable detergent, or 2% bleach. If you cannot clean it, dry it completely and wait 48 hours before using it again.

Questions

Is one fishing licence good for Bariloche and Lago Strobel?

No. One shared Patagonian licence covers Neuquén, Río Negro, Chubut and the National Parks, which takes in Nahuel Huapi, Traful, Lácar, Los Alerces and Lago Fontana. Lago Strobel is in Santa Cruz, which sells its own licence through its SIPESQ system. For 2026/2027 a visitor from outside Argentina paid 280,000 pesos for a season on the shared licence (50,000 for one day), and 450,000 pesos for a season in Santa Cruz (90,000 for one day). Trolling needs an extra permit. Checked 28 November 2026.

How many trout can I keep at Lago Nahuel Huapi?

Two salmonids (trout or salmon) a day from 1 November 2026 to 1 May 2027. In May and October 2027 the limit is one fish under 40 cm, and you may not fish within 200 metres of a river or stream mouth. Native fish and Atlantic (landlocked) salmon must always be released. Checked against the 2026/2027 Patagonian regulation on 28 November 2026.

When is the fishing season in Patagonia?

For Neuquén, Río Negro, Chubut and the National Parks the 2026/2027 season runs from 1 November 2026 to 1 May 2027, with exceptions for some lakes: Futalaufquen is open all year, Lácar is open to 31 May 2027 and again in October 2027, and Lago Fontana opens for catch and release on 1 October. Santa Cruz runs its own season, 1 October 2026 to 30 September 2027, and Lago Strobel is open all year.

Can I keep a fish or use my own boat on Lago Strobel?

No to both. Under Santa Cruz's 2026/2027 rules Lago Strobel is catch and release only, with a single hook, and boats with an engine or any mechanical drive are banned outright. Gear must be cleaned and disinfected at the end of each day, and anglers on the Río Barrancoso must carry a daily disinfection certificate. Most visitors fish it from a lodge.

Which towns should I stay in?

Bariloche or Villa La Angostura for Lago Nahuel Huapi; Villa Traful for Lago Traful; San Martín de los Andes for Lago Lácar; Esquel, or the park's own cabins, for Futalaufquen and Los Alerces; and Gobernador Gregores, or El Calafate as the gateway by air, for Lago Strobel. Each has a hotel button on this page pinned to that town on Trip.com, and a vacation-homes button. Lago Fontana has no booking button.
