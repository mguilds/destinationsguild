Where to Stay in Chicago — the Loop, the Magnificent Mile, River North & Lincoln Park

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated November 2026

Chicago is a big city that is easy to visit, because almost everything a first-time visitor wants sits in a short stretch along the lake. The Loop has Millennium Park and the river; the Magnificent Mile and the Gold Coast just north of the river have the shopping and the grand old hotels; River North is the place for restaurants and nightlife; Streeterville runs out to Navy Pier; and Lincoln Park, a little further up the shore, is greener and quieter. This page sorts the areas and names the hotels we could confirm today.

Choosing Your Area in Chicago

Five parts of the city, each suited to a different trip.

The Loop

Choose Chicago, the city’s official visitors bureau, calls the Loop the city centre and “home to some of Chicago’s most iconic attractions and architecture”. Millennium Park and the Chicago River are both here.

Stay here for sightseeing and museums. It is busiest on weekdays with office workers and quieter at night than River North.

The Magnificent Mile and the Gold Coast

North Michigan Avenue, just over the river. The bureau describes the Magnificent Mile as “one of the world’s most beautiful and iconic shopping districts” and the Gold Coast beside it as “a luxe haven for designer showrooms and chic restaurants”. The lakefront and Oak Street Beach are at its northern end.

Good for shoppers, couples and anyone who wants the classic Chicago hotel experience.

River North

West of Michigan Avenue and north of the river. The bureau calls it “an urban nightlife mecca with plenty of dining and jaw-dropping architecture”.

Pick it if dinner and a night out matter more than a quiet street. It is walkable to the Loop and the Magnificent Mile.

Streeterville and Navy Pier

East of Michigan Avenue, out to the lake. The bureau describes “big city buzz” in a neighbourhood “nestled near the lakefront’s Navy Pier”.

A good base for families: the pier, the lakefront path and the Magnificent Mile are all close by.

Lincoln Park and Old Town

North along the lake. The bureau calls Lincoln Park “one of the city’s most picturesque areas”, with boutiques, museums, dining, a zoo and plenty of waterfront, and says Old Town has “a legendary improv theatre” and loads of charm.

Choose it for a calmer, neighbourhood stay with the park and the beach on your doorstep. Downtown is a bus or train ride away.

Getting There & When to Go

The handful of Chicago details that change how a trip goes.

Getting to Chicago

Chicago has two big airports. Choose Chicago says O’Hare International (ORD) lies 16 miles from downtown and Midway International (MDW) only 11 miles. The CTA Blue Line ‘L’ train runs 24/7 and connects directly to O’Hare, and the Orange Line connects directly to Midway; taxi, Lyft and Uber pick-up points are at both airports.

By rail, Union Station downtown is served by Amtrak and by Metra trains to northeast Illinois.

Getting around

You do not need a car downtown. The bureau says the CTA runs around 130 bus routes and that the ‘L’ serves more than 140 stations across the city and nearby suburbs. If you do drive, check your hotel’s own parking page before you arrive.

When to go

Choose Chicago’s weather guide gives April temperatures typically in the 50s°F, with May reaching up to around 70°F. Summer brings festivals and patio season, and June is typically the wettest month. In autumn October is usually the driest month, with temperatures falling from around 70°F to the mid-40s°F.

Winter is cold: the bureau gives average daily highs around 30–38°F and average annual snowfall of about 37 inches. The bureau also points to the holiday light displays, such as ZooLights at Lincoln Park Zoo.

Chicago Hotels FAQ

The questions travellers ask most before booking a room here.

Where is the best area to stay in Chicago?

The Loop for Millennium Park, the museums and the river. The Magnificent Mile or the Gold Coast for shopping and classic hotels. River North for restaurants and nightlife. Streeterville for Navy Pier and the lakefront. Lincoln Park for a quieter neighbourhood stay near the zoo and the beach.

How do I get from O’Hare or Midway to downtown Chicago?

The CTA Blue Line train runs 24/7 and connects directly to O’Hare, which Choose Chicago puts 16 miles from downtown. The Orange Line connects directly to Midway, 11 miles from downtown. Taxis, Lyft and Uber pick up at both airports.

Do I need a car in Chicago?

Usually not if you stay downtown or in Lincoln Park. The CTA runs around 130 bus routes and the ‘L’ serves more than 140 stations.

What is the best time of year to visit Chicago?

Late spring to early autumn is the easiest weather: May can reach around 70°F, summer is festival season, and October is usually the driest month. Winter highs average around 30–38°F with about 37 inches of snow a year, and holiday light displays.

Which part of Chicago is best for families?

Streeterville, for Navy Pier and the lakefront, or Lincoln Park, where the Lincoln Park Zoo is free and North Avenue Beach is close by. Both are a short ride from the Loop museums.
