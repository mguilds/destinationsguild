The Amalfi Coast — Where to Stay

Where to Stay on the Amalfi Coast — Towns & Hotels

The Amalfi Coast isn't one place to book — it's a string of cliff-clinging towns, each with its own character. The right base depends on whether you want Positano's glamour, Ravello's serene heights, Amalfi's history, Praiano's quiet, or Sorrento's easy connections. Below we sort the coast by town, with an honest note on who each suits and hand-picked hotels. Every pick has a Book Me link that opens that exact property on Trip.com — just choose your dates.

Positano — The Postcard

Best for honeymoons and first-timers who want the iconic vertical view — glamorous, romantic, and steep (expect a lot of stairs).

Le Sirenuse

$$$$ LuxuryPositanoHoneymoonSea view

The former summer home of the Sersale family, still family-run, and the definitive Positano address — all sun-bleached terracotta, blue-and-white tiles, and a terrace pool aimed straight at the sea. The Michelin-starred La Sponda dining room is lit by hundreds of candles at night. This is the splurge people remember for a lifetime.

Il San Pietro di Positano

$$$$ LuxuryPositanoCliffsidePrivate beach

Carved into the cliff just outside town, this Relais & Châteaux icon is defined by cascading flowered terraces and an elevator that drops through the rock to a private sea platform. Quieter and more secluded than the in-town hotels — the choice for couples who want privacy over foot traffic, with a shuttle into Positano when you want it.

Hotel Poseidon Positano

$$$ UpscalePositanoFamily-runGreat value

A warm, family-owned four-star that delivers the same postcard panorama for meaningfully less than the grand dames. The pool terrace and many rooms look straight over the domed church and the bay, and the welcome is genuinely personal. The sweet spot for a Positano stay that feels special without the top-tier price.

Amalfi & Atrani — Central & Historic

Best for travelers who want a walkable town at sea level, ferry connections in every direction, and real history at the door — the coast's most central base.

Hotel Santa Caterina

$$$$ LuxuryAmalfiSea viewSaltwater pool

A century-old, family-run grande dame perched just outside Amalfi town, with an Art Nouveau villa, terraced citrus gardens, and a glass elevator down to a saltwater pool and sea deck. Understated old-world elegance rather than showy glamour — a longtime favorite for guests who value gracious service and privacy close to town.

Anantara Convento di Amalfi Grand Hotel

$$$$ LuxuryAmalfiHistoric conventClifftop

A 13th-century former monastery set high above Amalfi, wrapped by an original cloister and a dramatic cliff-edge infinity pool. The setting is genuinely unusual — vaulted ceilings and a hanging garden of wisteria — and the elevated position trades beach access for one of the most memorable views on the coast.

Ravello — High, Quiet & Full of Gardens

Best for honeymooners and travelers seeking calm, culture, and the coast's most sweeping views — refined and peaceful, but perched high above the water (no beach).

Palazzo Avino

$$$$ LuxuryRavelloHoneymoonPanoramic view

A 12th-century villa turned coral-pink five-star, known as "The Pink Palace," with impeccable service and a terrace that seems to float over the whole coastline. Elegant and romantic, with a Michelin-starred restaurant and a beach club shuttle far below — the polished choice for a Ravello honeymoon.

Belmond Hotel Caruso

$$$$ LuxuryRavelloInfinity poolHistoric

An 11th-century palace whose cliff-edge infinity pool is one of the most photographed in Italy, appearing to spill straight into the sea more than a thousand feet below. Frescoed ceilings, terraced gardens, and Belmond's polish make it the grandest stay in Ravello — pure serenity at altitude.

Hotel Villa Cimbrone

$$$ UpscaleRavelloLegendary gardensQuiet

A storied villa attached to the famous Ravello gardens and their Terrace of Infinity, once a retreat for Greta Garbo and Bloomsbury writers. Wonderfully secluded at the end of a pedestrian lane, it's for guests who want history and hush over resort amenities — you stay inside one of the coast's great romantic legends.

Praiano — Quieter, Between Positano & Amalfi

Best for couples and repeat visitors who want the views and sunsets without the crowds — central to both Positano and Amalfi, and noticeably calmer.

Casa Angelina

$$$$ LuxuryPraianoDesign hotelSunset views

A striking all-white, design-forward hotel that breaks from the coast's traditional tiled palette, with contemporary art and clean lines framing an open sea view. Praiano's west-facing position gives it the finest sunsets on the coast, and an elevator lift plus shuttle reach a small swimming platform below. Chic, adults-focused, and blissfully quiet.

Hotel Tramonto d'Oro

$$$ UpscalePraianoRooftop poolFamily-run

Its name means "golden sunset," and the rooftop pool terrace lives up to it, looking back toward Positano as the light fades. A long-running, family-owned four-star with warm service and honest value, well placed for the coastal bus and walking paths. A dependable, unpretentious Praiano base.

Sorrento — The Best-Connected Base

Best for families, first-timers, and anyone doing day trips (Pompeii, Capri, the coast) — flat, walkable, and served by direct trains, ferries, and buses just outside the coast proper.

Grand Hotel Excelsior Vittoria

$$$$ LuxurySorrentoHistoricClifftop gardens

Owned by the same family since 1834 and set in five acres of clifftop gardens above the marina, this is Sorrento's grande dame — Caruso sang here and it has hosted royalty for generations. Belle Époque grandeur, a private lift down to the port, and a level of history few hotels on the coast can match.

Bellevue Syrene

$$$$ LuxurySorrentoCliffsideRomantic

A romantic 1820s villa built over an ancient Roman site, cascading down the cliff to its own sea access with frescoed lounges and antique-filled rooms. Intimate and characterful rather than large, with sweeping bay views toward Vesuvius. A lovely, atmospheric alternative to the bigger grand hotels.

Value & Quieter Bases — Minori, Maiori & Atrani

Best for budget-minded travelers and families who want a real beach, local prices, and fewer crowds — a short bus or ferry hop from Amalfi and the marquee towns.

Hotel Villa Romana (Minori)

$$ Mid-rangeMinoriRooftop poolGood value

A friendly, well-run hotel in low-key Minori, a genuine working town with a sandy beach and a famous pastry tradition. A panoramic rooftop pool and terrace deliver coast views at a fraction of Positano's rates, and Amalfi is minutes away by bus or ferry. Smart value for travelers who'd rather spend on the coast than on the room.

Hotel Botanico San Lazzaro (Maiori)

$$$ UpscaleMaioriInfinity poolPanoramic

Perched high above Maiori — the coast's longest beach town — this boutique hotel offers an infinity pool and terraces with an enormous, uncluttered sea view. It over-delivers on setting for the price, and a shuttle connects to the beach and center below. A relaxed, scenic base away from the crush.

Palazzo Ferraioli (Atrani)

$$ Mid-rangeAtraniBoutiqueWalk to Amalfi

A boutique four-star in tiny Atrani, the coast's smallest and most intact fishing village, a ten-minute seaside walk from Amalfi's cathedral square. Atrani keeps its everyday, un-touristy feel, and a spa plus rooftop terrace round out a characterful stay in the heart of the coast at a fair price.
