São Paulo’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in São Paulo — Best Hotels by Style

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated July 2026

São Paulo is vast, but visitors rarely stray far from a few well-connected districts: chic Jardins for restaurants and design, Avenida Paulista for museums and the metro, and the business towers of Faria Lima and Berrini. We’ve grouped the city’s best hotels by style — each with an honest note and a Book Me link that opens that exact property on Trip.com.
