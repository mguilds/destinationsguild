Lake Fishing in Southern California — Eight Waters, from Mountain Trout to City Bass

A high lake in the pines above San Bernardino, trophy bass an hour from Los Angeles, and cheap day permits at the San Diego reservoirs: the lakes, the rules on bringing a boat, and where to sleep.

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Checked against the agencies that run each lake and the California Department of Fish and Wildlife, 4 November 2026

Southern California is better known for its beaches, but it is full of lakes, nearly all of them man-made and built to hold water for the cities. That makes them easy to reach and well organised, and it means bass, stocked trout and catfish within an hour or two of twenty million people. The best are worth a trip of their own.

This is the lake volume of our California fishing guide, written for families with a spinning rod, bass anglers with a boat, and people who just want a lake and a cabin. Everything below that is a rule or a price was read from the agency that sets it on 4 November 2026. Where we could not confirm something, we say so.

Rent at the lake and nearly all of this goes away.

Why the rules are so strict now

Tiny freshwater mussels, quagga and now golden mussels, can clog a water system, and these lakes are water systems. Golden mussels were first found in North America in California in October 2024 and reached Silverwood and Pyramid lakes by 2025. Each agency has set its own response, so a boat that is welcome at one lake can be refused at the next.

Big Bear Lake — the mountain lake

The summer escape, at 6,752 feet in the San Bernardino Mountains.

Big Bear Lake

What swims there. Rainbow and brown trout, largemouth and smallmouth bass, channel and blue catfish, crappie, bluegill and carp, says the water district’s record list.

How people fish it. From a rented boat, from your own, or from shore. The district lists at least seven marinas. It is a village lake: you can fish in the morning and walk to lunch.

Confirmed with the Big Bear Municipal Water District, 4 November 2026 — “All vessels, both motorized and non-motorized must be inspected and permitted BEFORE they enter Big Bear Lake!” 2026 permits: registered vessels one day $46, season $148; non-registered craft such as kayaks and float tubes one day $29, season $68. The lake stood 9.48 feet down from full, at 52.2°F; the East Ramp was open daily from 7 a.m. to 3 p.m. and the West Ramp closed. OEHHA advice for women 18–49 and children: rainbow trout and crappie up to 5 servings a week, black bass and carp 1.

Boats and bait: Pine Knot Marina and Holloway’s Marina, which rents fishing boats and sells tackle. Nearest town: Big Bear Lake.

Nearby, and not open to all: Lake Arrowhead

Lake Arrowhead is run by the Arrowhead Lake Association for its members. We could not read its current fishing rules, so ask before you plan on it.

Castaic, Casitas and Cachuma — the coast-side lakes

Big lakes within a morning’s drive of Los Angeles and Santa Barbara.

Castaic Lake, off Interstate 5

What swims there. Trophy largemouth bass, striped bass and rainbow trout, says the lake’s website, with catfish and sunfish as well.

How people fish it. From shore, a rental boat, your own boat, a kayak or a float tube. Fishing from the dam is allowed in designated areas; fishing from docks and launch ramps is not. Castaic Landings at the West Ramp rents fishing boats, $100 a weekday and $130 a weekend day, Wednesday to Sunday, first come first served.

Confirmed from the Castaic Lake site and state records, 4 November 2026 — “Castaic Lake will be closed on Mondays and Tuesdays until further notice.” Vehicle $15, boat launch $13, non-motorised $9; state park passes are not accepted. The 24-hour fishing pier is closed until further notice. Storage was 84% of capacity on 3 November. The site still shows an old 15-inch bass limit; the state regulation is what counts. OEHHA advice for women 18–49 and children: black bass and channel catfish, DO NOT EAT.

Nearest towns: Castaic and Santa Clarita.

Lake Casitas, near Ojai

What swims there. Largemouth bass, rainbow trout, channel catfish, bluegill, crappie and redear sunfish. The water district buys rainbow trout and plants them at intervals through the year.

How people fish it. From a rented boat or from shore. The Marina Bait & Tackle Shop rents boats, cash only for licences; telephone (805) 649-2043 for rates. Do not bring your own boat without planning five weeks ahead.

Confirmed with Casitas Municipal Water District, 4 November 2026 — “All vessels, including kayaks, canoes, and trailered boats, must undergo a mandatory inspection and 35-day quarantine before launching at Lake Casitas.” “Inflatable watercraft are not permitted.” Boats 11 to 35 feet; day launch $20; first inspection with cable and lock $75. See the district’s page. OEHHA advice for women 18–49 and children: black bass and carp 1 serving a week.

Nearest towns: Ojai and Ventura.

Lake Cachuma, in the Santa Ynez Valley

What swims there. Planted rainbow trout, bass and carp. We could not read Santa Barbara County’s own lake pages, so treat this entry as a pointer and telephone before you drive.

How people fish it. Cachuma Lake Marina and Boat Rentals, (805) 688-4040, is reported to rent boats and kayaks. Reports say private boats need inspecting and tagging 30 days ahead.

Confirmed with the state, 4 November 2026 — Storage was 83% of capacity and 133% of average on 3 November, one of the fullest lakes in this guide. OEHHA advice for women 18–49 and children: black bass, DO NOT EAT.

Nearest towns: Santa Ynez and Solvang. Wine country, which is no hardship.

Diamond Valley and Perris — the Inland Empire lakes

Big, modern reservoirs, an hour or so east of Orange County.

Diamond Valley Lake, Hemet

What swims there. Largemouth and smallmouth bass, channel and blue catfish, rainbow trout, bluegill and redear sunfish.

How people fish it. Mostly by boat: the marina rents bass boats and pontoons, to those aged 21 or over. Shore fishing is limited to about two and a half miles of shoreline, a quarter of a mile or more from the parking lot, on a moderate to difficult hike.

Confirmed from the Diamond Valley Marina, 4 November 2026 — An $11 parking pass and a $9 Fishing Access Pass for everyone who fishes, adults and children alike, plus $4 for shoreline. Boats must be at least 12 feet long, 42 inches wide; sit-on-top kayaks are not allowed. Speed limit 25 mph. The marina home page shows it closed Mondays and Tuesdays. OEHHA advice for women 18–49 and children: black bass, DO NOT EAT. See dvmarina.com.

Nearest town: Hemet.

Lake Perris, State Recreation Area

What swims there. Bass, carp, sunfish and silverside, by the state’s health survey, with trout and catfish reported as well. State Parks does not publish a species list.

How people fish it. From shore, or from a rental: Rec-Out at Lake Perris Marina rents fishing boats and kayaks and sells bait and tackle. Fishing is not allowed in swim areas, or from launch ramps and courtesy docks.

Confirmed with California State Parks, 4 November 2026 — Vehicle $10, summer weekends and holidays $20; boat launch $10. The east end closes on waterfowl hunting days until 12:30 p.m. Storage 89% of capacity on 3 November. OEHHA advice for women 18–49 and children: black bass 2 servings a week, the mildest in this guide.

Nearest towns: Perris and Moreno Valley.

Two more worth a day

Irvine Lake in Orange County is open for shoreline fishing only, Fridays to Sundays, 7 a.m. to 4 p.m. OC Parks says no fishing licence is required there. Silverwood Lake State Recreation Area has a marina and charges $10 a vehicle; golden mussels were found there in 2025, so boats are inspected when they leave.

Lake Isabella — the south end of the Sierra

Pair it with the Kern River. Low this year, and windy every year.

Lake Isabella, Kern County

What swims there. Trout, bass, catfish, crappie and bluegill, says French Gulch Marina.

How people fish it. From a boat or the shore. French Gulch Marina is open all year, rents fishing boats and pontoons, and sells bait, tackle and boat permits. No fishing from the courtesy dock.

Confirmed with the Forest Service and the state, 4 November 2026 — “Kern County requires all watercraft over 7 feet and any watercraft which utilizes a motor to obtain a Lake Isabella permit.” The Forest warns of reliable afternoon winds, from 35 to 50 miles an hour, so be off the water early. Storage 24% of capacity on 3 November, 97% of average for the date. OEHHA advice for women 18–49 and children: black bass and carp 1 serving a week.

Nearest towns: Lake Isabella and Kernville.

The rules they share

What swims there. Largemouth bass, sunfish, catfish, crappie and carp; Miramar and Murray add trout.

Confirmed with the City of San Diego, 4 November 2026 — “Anglers 16 years of age or older must have a valid California fishing license.” City permit: adults $10, youth 8–15 $5, seniors 65 and over $7, under 8 free; cash or check only. All are closed on Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year’s Day. No paddleboards and no swimming. Upper Otay is catch and release only, with artificial lures and barbless hooks. OEHHA advice at El Capitan for women 18–49 and children: black bass and blue catfish, DO NOT EAT. See the City’s reservoir pages.

Children and stocked city ponds

Under 16 needs no licence. CDFW’s Fishing in the City programme stocks urban lakes with trout and catfish in the Los Angeles, Orange County and Inland Empire regions, and its fish planting schedule shows what went in and where. Start there with a youngster.

Questions

Do I need a fishing licence for a lake in Southern California?

Yes, from age 16. California’s 2026 annual licence is $64.54 for residents and $174.14 for visitors, valid 365 days from purchase; a one-day licence is $21.09. Some lakes also charge their own fee: Diamond Valley sells a $9 Fishing Access Pass, and San Diego’s city lakes sell a $10 adult permit.

Can I bring my own boat to Lake Casitas?

Only with planning. Casitas requires a mandatory inspection and a 35-day quarantine before launching, for every vessel including kayaks, and inflatables are not allowed. For a visitor it is far simpler to rent at the lake.

Can I fish Lake Arrowhead?

Lake Arrowhead is run by the Arrowhead Lake Association for its members. We could not read its current rules, so ask the association before you plan on it. Big Bear Lake, further along the mountains, is open to everyone.

Is it safe to eat bass from Southern California lakes?

Often not for everyone. The state’s OEHHA says women aged 18 to 49 and children should not eat black bass from Castaic, Cachuma, Diamond Valley or El Capitan, because of mercury. Lake Perris has the mildest advice of the lakes here. Bass are a good fish to release.

Which Southern California lake is best for a family with a rented boat?

Big Bear Lake in summer: it has several marinas and a village to walk. Lake Perris is the easy low-country choice, and Castaic Landings rents fishing boats by the day, but note the park’s two weekly closed days.
