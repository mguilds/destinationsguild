Xi’an’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Xi'an — Best Areas & Hotels

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Most of Xi’an’s life happens inside its great Ming City Wall, around the floodlit Bell Tower and the fragrant lanes of the Muslim Quarter — and that’s where most travellers want to sleep, with the Terracotta Army an easy day trip east. We’ve grouped Xi’an’s most enduring, well-run hotels by style and character, each with an honest note and a Book Me link that opens that exact property on Trip.com so you can just choose your dates.

Luxury

Xi’an’s finest hotels blend Tang-dynasty grandeur with five-star polish, from a garden palace that once hosted state guests to a sleek tower with skyline views. Book here when you want the stay to feel as historic as the city.

Sofitel Legend People’s Grand Hotel Xi’an

$$$$ LuxuryInside the City WallHistoric gardensGrand

Set in a landmark 1950s state guesthouse within leafy walled gardens inside the City Wall, this is Xi’an’s grandest address, with opulent rooms, a fine spa and a real sense of occasion. It’s central, serene and steeped in history. The definitive luxury choice for travellers who want heritage grandeur a short drive from the Bell Tower.

Budget & Value

Xi’an is kind to a budget: a clean, central room near the City Wall costs little, and puts the Bell Tower, the Muslim Quarter and the metro to the Terracotta Army within easy reach. Trade frills for location and reliability.

$ BudgetBy the City WallReliable chainMetro nearby

A tidy, dependable budget hotel right by the Heping Gate of the City Wall, with compact modern rooms, comfortable beds and the reassuring consistency of the Ibis brand. A metro stop and the walled centre are within easy reach. A smart, low-cost base for travellers who want somewhere clean and central and plan to be out sightseeing all day.

Adults-Only & Romantic Retreats

For a honeymoon or a grown-up escape, these are Xi’an’s most romantic addresses — a hot-spring spa resort at the foot of Mount Li and a serene garden hotel within the walls. Not formally child-free, but adult and restful in mood; confirm any specifics when you book.
