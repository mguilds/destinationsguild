The Guild Fishing Library

The fishing wing of Destinations Guild: lake guides state by state, fly fishing books for rivers at home and abroad, and saltwater guides — every one of them tied to a real town with a bed in it.

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Opened 4 November 2026

Welcome in. This is the front door to the Guild’s fishing shelves, and it is being built one guide at a time, in the open, where you can see exactly how far along it is.

What is behind this door: lake fishing guides, state by state — the waters worth the drive, the rules that govern them, the months they fish best; fly fishing books for rivers here and abroad; a directory of guides and outfitters; and saltwater guides for the flats. Every guide is written by the Guild Explorers to be fished from rather than browsed, which means two things we hold to. Every rule, limit, season and licence price is read from the agency that sets it — the state wildlife department, the Park Service, the Forest Service, the water authority — and carries the date it was read. And every water is tied to the nearest town that has somewhere to sleep, pinned to that exact town, because a lake you cannot stay beside is a lake you cannot fish properly.

Where a guide is not written yet, this page says Being written and gives you no link. We would rather show you an honest gap than a door that opens onto nothing. One new state goes up a day.

How these guides are written

The rule comes from the body that sets it

Licence prices, bag limits, tackle rules, seasons and closures are read from the state wildlife agency, the Park Service, the Forest Service or the water authority — never from a lodge, a marina or a booking site — and every guide prints the date it was read. Rules change, and they differ water by water. Always check the agency before you fish.

Nothing is invented

We do not invent a lodge, a guide, a price, an opening time or a limit. Where we could not confirm something, the guide says so in plain words instead of filling the gap.

Every water is tied to a bed

Each lake is matched to the nearest town or two that has somewhere to stay, and the booking link is pinned to that exact town rather than searched by name — which is how readers end up in the wrong state. A lake with no town near it that we could confirm is described honestly and gets no booking link.

One guide a day, not a flood

Fewer, correct guides beat a shelf full of thin ones. One new state goes up a day, and we check how the first ones are faring before speeding up.

Questions

What is in the Guild Fishing Library?

It is the fishing wing of Destinations Guild: lake guides state by state, fly fishing books for rivers at home and abroad, a directory of guides and outfitters, and saltwater guides. Every guide is written to be fished from — the waters, the rules that govern them, the best months, and the nearest towns with somewhere to stay.

Which United States fishing guides are live so far?

Seven states: Arizona, Arkansas, Colorado, Idaho, Montana and Utah have lake fishing guides, and Alaska is covered by our British Columbia and Alaska fly fishing book. Colorado also has its own fly fishing book. The rest of the fifty states and the District of Columbia are listed on this page and marked "Being written".

Why do some states say "Being written" instead of linking somewhere?

Because the guide is not finished yet, and we would rather show you nothing than send you to a page that is not there. We add one state a day, strongest fishing states first. A state shows a link on this page only once its guide is actually live.

Do the fishing guides tell me where to stay?

Yes, and that is the point of them. Every lake on every guide is tied to the nearest town or two that has beds, with hotel and vacation-home links pinned to that exact town. Where a lake has no town near it that we could confirm, we say so plainly and give no booking link rather than guess.

Where do the fishing facts in these guides come from?

From the body that sets the rule: the state wildlife agency, the Park Service, the Forest Service, the water authority. Every licence price, bag limit, season and closure is read from the agency and carries the date it was read. Rules change and differ water by water, so always check the agency before you fish.
