Agadir’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Agadir — Best Beachfront & Resort Hotels

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Agadir is Morocco’s sunniest and most popular beach resort — a long crescent of soft sand backed by a palm-lined promenade, with reliable sunshine almost all year and a tempered Atlantic breeze that keeps it far cooler than inland Marrakech. Most visitors come for the sun, the sea and an easy all-inclusive or beachfront stay, whether that means a family resort, an adults-only retreat or a smart hotel by the marina. Agadir Hotels FAQ

The questions travellers ask most when choosing where to stay in Agadir.

What is the best area to stay in Agadir?

The seafront strip along Agadir Bay is the heart of it — a long crescent of sand backed by a beachfront promenade, with most of the resorts, all-inclusives and restaurants within walking distance. For a livelier, more upscale scene head to the marina end; for surf and a younger crowd, the beaches at Taghazout, a short drive north, are the alternative base.

Is Agadir good for an all-inclusive beach holiday?

Very much so. Agadir is Morocco’s main purpose-built beach resort, with a long, safe, gently shelving bay, reliable sunshine most of the year, and a big choice of all-inclusive and beachfront hotels at prices below much of the Mediterranean. It is a relaxed sun-and-sand base, with day trips to Taghazout, the souk and the Atlas within easy reach when you want a change.

Is the weather in Agadir good year-round?

Largely yes. Agadir enjoys warm, dry, sunny weather almost all year and is Europe’s reliable source of winter sun, with pleasant days even in December and January. Summer is warm rather than scorching thanks to the Atlantic breeze, which keeps it far cooler than inland Marrakech. Evenings can turn cool and breezy, especially in winter, so pack a light layer.

How far is Agadir from the airport?

Agadir Al Massira airport (AGA) is about 25 km southeast of the resort, roughly 30 to 40 minutes by taxi or transfer. Many all-inclusive packages include a transfer; otherwise taxis are readily available. Taghazout is a little farther, around 40 to 50 minutes from the airport, north along the coast.
