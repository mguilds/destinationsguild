Agadir’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Agadir — Best Beachfront & Resort Hotels

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Agadir is Morocco’s beach capital — a six-mile crescent of golden sand, reliable Atlantic sunshine and a wall of resorts rebuilt in modern style after the 1960 earthquake. Most visitors come for the beachfront and all-inclusive resorts, the spas and the surf up the coast at Taghazout. Below we sort the town’s best-run stays by category — from luxury and boutique to family, adults-only and pet-friendly, plus the beach essentials — each with an honest note and a Book Me link that opens that exact property on Trip.com.

Luxury

Agadir’s top end runs from a five-star flagship on the town beach to the sleek new resorts of Taghazout Bay just up the coast — big spas, ocean-view rooms and polished service. Book here when you want beach comfort with a designer edge.

Sofitel Agadir Royal Bay Resort

$$$$ LuxuryBeachfrontBig poolsSpa

A handsome five-star set right on Agadir’s beach, with lush gardens, a vast lagoon pool, a spa and several restaurants, all in easy reach of the promenade. Spacious, elegant rooms and slick Sofitel service make it the town’s benchmark luxury address. The pick when you want a full-service beachfront resort with genuine polish and space to spread out.

Boutique

Agadir is resort country rather than riad country, but a few smaller design-led hotels bring intimacy and style to the beach town — personal service, a pretty pool and a calmer mood than the big all-inclusives.

A stylish small hotel near the marina and beach, blending contemporary Moroccan design with a rooftop pool, a spa and a hammam, and warm, personal service that the big resorts can’t match. Its intimate scale and central-yet-calm setting make it a refreshing boutique choice. Ideal for couples and independent travellers who want character and comfort close to the sea.

Budget & Value

Agadir is one of Morocco’s best-value beach breaks, with large, comfortable hotels a short walk from the sand at prices well below Europe’s resorts. You trade five-star gloss for space, a pool and a great location near the promenade.

Anezi Tower Hotel

$$ Mid-rangeNear the beachPoolsSea-view rooms

A large, long-running hotel a short walk from the beach and promenade, with roomy sea-view rooms, outdoor and indoor pools and a dependable, good-value operation. Its scale, pools and handy location make it an easy base for a sunshine break without a resort price tag. Comfortable and practical rather than stylish, and popular with returning value-seekers.
