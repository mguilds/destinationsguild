Lake Fishing in Northern California — Ten Lakes, from Shasta to the High Sierra

Houseboats on Shasta, a trout found nowhere else at Eagle Lake, the bass of Clear Lake, mackinaw in deep Tahoe and the Eastern Sierra opener: the lakes, the rules on bringing a boat, and where to sleep.

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Checked against the agencies that run each lake and the California Department of Fish and Wildlife, 4 November 2026

The north of California holds the state’s biggest lakes and the widest range of them: reservoirs so long they take a day to cross, a high-desert lake with its own kind of trout, the old natural basin of Clear Lake, the blue depth of Tahoe, and the small trout lakes strung along the eastern side of the Sierra.

This is the lake volume of our California fishing guide, written for families with a spinning rod, bass anglers with a boat, trollers, and people who just want a lake, a cabin and a fish for supper. Everything below that is a rule or a price was read from the agency that sets it on 4 November 2026. Where we could not confirm something, we say so.

Golden mussels changed the rules

Golden mussels were first found in North America in California in October 2024, and in June 2026 CDFW confirmed them at the Port of West Sacramento. Each agency has set its own response, so these rules are a patchwork.

LakeWhat a visiting boat faces
Shasta, TrinityA signed Invasive Mussel-Free Certification; clean, drain and dry for 30 days after an out-of-town trip, 5 days between local lakes.
OrovilleInspections, decontamination and seal checks were dropped on 29 April 2026. The easiest big lake to launch on.
Clear LakeLake County sticker and screening for trailered boats; breaking the rule carries a minimum $1,000 fine.

Crowley, June Lake LoopFrom April 2026, all boats in Inyo and Mono counties must be inspected. Kayaks and float tubes are generally excluded.

Confirmed with CDFW, 4 November 2026 — CDFW’s advice is to contact the managing agency of the water you plan to visit for launching requirements and closures. California-registered boats need the state Mussel Fee Sticker on fresh water. DWR on Oroville, 29 April 2026: inspections, decontamination and seal checking “are no longer required.”

Shasta, Trinity and Oroville — the great reservoirs

Big water with everything, and with levels that fall by autumn.

Shasta Lake, north of Redding

What swims there. Rainbow and brown trout, landlocked Chinook salmon, largemouth, smallmouth and spotted bass, channel and white catfish, crappie, bluegill, white sturgeon and brown bullhead, says the Forest Service, and open all year.

How people fish it. By boat. This is houseboat country: Holiday Harbor hires fishing boats and has a store; Bridge Bay and Shasta Marina at Packers Bay are the other big names.

Confirmed with the Forest Service and the state, 4 November 2026 — “Invasive Mussel-Free Certifications are required for launching boats on Shasta Lake.” Some ramps close as the lake drops; storage was 50% of capacity and 93% of average on 3 November, so telephone the ranger station on (530) 275-1587 for the ramp you want. OEHHA advice for women 18–49 and children: channel catfish, DO NOT EAT; black bass and Chinook 1 serving a week.

Nearest towns: Redding to the south, Lakehead to the north.

Trinity Lake, in the Trinity Alps

What swims there. Largemouth and smallmouth bass, rainbow and brown trout, kokanee and landlocked Chinook, white catfish, bullhead and sunfish. The Forest Service says the state record smallmouth bass came from here. It is the quieter neighbour of Shasta.

How people fish it. By boat, for bass in the coves and trout at the mouths of the big tributaries in spring and summer. The Pine Cove ramp has wheelchair fishing access with decks. Trinity Lake Resort & Marina hires boats and houseboats.

Confirmed with the Forest Service and the state, 4 November 2026 — Open all year. The same mussel-free self-certification applies. Storage 67% of capacity, 115% of average on 3 November, among the best of the big northern lakes. The Forest Service mentions special bass rules for Trinity; check the current CDFW booklet.

Nearest town: Weaverville. The Trinity River below is in our rivers guide.

Lake Oroville, Butte County

What swims there. Largemouth and smallmouth bass, Chinook, catfish, brown trout and more, says State Parks.

How people fish it. Fishing is permitted all year. Bidwell Canyon and Lime Saddle marinas rent boats, and Lime Saddle sells tackle and bait.

Confirmed with DWR, State Parks and the state, 4 November 2026 — Launching is available 24 hours a day with no inspection since April 2026. Vehicle and launch combination $13. Storage 43% of capacity, 81% of average on 3 November: check which ramps reach the water. OEHHA advice for women 18–49 and children: black bass and catfish, DO NOT EAT. State Parks mentions a bass slot limit; go by the current CDFW booklet.

Nearest town: Oroville.

Lake Almanor and Eagle Lake — the high north-east

Cool high-country water, a long way from the crowds.

Lake Almanor, Plumas County

What swims there. A variety of trout, bass, salmon and catfish, says the Lassen National Forest.

How people fish it. Wading, shore, motor boat, float tube, fly, spin or bait, the Forest lists them all. Plumas Pines Resort has a full-service marina with rentals; Knotty Pine Resort is another base.

Confirmed with the Forest Service, 4 November 2026 — Almanor Boat Launch, generally open mid-April through October, depending on conditions; no fishing from the dock. OEHHA advice for women 18–49 and children: rainbow trout 2 servings a week, black bass 1, Sacramento sucker DO NOT EAT.

Nearest town: Chester.

Eagle Lake, Lassen County — a trout found nowhere else

What swims there. The Eagle Lake rainbow trout, which lives naturally in this lake and its main tributary, Pine Creek, and nowhere else. The water is unusually alkaline, and the fish grow well in it: about 18 inches and two pounds, says CDFW, and they have been known to reach 28 inches and ten pounds.

How people fish it. Trolling in summer with heavy lures or downriggers; float tubes and pontoon boats in autumn. CDFW warns small craft to stay close to shore, or the wind may blow them out into the lake. The Forest Service says Gallatin Marina offers boat and fishing equipment rentals, licences and supplies.

Confirmed with CDFW and the Forest Service, 4 November 2026 — State regulations: Saturday preceding Memorial Day through the last day in February; 2 trout a day, 4 in possession. The lake inside the Gallatin Marina breakwater, Pine Creek Slough, and Pine Creek below Highway 44 are closed to all fishing all year. Low water has limited launching in recent years: the 2026 ramp position is not confirmed, so telephone the marina.

Nearest town: Susanville.

Clear Lake — the bass lake

An old natural lake, in the wine country north of Napa.

Clear Lake, Lake County

What swims there. Largemouth bass, crappie, bluegill and channel catfish, says State Parks. Bass are the reason people come.

How people fish it. By boat along the tule beds and docks, and from Clear Lake State Park on the west shore. Disney’s Boat Rentals in Lakeport hires pontoon and fishing boats. Spring is the time; late summer can bring algae, so check Lake County’s notices.

Confirmed with Lake County and State Parks, 4 November 2026 — “The Lake County Quagga/Zebra (QZ) Sticker is required, in addition to a State DMV sticker, for all vessels launched in Lake County water bodies.” Trailered boats must be screened, and screened again after any trip outside the county; see nomussels.com. OEHHA advice for women 18–49 and children: black bass, DO NOT EAT.

Nearest towns: Lakeport, Kelseyville and Clearlake.

Lake Tahoe — deep water and one licence for two states

Most visitors never fish it. The fish are there, and very deep.

Lake Tahoe, California side

What swims there. Mackinaw (lake trout), rainbow and brown trout and kokanee, says Mile High Fishing Charters.

How people fish it. With a charter, unless you know the lake. Mile High Fishing Charters runs all year from Tahoe Keys Marina. Tahoe City Marina’s rental boats had closed for the season when we read its site.

Confirmed with CDFW and the Tahoe inspection programme, 4 November 2026 — 2026 regulations: anyone 16 or older “fishing anywhere in Lake Tahoe or Topaz Lake must possess either a California sport fishing license or a Nevada sport fishing license (Nevada also requires a trout stamp).” Fishing is prohibited from two hours after sunset to one hour before sunrise. The main lake has no special row in the booklet, which we read as open all year at 5 trout. Its tributaries, and within 300 feet of their mouths, are open only from the Saturday before Memorial Day to 30 September, with artificial lures, barbless hooks and 0 trout. Boats: all motorised vessels need inspection and decontamination, including hand-launched electric craft, and in winter inspections are by appointment on selected Wednesdays at Meyers. See tahoeboatinspections.com.

Nearest towns: South Lake Tahoe and Tahoe City.

Crowley Lake and the June Lake Loop — the Eastern Sierra

Summer water with a hard close. The season here ends on 15 November.

Crowley Lake, near Mammoth

What swims there. Trout, including cutthroat and rainbow, and Sacramento perch. Opening weekend here is a tradition across the Eastern Sierra.

How people fish it. Mostly by rented boat from Crowley Lake Fish Camp: a 15-horsepower aluminium boat is $100 a full day, $80 a half. It says it is closed for the season, with 2027 reservations opening on 2 January at 8 a.m. Book early for the opener.

Confirmed with CDFW, LADWP and the Eastern Sierra inspection programme, 4 November 2026 — State regulations: last Saturday in April through 31 July, 5 trout. 1 August through 15 November, only artificial lures, 18-inch minimum, 2 trout. Closed within 1,800 feet of the outlet dam. Boats must be inspected; trailered boats enter at the South Landing. OEHHA advice for women 18–49 and children: 1 serving a week of cutthroat, rainbow or perch.

Nearest town: Mammoth Lakes.

June, Gull, Silver and Grant lakes

What swims there. Trout. Mono County spends $110,000 a year stocking local waters with planters and trophy fish.

How people fish it. Four small resort lakes on State Route 158, good for families: from shore or from a small rented boat. Gull Lake Marina rents boats and has a tackle shop; Silver Lake Resort and Ernie’s Tackle are the other names to know.

Confirmed with CDFW and the Forest Service, 4 November 2026 — State regulations for all four lakes: last Saturday in April through 15 November, 5 trout. Visiting boats are inspected at Bridgeport before driving south. The Inyo National Forest has boat launches at Silver and Gull.

Nearest town: June Lake.

New Melones — bass and deep kokanee

Gold Country, in the Sierra foothills.

New Melones Lake, Calaveras and Tuolumne counties

What swims there. Largemouth and smallmouth bass, black crappie, bluegill, channel catfish, kokanee and rainbow trout, says the Bureau of Reclamation.

How people fish it. Reclamation’s tip is to troll for kokanee at 80 to 100 feet and trout at 40 to 50. New Melones Lake Marina hires fishing boats and houseboats, and decontaminates private boats for same-day access at a fee.

Confirmed with the Bureau of Reclamation and the state, 4 November 2026 — Launch: Glory Hole Point 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. every day; Angels Creek and Tuttletown closed. Boats must be left out of the water for 30 days with a documented wire and red seal, and that seal works for New Melones only. Storage 63% of capacity, 117% of average on 3 November. OEHHA advice for women 18–49 and children: black bass, DO NOT EAT.

Nearest towns: Angels Camp and Sonora.

Also worth a look

Lake Davis in Plumas County: 79% full on 3 November, and the Forest Service calls it one of the premier fishing lakes in Northern California. Lake Berryessa near Napa is 83% full, with a 30-day seal from Reclamation. We have not researched these to the same standard yet.

The licence and the limits, plainly

One California licence covers every lake here. Full detail is in our California guide.

Statewide limits for lakes — unless a lake says otherwise

Trout5 (10 in possession); lakes are open all year unless listed
Black bass5, at a 12-inch minimum
Striped bass2, at an 18-inch minimum
Sunfish and crappie25 combined
Kokanee5 (10 at Trinity Lake)

Confirmed with CDFW, 4 November 2026 — 2026 Freshwater regulations (updated 10 July 2026): “All inland lakes, reservoirs, and ponds, including those on private lands, are open to fishing all year with a five-trout daily bag limit and 10 trout possession limit”, except waters listed in the special regulations, which include Eagle, Crowley and the June Lake Loop above. On those listed waters, possession is one day’s limit unless the listing says otherwise. A second rod needs a $20.26 validation.

Children and stocked city ponds

Under 16 needs no licence. CDFW’s Fishing in the City programme stocks Bay Area and Sacramento waters, and its fish planting schedule shows what went in and where.

Questions

Do I need a separate licence for the Nevada side of Lake Tahoe?

No. California’s regulations say anyone 16 or older fishing anywhere in Lake Tahoe must have either a California or a Nevada sport fishing licence, and that Nevada also requires a trout stamp.

When is Eagle Lake open?

From the Saturday before Memorial Day through the last day of February, with a limit of 2 trout a day and 4 in possession. Inside the Gallatin Marina breakwater and Pine Creek Slough it is closed all year.

When is the Crowley Lake opener?

The last Saturday in April. In 2026 that was 25 April. From 1 August to 15 November the rules tighten to artificial lures only, 2 trout, 18-inch minimum. Crowley Lake Fish Camp has already closed for 2026, and says 2027 reservations open on 2 January at 8 a.m.

Can I bring my boat to Shasta Lake?

Yes, with a signed Invasive Mussel-Free Certification. The Forest Service asks boaters to clean, drain and dry for 30 days after an out-of-town trip, and 5 days when moving between local lakes. Tahoe, New Melones, Clear Lake and the Eastern Sierra each have rules of their own.

Is the fish safe to eat from Clear Lake or Oroville?

The state’s OEHHA says women aged 18 to 49 and children should not eat black bass from Clear Lake, Oroville or New Melones, because of mercury. Others can eat limited amounts. Check the advice for each lake before you keep a fish.

Which Northern California lake is best for a visitor without a boat?

Rent one. Marinas at Shasta, Oroville, Almanor, Clear Lake and New Melones hire fishing boats. A charter is the simple answer for Lake Tahoe, where Mile High Fishing Charters runs year-round from Tahoe Keys Marina. In summer June Lake Loop has small boats and easy shore fishing.

Five honest gaps

Special bass rules. The Forest Service mentions them for Trinity and State Parks mentions a slot limit for Oroville. We could not match either to the current regulation, so we have not printed one. Follow the CDFW booklet.

Ramp status. Ramps open and close with the water, especially at Shasta, Oroville and Eagle. Telephone first.

Almanor and mussels. Our only word is from a local guide, not an agency.

Fees. We did not confirm Tahoe’s inspection fees, the Lake County sticker price or New Melones’ use fees.
