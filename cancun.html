Cancún Travel Guide

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated July 2026

White sand, turquoise Caribbean water, world-class resorts, and ancient Maya wonders a short drive away. Here's everything you need to plan the trip — and book it.

Cancún is the number-one international beach destination for American travelers — and it's easy to see why. A short nonstop flight from dozens of U.S. cities lands you on the Yucatán Peninsula, where the Hotel Zone stretches for 14 miles of powder-soft sand between the Caribbean Sea and a calm lagoon. Beyond the beach, Cancún is your launch pad to the wider Riviera Maya: snorkel the world's second-largest coral reef, stand before the pyramids of Chichén Itzá, swim in a jungle cenote, or hop a quick ferry to laid-back island life. Whether you're planning an all-inclusive family escape, a romantic getaway, or a diving adventure, this guide covers where to stay, what to do, and when to go.

Why visit Cancún

Four reasons Cancún tops the list year after year.

Easy to reach

Nonstop flights from across the U.S. land at Cancún International (CUN), just 20 minutes from the Hotel Zone. No connections, no long-haul jet lag.

Beaches for everyone

Calm, shallow water on the north end is ideal for families; the eastern stretch brings the surf and the postcard views. All of it is that famous Caribbean turquoise.

All-inclusive value

Cancún's resorts are among the best-priced all-inclusives anywhere — meals, drinks, and activities bundled into one easy rate, perfect for couples, families, and groups.

Adventure next door

Maya ruins, cenotes, eco-parks, and reef diving are all day-trip close. Cancún is a beach vacation and a bucket-list adventure in one.

Top things to do in Cancún

From ancient wonders to underwater worlds — the experiences worth building your trip around.

● Chichén Itzá

One of the New Seven Wonders of the World. Stand before the towering El Castillo pyramid on a day trip into the jungle interior.

● Snorkel the reef

The Mesoamerican Reef — the second-largest on Earth — teems with rays, turtles, and tropical fish just offshore.

● Swim a cenote

Dip into the crystal-clear freshwater sinkholes the Maya considered sacred, tucked into the surrounding jungle.

● Isla Mujeres day trip

A short ferry reaches this slow-paced island of golden beaches, golf-cart lanes, and the calmest swimming water around.

● Xcaret & Xel-Há

Nature-and-culture eco-parks with underground rivers, wildlife, and evening shows — a family favorite.

● Hotel Zone nightlife

From beach clubs to rooftop lounges, Cancún's after-dark scene runs from mellow to legendary.

Where to stay in Cancún

Cancún splits into two worlds. The Hotel Zone (Zona Hotelera) is the beachfront strip — this is where you'll find the big-name all-inclusive resorts, calm swimming beaches on the north end, and easy access to nightlife. Downtown Cancún (El Centro) sits inland and trades ocean views for authentic local restaurants, lower prices, and a more Mexican feel. Families and first-timers usually pick the Hotel Zone; budget travelers and repeat visitors often prefer Downtown.

Best time to visit & getting there

When to go

December to April is peak season — dry, sunny, and calm, with the best beach weather. May and November are the sweet spot for value, with warm water and thinner crowds. Hurricane season peaks September to October.

Getting there

Fly into Cancún International Airport (CUN), roughly 20 minutes from the Hotel Zone. Nonstop service runs from most major U.S. hubs, making a long-weekend trip completely doable.

Cancún travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Cancún?

December through April brings the driest, sunniest weather and calm seas, which is why it is peak season. For fewer crowds and lower prices, May and November are excellent shoulder-season months.

How many days do you need in Cancún?

Five to seven days lets you enjoy the beaches, take a day trip to Chichén Itzá or Tulum, and still have time to relax. A long weekend of three or four days works well if Cancún is your main focus.

Is Cancún good for families?

Yes. Calm, shallow water on much of the Hotel Zone, family-friendly all-inclusive resorts, and eco-parks like Xcaret and Xel-Há make Cancún one of the most popular family beach destinations for American travelers.

Which airport do you fly into for Cancún?

Cancún International Airport (CUN) is the gateway, with nonstop flights from dozens of U.S. cities. It is about a 20-minute drive from the Hotel Zone.
