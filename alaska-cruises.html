Alaska Cruises

Alaska is the bucket‑list cruise — glaciers calving into the sea, whales breaching off the bow, and frontier towns you reach best by ship. Most sailings run the sheltered Inside Passage round‑trip from Seattle or Vancouver; one‑way Gulf of Alaska voyages add Hubbard Glacier and connect to Denali land tours.

Best time to cruise

The season is short: May through September. June to August bring the warmest days and longest light; May and September are quieter and cheaper, with better odds of clear glacier views and fall color late in the run.
