Meet the Guild

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

The books in the Guild Travel Library are written by our Guild Explorers — the research team who go looking for the current facts, prices and seasons of a place and then write it all down in plain English. That is why every guide is signed the same way at the top of the page: Curated by Our Guild Explorers.

But a book still has to be handed to you by somebody. Four faces do that work across this site, and if you have been reading a while you have already met all of them — a parrot at the Library door, a second parrot offering to answer your questions, and a pair in ranger hats waiting at the top of every American state guide. This page is simply the introduction we owe you.

Polo — Chief Navigator

The one who keeps the shelves, and the one to see if you want to write a guide of your own.

Chief Navigator
PoloChief Navigator · keeper of the Library

You meet Polo in the Guild Travel Library. The Library is tended by two librarians — Polo the Chief Navigator and Polly the Travel Concierge — and it is Polo’s name on the shelves: which books are open, which are still being written, and where a country sits on the case. He keeps the Seven Wonders shelf too, which is his sort of errand: a short list, properly ordered, no padding.

The navigator’s title is not decoration. The Guild’s masthead spells TRAVEL in the international maritime signal code, the site menu calls itself the Chart Room, and Our Story tips its hat to the navigators who charted the world long before any of us. Polo is the part of the Guild that still thinks in charts — where a thing is, how you get there, and what order to do it in.

He is also the one to see about writing. Open the Polo & Polly button in the corner of the Library and the first thing on his side of the menu is write your city — the door to becoming a Guild Reporter. If you would rather send a note than fill in a form, the Ask Polo & Polly message on the Get Involved page reaches the Guild by email, and a person reads it.

One honest note: Polo does not chat live yet. The Library says so plainly — live answers from Polo are still being wired up. For now he keeps the shelves and the Reporter’s door, and questions go to Polly.

Polly — Travel Concierge

The scarlet macaw who answers travel questions, free, without an account.

Travel Concierge
PollyTravel Concierge · ask her anything

Polly is the Guild’s concierge, and she is the reason a parrot keeps offering to help you. Ask Polly is a chat window. On the home page you will find a small parrot button in the bottom corner of the screen marked Ask Polly, and further down the page a wider bar with her name and the words Ask me anything. Tap either one and the panel opens.

Inside there are four quick buttons — Hotels, Flights, Things to Do, Airport Pickup — and a box where you can simply type the question in your own words. Where should I go in October. Is this a good month for Paris. Find me somewhere quiet near the port. She handles hotels, beaches, cruise ports, itineraries and the ordinary logistics of getting somewhere, anywhere on earth. There is no sign-up, no email required, and no charge; if a booking follows, it happens on Trip.com, and the Guild earns a small commission that keeps the Library free.

She also flies. Every cruise-port guide the Guild publishes carries her in the banner at the top — a parrot in the corner of the header on the Aruba and Cozumel port guides, bobbing gently while you read. In the Library she is the one who pulls a book off the shelf when you tap a spine, and the picks marked Polly’s Pick on the home page are hers.

If you would rather write than chat, the same question can be sent from the Get Involved page. We read every one and reply.

“Hi, I’m Polly! Your free travel guide — tap me to plan a trip, find a hotel, or ask me anything.”

Bodhi & Nolie — The Guild’s American Road Guides

Two ranger-hatted guides who ride shotgun through every state.

Open any American state guide and, before the hotels and the itineraries, a green-and-gold strip says hello. That is Bodhi and Nolie. They greet you at the top of all fifty states, Washington D.C. and the U.S. territories — so each book reads like a road trip with friends rather than a wall of listings.

Bodhi

The Head · the story & the map

He has driven every back road twice and knows why the town got its name. Bodhi brings the why — the history, the route, and the one fact that makes a place click. He is the reason Texas opens by telling you it was its own country for nearly a decade, and Hawaii by reminding you it had a queen and a palace.

“Now here’s the good part…”

Nolie

The Heart · the feel & the secret

Where Bodhi has the map, Nolie has the feel — which overlook at golden hour, where the locals actually eat, and which “tourist trap” is secretly worth it. Hers is the line about Texas brisket giving you opinions for life, and the warning that Alaskan weather does not read the calendar.

“Trust me on this one.”

You will know them the same way every time — the evergreen flat-brim ranger hat, the gold badge, and the hand-stitched “Hi, I’m ___” nametag. An affectionate tip of the hat to the great park guides, and never the official article.

Frequently Asked Questions

What readers ask us most about the four of them.

Who writes the Guild’s books?

Every guide in the Guild Travel Library is the work of the Guild Explorers, our research team. They gather the current facts, prices and seasons for a place, check them against public sources, and write the guide so you do not have to do the digging. That is why every book carries the same signature at the top: Curated by Our Guild Explorers. Polo, Polly, Bodhi and Nolie are the faces those books are handed to you by; the Explorers are the people behind them.

What is Ask Polly, and does it cost anything?

Ask Polly is the free chat concierge on the Destinations Guild home page. Look for the small green parrot button in the bottom corner of the screen marked Ask Polly, or the wide Polly, Travel Concierge bar partway down the page. Tap either one and a chat panel opens with quick buttons for Hotels, Flights, Things to Do and Airport Pickup, plus a box where you can type a question in your own words. There is nothing to sign up for, no email required, and no charge. If you would rather write to us instead, the Ask Polo and Polly form on the Get Involved page reaches the Guild by email and we answer every message.

Are Polo, Polly, Bodhi and Nolie real people?

They are the Guild’s hosts, not our staff list. The research and the writing are done by the Guild Explorers, and the four characters are how that work is handed to you: Polo at the Library door, Polly at the concierge desk, Bodhi and Nolie in the passenger seat on an American road trip. We would rather say so plainly than let anyone wonder. The facts in the guides are checked either way, and we always tell you to confirm prices and hours before you travel.

Where do I meet Bodhi and Nolie?

At the top of every American state guide. Each of the fifty states, plus Washington D.C. and the U.S. territories, opens with a short green-and-gold greeting strip where the two of them say hello and tell you one true thing about the state before you read another word. The quickest way to find them is the America bookcase on the state-by-state page, where every state sits on the shelf as its own book.
