Lake Fishing in Florida — Six Great Bass Lakes and the Towns Beside Them

Lake Okeechobee, Lake Tohopekaliga, Lake Kissimmee, Rodman Reservoir, Lake George and Lake Istokpoga: what swims in each, how people fish them, the best months, the 2026 rules and where to sleep.

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Checked against the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission, 9 November 2026

Florida’s big lakes are shallow, warm and full of weed, and they grow big largemouth bass. The fish here is the Florida bass, and the state’s own fisheries agency, the FWC, says it is working to make Florida “the undisputed Bass Fishing Capital of the World”. Bass are not the whole story, though. In the cool months the same lakes fill with anglers drifting minnows for crappie — “specks” to Floridians — and in late spring and summer the bream (bluegill and shellcracker) bite in the marshes.

This guide belongs to The Guild Fishing Library. It is written for families with a spinning rod, boat anglers, and anyone who wants a lake, a fish camp and a good breakfast before dawn. Florida’s fishing season does not close for winter: these lakes are at their best from late autumn to spring, just when the north is frozen. If salt water is more your style, our flats fishing book covers the Florida Keys.

Where these facts come from. Every rule, limit and price on this page was read on 9 November 2026 from the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission (FWC): its freshwater fishing regulations pages (General Statewide Bag and Length Limits, Special Bag and Length Limits and Fish Management Areas), its licence, exemption and licence-free-days pages, and its fishing forecast pages for each of these six lakes. The FWC says there are no new freshwater rule changes for 2026–2027. Not from a fish camp, a guide or a booking site. Rules change — always check the agency before you fish.

The best months, in plain words

The seasons described here come from the FWC’s own fishing forecasts; the plain advice is ours.

WhenWhat happensWhy
October to DecemberBig bass move up before spawning; crappie start to biteThe FWC’s Okeechobee forecast says autumn brings “good sized pre-spawning Florida bass” and that crappie fishing should pick up in November and December. Cooler water also wakes up crappie on Lake Toho.
January to MarchSpeck season and the bass spawnThe FWC says January typically gives the best catch of keepable crappie on Lake Okeechobee, and that bass peak spawning in late winter and early spring. Istokpoga’s best speck fishing is in the winter months. Rodman fishes well all winter.
April to JuneBream on the beds; the two free fishing weekendsOur guidance: bluegill and shellcracker fishing in the shallows is easy and fun for children. Florida’s licence-free freshwater weekends fall in April and June.
July to SeptemberHot, early and late fishing onlyOur guidance: fish at first light and in the evening, carry water and watch for afternoon thunderstorms. The FWC reports good summer bream fishing in Okeechobee’s marshes.

Where a row says “our guidance”, that is our own plain advice, not an agency rule. Florida has no closed season for bass or crappie on these lakes.

Lake Okeechobee — the “big water”

Florida’s largest lake, wrapped by a levee and touched by five counties: Palm Beach, Martin, Glades, Okeechobee and Hendry.

Lake Okeechobee, from Okeechobee, Clewiston and Moore Haven

What swims there. The FWC says the lake is nationally known for its largemouth bass and black crappie, with bluegill and redear sunfish (shellcracker) in the marshes and plenty of Mayan cichlids in the canals. It covers some 730 square miles but averages only about nine feet deep, which is why its edges are a maze of bulrush, eelgrass and hydrilla.

How people fish it. Mostly from boats along the outer edge of the reeds and grass — live golden shiners for big bass, topwater baits as the bass gather to spawn, jigs and minnows for crappie, worms and crickets for bream. The FWC notes good bank fishing for Mayan cichlids in the Indian Prairie and Harney Pond canals.

Keeping a fish. Bass follow the statewide rule: five a day, only one 16 inches or longer. Crappie under 10 inches must be released; the bag is 25. Mayan cichlids are non-native and have no bag or size limit.

Confirmed with the FWC, 9 November 2026 — Lake Okeechobee forecast page: “Black crappie (Speck) under 10 inches must be released. The bag limit is 25.” Special Bag and Length Limits page: “Lake Okeechobee: Crappie (speckled perch) less than 10 inches in total length must be released immediately.”

Low water — take care. The FWC warns of shallow-water and rock hazards and has published a hazard map with the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers. It recorded the lake at 11.62 feet on 21 September 2026 and suggests that newcomers go out with a guide while the water is low. If you catch a bass with a yellow tag, the FWC asks you to cut off the tag and call the number on it to claim a $100 reward.

Nearest towns: Okeechobee on the north shore, Clewiston on the south shore, and Moore Haven on the west side where the Caloosahatchee leaves the lake.

Lake Tohopekaliga — “Toho”, beside Kissimmee

An 18,810-acre lake on the edge of the town of Kissimmee, at the top of the Kissimmee Chain of Lakes.

Lake Toho and East Lake Toho, from Kissimmee and St. Cloud

What swims there. Florida bass first of all — on the day we checked, the FWC’s TrophyCatch tracker showed 519 bass of 8 to 9.9 pounds and 89 of 10 to 12.9 pounds documented from Lake Toho — plus bluegill, redear sunfish and black crappie.

How people fish it. From boats, around bulrush, Illinois pondweed and the edges of hydrilla. The FWC has sunk fish attractors here and publishes a map of them. After rain it suggests the moving water at the mouth of Shingle Creek, the St. Cloud canal and Partins Ditch. For crappie in the cool months, it suggests drifting live minnows near Shingle Creek, Little Grassy Island and Goblets Cove.

Keeping a fish. Statewide rules: five bass (only one 16 inches or longer), 25 crappie, 50 panfish. No bag limit on channel catfish in Lake Toho or East Lake Toho, which are FWC Fish Management Areas.

Confirmed with the FWC, 9 November 2026 — Fish Management Areas page, Northeast region: Lake Tohopekaliga (18,810 acres) and East Lake Tohopekaliga (11,968 acres) “Open to fishing”, “No bag limit for channel catfish.”

Boat ramps. The City of Kissimmee’s Big Toho Marina at 69 Lakeview Drive is, in the city’s words, Lake Toho’s largest boat ramp and marina, with boat slips, a fishing pier and a bait shop. On East Lake Toho, the City of St. Cloud’s Lakefront Park has a boat ramp, a marina and a fishing pier.

Nearest towns: Kissimmee for Lake Toho; St. Cloud for East Lake Toho. Orlando is close by for a bigger choice of hotels.

Lake Kissimmee — the big lake at the foot of the chain

A 34,948-acre lake about 40 miles south of Orlando and 18 miles east of Lake Wales, joined to Lakes Cypress and Hatchineha above it.

Lake Kissimmee, from Lake Wales

What swims there. Florida bass, bluegill, redear sunfish and black crappie. The FWC’s tracker counted 835 bass of 8 to 9.9 pounds, 174 of 10 to 12.9 pounds and two of more than 13 pounds documented from the Kissimmee Chain when we checked.

How people fish it. From boats in the open pockets of hydrilla and along the edges of bulrush, cattail and pondweed. The FWC names Osceola Slough, Philadelphia Point and the Pig Trail for bass, the water between Shady Oaks Fish Camp and the Rocks near State Road 60 at the south end, and Bird, Brahma, Rabbit and Grassy islands for bluegill.

Keeping a fish. Statewide rules, with no bag limit for channel catfish (the Kissimmee Chain is a Fish Management Area).

Confirmed with the FWC, 9 November 2026 — Fish Management Areas page: “Lakes Tohopekaliga (West Lake Tohopekaliga), Cypress, Hatchineha, and Kissimmee, Osceola and Polk counties … Open to fishing. No bag limit for channel catfish.”

Boat ramps. Lake Kissimmee State Park, on Camp Mack Road, has a boat ramp and a canoe and kayak launch. Note: Florida State Parks says the park’s campgrounds have been closed for renovation since 17 February 2026. Camp Mack, a lodge, marina and RV resort a little farther down the same road, has a boat ramp of its own.

Nearest town: Lake Wales, which the FWC puts 18 miles west of the lake.

Rodman Reservoir — flooded timber and trophy bass

A 9,500-acre reservoir about 15 miles long, south of Palatka off U.S. Highway 19, made in 1968 by damming the Ocklawaha River.

Rodman Reservoir (Lake Ocklawaha), from Palatka

What swims there. The FWC calls Rodman “one of Florida’s premier trophy bass fisheries” — its tracker showed 1,176 bass of 8 to 9.9 pounds, 261 of 10 to 12.9 pounds and 10 of 13 pounds or more when we checked — with good numbers of black crappie, bluegill, redear sunfish, redbreast sunfish and catfish.

How people fish it. From boats, slowly, around stumps, standing timber and the edges of the old river channel and the Cross Florida Barge Canal, which run up to 30 feet deep. In winter the FWC suggests Texas- and Carolina-rigged worms, jigs and live golden shiners for big bass, and minnows or small jigs along the channel for crappie. The river stretch from Orange Springs up to Eureka is good for panfish.

Take care. The FWC warns that submerged stumps, standing timber and floating logs are everywhere. Stay in marked channels and go slowly in water you do not know.

Drawdowns. The reservoir is drawn down every three to four years to control weed. The last drawdown ran from November 2025 to March 2026, when bass were catch-and-release only. Check the FWC’s Rodman page before you go for any new drawdown.

Confirmed with the FWC, 9 November 2026 — Rodman Reservoir forecast page: “Drawdowns are conducted every three to four years on the reservoir for aquatic plant control and fish and wildlife habitat enhancement.” No special bag rule is listed for Rodman, so the statewide limits apply.

Boat ramps. Rodman Recreation Area, at 410 Rodman Dam Road, Palatka, has a two-lane boat ramp beside the spillway and two fishing piers, open 8 a.m. to sunset with no fee, according to Florida State Parks. The FWC’s Florida Boat Ramp Finder lists the others, including Kenwood and Orange Springs.

Nearest towns: Palatka to the north; Ocala to the south-west for a wider choice.

Lake George — the widest bend of the St. Johns River

A 46,000-acre natural lake on the St. Johns River between Putnam and Volusia counties, on the edge of the Ocala National Forest.

Lake George, from Astor, Georgetown and DeLand

What swims there. Florida bass, sunshine bass and striped bass, black crappie, bluegill, redear sunfish and brown bullhead, according to the FWC.

How people fish it. From boats. The lake is mostly under ten feet deep, with a natural channel that carries barges. When the river is flowing, the FWC suggests live shad and grass shrimp near the jetties at the south end, where the St. Johns enters, for bass and sunshine bass; striped bass around Ninemile Point and the jetties as the water cools; and crappie in Willow Cove on the east shore later in the season. It also says weed and fish cover on the lake are sparse at present, so fish the pilings, cypress knees and fallen wood.

Keeping a fish. Statewide rules: five bass (only one 16 inches or longer); 20 striped, white and sunshine bass together, only six of them 24 inches or longer; 25 crappie; 50 panfish.

Confirmed with the FWC, 9 November 2026 — Special Bag and Length Limits page: “Silver Glen Springs in Lake and Marion counties and Salt Springs in Marion County: Fishing is prohibited in and within 50 yards of the springs.”

Boat ramps. The FWC says public access is from Blue Creek Road to Lake George Road off Highway 40. Georgetown Marina & Lodge, on the north-east corner of the lake, is a private full-service marina with cabins, bait and tackle and fishing licences.

Nearest towns: Astor, on the river just south of the lake; DeLand, a larger town to the south-east. Georgetown, on the east shore, is a small community with a marina and lodge.

Lake Istokpoga — big bass and winter specks

A 27,692-acre lake five miles north-east of Lake Placid in Highlands County, its deepest point only about ten feet.

Lake Istokpoga, from Lake Placid and Sebring

What swims there. The FWC says Istokpoga has quality crappie fishing and “one of the highest largemouth bass catch rates in the state”. Its tracker showed 494 bass of 8 to 9.9 pounds, 103 of 10 to 12.9 pounds and two of 13 pounds or more when we checked. Bluegill and shellcracker too.

How people fish it. For specks in winter, drift over open water in the north-east and south-west corners, or try Henderson’s Cove, the cuts around the islands and the edge of the lily pads. For bass, fish the bulrush lines, the islands and the creek mouths (Arbuckle and Josephine creeks) when water is flowing. When its gates are open, the Istokpoga Canal off County Road 621 offers bank fishing for bass. You can also wade-fish from the Cow House Road boat ramp.

Keeping a fish. Statewide rules, with no bag limit for channel catfish (Istokpoga is a Fish Management Area).

Confirmed with the FWC, 9 November 2026 — Lake Istokpoga forecast page: public boat ramps “are located on the north, northeast, and southwest shorelines off of U.S. Route 98, Lake Boulevard off Cow House Road, and Highland Lake Drive off of County Route 621”; “There are also six fish camps/resorts on the lake.”

Nearest towns: Lake Placid to the south-west and Sebring to the north-west.

The licence and the limits, plainly

One Florida freshwater licence covers every lake on this page.

Who needs one, and what it costs

Anyone who fishes, from sixteen. The FWC says residents and visitors need a licence to fish, even if they release everything they catch. Children under 16 are exempt, and so are Florida residents aged 65 and older with proof of age and residency. Anyone helping in the catch, even by baiting hooks, needs a licence too.

3 days$17—
7 days$30—

Five years—$79

Buy online at GoOutdoorsFlorida.com, or from a licence agent; many marinas and bait shops sell them. A vendor may add a fee of 50 cents. On the two licence-free freshwater weekends (the first Saturday and Sunday in April and the second Saturday and Sunday in June) nobody needs a licence, but all the other rules apply.

Confirmed with the FWC, 9 November 2026 — Recreational Freshwater Licenses page: “Resident Annual: $17.00; Resident Five-Year: $79.00; Non-Resident Annual: $47.00; Non-Resident 3-Day: $17.00; Non-Resident 7-Day: $30.00”. Exemptions page: “Youth under 16 years of age”; “Resident seniors age 65 or older with proof of age or residency”.

Statewide daily limits — unless a lake says otherwise

FishDaily limit (FWC statewide rule)
Black bass (Florida, largemouth and others)5, only one of which may be 16 inches or longer; no minimum length for Florida or largemouth bass
Crappie (speckled perch)25 (on Lake Okeechobee, each at least 10 inches)

Channel catfishNo bag limit on the Fish Management Area lakes above (Toho, East Toho, Kissimmee, Istokpoga)

You may not have more than two days’ bag limit in your possession. Bass, striped bass and their relatives may not be filleted, nor their heads or tails removed, until you have finished fishing for the day. Never release a fish caught in one lake into another.

Confirmed with the FWC, 9 November 2026 — General Statewide Bag and Length Limits page: “5 Black bass … only one of which may be 16 inches or longer in total length. There is no minimum length limit for Florida bass or largemouth bass.” “50 Panfish”; “25 Crappie (speckled perch)”; “20 Striped bass, white bass, and sunshine bass … of which only 6 may be 24 inches or longer”.

TrophyCatch

The FWC rewards anglers who photograph, weigh and release bass of 8 pounds or more through its TrophyCatch programme. The rules allow you to keep such a bass alive for a short time, over the bag and length limit, just long enough to document it, and then you must release it into the same lake.

Questions

Do I need a fishing licence to fish a lake in Florida, and what does it cost?

Yes, unless you are exempt. The FWC says residents and visitors need a freshwater fishing licence to fish, even for catch and release. Children under 16 are exempt, and so are Florida residents aged 65 or older with proof of age and residency. On 9 November 2026 the FWC listed a non-resident 3-day licence at $17, a 7-day at $30 and an annual at $47; a resident annual is $17 and a resident five-year $79. A vendor may add a 50-cent fee. Buy online at GoOutdoorsFlorida.com.

How many bass can I keep in Florida?

Five black bass a day, of which only one may be 16 inches or longer. There is no minimum length for Florida or largemouth bass. That is the FWC’s statewide rule, and it applies on Lake Okeechobee, the Kissimmee Chain, Rodman Reservoir, Lake George and Lake Istokpoga, which have no special bass rule of their own. Many anglers release their big bass; a bass of 8 pounds or more can be photographed and entered in the FWC’s TrophyCatch programme before release.

Is there a size limit on crappie at Lake Okeechobee?

Yes. On Lake Okeechobee crappie (speckled perch) under 10 inches must be released immediately, and the daily bag is 25. The FWC’s special rule also covers the connecting canals it lists, such as Harney Pond Canal, Indian Prairie Canal, Taylor Creek and Fisheating Creek up to U.S. 27.

When are Florida’s free freshwater fishing days?

The FWC sets two licence-free freshwater weekends: the first consecutive Saturday and Sunday in April, and the second consecutive Saturday and Sunday in June. Residents and visitors may fish without a licence on those days, but every other rule, including bag and size limits, still applies.

Can I fish at Silver Glen Springs on Lake George?

No, not in or within 50 yards of the spring. The FWC prohibits fishing in and within 50 yards of Silver Glen Springs in Lake and Marion counties and of Salt Springs in Marion County. The rest of Lake George follows the statewide rules.

What we could not check today, and will not pretend to have

The part of a fishing page most sites leave out.

Five honest gaps

Fishing guides. We have not yet checked the independent bass guides on these lakes against their own websites. Several local bait shops and fish camps the FWC names did not have a working website of their own when we looked, so we have not listed them. They will be added at the same standard.

Lake Istokpoga fish camps. The FWC says there are six on the lake; none we tried had a website of its own that we could open on 9 November 2026. The Highlands County visitor bureau lists them.

Water levels and drawdowns. These change month to month. Lake Okeechobee’s level is from the FWC’s page dated 21 September 2026. Check the FWC page for each lake, and the South Florida Water Management District for Okeechobee, before you go.

Alligators. All of these lakes have them. We have not summarised the FWC’s alligator safety advice here; read it before your trip, keep children and pets back from the water’s edge, and never feed an alligator.

Fish consumption advice. The Florida Department of Health publishes fish consumption advisories by lake and species. We have not summarised them; read them before you keep fish to eat.
