Where to Stay in Khiva — Inside the Walls, or Just Outside

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated November 2026

Khiva is the smallest and strangest of the three Silk Road cities, and the only one where the question of where to sleep has a simple answer: inside the walls if you can. The Itchan Kala is the walled inner town, a few hundred metres across, mud-brick and almost entirely intact, and when the day visitors leave in the late afternoon it empties out and becomes something else. A handful of hotels are inside those walls — one of them in a nineteenth-century madrasa — and more sit just outside the gates. Khiva questions, answered

Is it worth staying inside the Itchan Kala?

Yes, and it is the single piece of advice worth giving about Khiva. Most visitors arrive from Urgench in the morning, walk the walled town for four or five hours and leave. If you sleep inside the walls you get the two hours that those people miss — the early morning and the hour after sunset, when the lanes are empty, the mud brick goes orange and the place stops being a museum. The rooms inside are smaller and plainer than you would get outside for the money. Take them anyway.

How long do you need in Khiva?

One night, two if you are not rushing. The Itchan Kala is only a few hundred metres across and a day covers the Kunya Ark, the Juma mosque with its forest of carved columns, the Islom Khoja minaret and the Pahlavon Mahmud mausoleum. The second night is not for seeing more; it is for being there when it is quiet.

How do you get to Khiva?

Through Urgench, about half an hour away, which has the airport and the main railway station and is where flights and most long-distance trains arrive. There is also a direct rail connection to Khiva itself. Coming from Bukhara overland means a long drive across the Kyzylkum desert — worth doing once, in daylight.

What is the weather like in Khiva?

Extreme at both ends, because this is the far north-west of the country and genuinely continental. July afternoons average about 104°F (40°C) and January ones about 37°F (3°C), with January nights averaging around 21°F (-6°C). April, May, September and October are the comfortable months, and they are also when the light on the walls is best.

Is there much to eat and do after dark inside the walls?

Less than in Bukhara, and that is part of the appeal. A handful of restaurants and rooftop terraces inside the Itchan Kala stay open in the evening, several of them worth climbing for the view over the roofs to the Kalta Minor. Beyond that, Khiva at night is a quiet walled town, which is what people who love it love it for.
