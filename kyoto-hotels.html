Kyoto Hotels — Quick Questions

What is the best area to stay in Kyoto?

For first-time visitors, the area around Kyoto Station is the most convenient base, with fast trains, luggage-friendly hotels and easy day trips. For atmosphere, Higashiyama, Gion and the Kamogawa riverside put you within walking distance of the great temples, geisha districts and lantern-lit lanes that most people come to Kyoto to see.

Is Kyoto a walkable city?

Kyoto is very walkable in its historic core — Gion, Higashiyama and the philosopher’s path reward slow wandering on foot. The city is spread out overall, so most travelers combine walking with the easy subway, buses and short taxi rides to reach outlying sights like Arashiyama and Fushimi Inari.

When is the best time to visit Kyoto?

Spring, roughly late March to mid-April, brings the famous cherry blossoms, and autumn, from mid-November into early December, brings brilliant maple color — both are beautiful but crowded, so book hotels early. Early summer and winter are quieter and cheaper, with fewer crowds at the temples, though summer can be hot and humid.
