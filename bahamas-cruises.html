Bahamas Cruises

Short, sunny and close to home — Bahamas cruises are the classic long‑weekend escape, sailing 3 and 4 nights from Florida. The real draw now is the private islands: each major line has its own slice of Bahamian paradise, included with your fare.

Best time to cruise

Great much of the year, with November to April the driest and calmest. Summer is warm and lively; watch the June–November hurricane window for the occasional itinerary change.
