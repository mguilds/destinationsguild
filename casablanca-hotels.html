Casablanca’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Casablanca — Best Areas & Hotels

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Casablanca is Morocco’s big-city gateway, and where you stay depends on your trip. Do you want the corniche at Ain Diab, with the ocean and the Hassan II Mosque nearby, the city centre for business and the train stations, or the leafy villas of the Anfa district? We’ve grouped the city’s most reliable, well-run hotels by style and budget, each with an honest note and a Book Me link that opens that exact property on Trip.com so you can just choose your dates.

Luxury

Casablanca’s finest hotels sit on the ocean corniche or in the smart city centre, pairing five-star comfort with sea views, spas and some of the best restaurants in the country. Book here when you want polish and a memorable base in Morocco’s biggest city.

Four Seasons Hotel Casablanca

$$$$ LuxuryAin Diab cornicheOcean viewsPool & spa

A gleaming beachfront flagship on the Ain Diab corniche, with large ocean-view rooms, landscaped pools, a spa and several restaurants, all a short drive from the Hassan II Mosque. Impeccable Four Seasons service and a breezy seaside setting make it the benchmark for luxury in the city. The definitive choice when you want the best on the water.

Hyatt Regency Casablanca

$$$$ LuxuryCity centreOn Place des Nations UniesRooftop pool

A long-established landmark right on the central Place des Nations Unies, steps from the old medina and the United Nations Square, with polished rooms, a rooftop pool and the famous Casablanca-themed Rick’s-style bar. Its location is unbeatable for sightseeing and business alike. A dependable, central five-star with genuine city-heart convenience.

Boutique

Casablanca’s boutique hotels lean into the city’s Art Deco heritage and its French-Moroccan style, with intimate rooms and a strong sense of place — a welcome change from the big business towers.

Hôtel & Spa Le Doge, Relais & Châteaux

$$$ BoutiqueArt DecoGourmet restaurantSpa

A jewel-box Relais & Châteaux hotel in a restored 1930s Art Deco townhouse, each room themed on a figure of the era, with a small spa, a rooftop and a celebrated gourmet restaurant. Intimate, elegant and impeccably run, it is the most characterful stay in the city. A refined pick for couples and design-lovers who prize charm over a big-hotel lobby.

Le Casablanca Hotel

$$$ BoutiqueCity centreAndalusian styleSpa & patio

A stylish boutique in the heart of the city, blending Andalusian and colonial design around a calm interior patio, with a spa, a good restaurant and warm, attentive service. Central for the train, the medina and the corniche, it offers boutique character without a resort’s scale. A comfortable, atmospheric base for independent travellers who want somewhere with personality.

Budget & Value

As Morocco’s business capital, Casablanca has plenty of dependable mid-range and budget hotels, especially in the city centre near the stations. You trade sea views for convenience and honest prices — ideal for a night on either side of a flight or train.

ibis Casablanca City Center

$ BudgetBy Casa-Voyageurs areaReliable chainGreat for transit

A tidy, modern budget hotel in the city centre, close to the rail links and an easy base for a short stay or an overnight before a flight. Rooms are compact, clean and well-kept, with air conditioning and a simple breakfast, all at a dependable price. The sensible, no-surprises choice for travellers who mainly need a comfortable, central bed.

Hôtel Idou Anfa

$$ Mid-rangeBoulevard d’AnfaRooftop poolCity views

A long-running Casablanca institution on Boulevard d’Anfa, this comfortable mid-range tower offers roomy, good-value rooms, a rooftop pool with city and ocean views, and a central location within reach of the corniche and downtown. It won’t dazzle, but the space, pool and price make it a solid all-rounder. A reliable value pick for sightseers and business travellers alike.

Family-friendly

Families do well in Casablanca’s larger hotels with pools, near the corniche beaches or in the city centre. Look for connecting rooms, a pool for downtime and an easy walk or drive to the Hassan II Mosque and the seafront.

Sofitel Casablanca Tour Blanche

$$$ UpscaleCity centrePool & spaFamily rooms

A polished five-star in a landmark central tower, with spacious rooms, a heated pool, a spa and several restaurants, walkable to the old medina and the Hassan II Mosque. Its space, service and central position make it a comfortable, easy base for families combining sightseeing with pool time. Reliable upscale comfort in the heart of the city.

$$$ UpscaleCity centreRooftop poolNear the mosque

A modern, well-run hotel a short walk from the Hassan II Mosque and the corniche, with generous rooms, a rooftop pool and an ample breakfast that families appreciate. Its recent build, central setting and dependable amenities make it an easy, comfortable pick for a family stay in the city. Contemporary and convenient rather than characterful.

Adults-only

Casablanca is a business and port city rather than a resort, so dedicated adults-only hotels are rare here. For a grown-up, tranquil stay, the boutique Le Doge or a corniche spa hotel usually delivers the calm without a formal age policy.
