Where to Stay in Kota Kinabalu

Where to Stay in Kota Kinabalu — Best Hotels & Areas

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Kota Kinabalu is the easygoing gateway to Sabah — Borneo’s island beaches, the reefs of Tunku Abdul Rahman Park, and mighty Mount Kinabalu inland. Stay on the seafront for sunsets over the South China Sea, or in the compact city centre for markets and island jetties. Below are KK’s best stays by style, each with an honest note and a Book Me link straight to Trip.com.
