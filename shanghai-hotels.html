Shanghai’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Shanghai — Best Areas & Hotels

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Shanghai wears many faces, and the hotel you choose sets the tone. Do you want a heritage room on the Bund facing Pudong’s towers, a plane-tree lane in the French Concession, or the buzz of Jing’an and the shops of Nanjing Road? We’ve grouped Shanghai’s most enduring, well-run hotels by style and character, each with an honest note and a Book Me link that opens that exact property on Trip.com so you can just choose your dates.

Luxury

Shanghai’s great hotels are destinations in themselves — Art Deco glamour on the Bund, celebrated restaurants and river views that rank among the world’s finest. Book here when you want the hotel to be as memorable as the city outside.

Waldorf Astoria Shanghai on the Bund

$$$$ LuxuryThe BundHeritage + towerLong Bar

Set in the restored former Shanghai Club on the historic Bund, this landmark pairs opulent heritage suites with a sleek adjoining tower, and its famous Long Bar is a destination in itself. Rooms look across the Huangpu to the Pudong skyline. The definitive Bund address for travellers who want old-Shanghai glamour with flawless modern service.

The Peninsula Shanghai

$$$$ LuxuryNorth BundArt DecoRolls-Royce fleet

The only new building on the Bund in decades, the Peninsula channels 1920s Art Deco in grand style, with large marble-clad rooms, a superb spa and pool, and a Rolls-Royce fleet at the door. It sits where the Bund meets the former British Consulate gardens. A benchmark of luxury for those who want space, service and a storied riverfront setting.

Boutique

Shanghai’s boutique hotels trade grand lobbies for design and neighbourhood soul — a converted factory on the South Bund, a green-roofed hideaway near the concession lanes. Ideal for independent travellers who want style and a real sense of place.

The Waterhouse at South Bund

$$$ BoutiqueSouth BundDesign iconRooftop bar

A celebrated design hotel carved from a 1930s warehouse on the South Bund, all raw concrete, Cor-ten steel and clever industrial-chic rooms, with a rooftop bar looking over to Pudong. It has just a handful of rooms and a cult following among architecture buffs. Best for design lovers who want something singular rather than a big-brand tower.

URBN Boutique Shanghai

$$$ BoutiqueJing’anEco-designLeafy courtyard

A pioneering carbon-neutral boutique hotel tucked down a quiet Jing’an lane, built from reclaimed materials around a serene courtyard, with warm, individually styled rooms. Its low-key green ethos and central-yet-hidden location win loyal fans. Ideal for travellers who want calm, character and a walkable base near the Jing’an Temple and the metro.

Budget & Value

Shanghai has plenty of well-run, well-placed mid-range and budget hotels that put you a short metro ride from the Bund and the concession lanes for a fraction of the landmark rates. You trade river views for location and reliability — and spend the savings on soup dumplings and rooftop bars.

Campanile Shanghai Bund Hotel

$$ Mid-rangeNear the BundReliableGood value

A comfortable French-brand three-star within walking distance of the Bund and the Yuyuan Garden, offering tidy modern rooms, friendly service and dependable value in a central spot. It won’t dazzle, but the price and position for old-town sightseeing are hard to beat. A sensible base for travellers who want to be near the action without the landmark bill.

Jinjiang Metropolo Hotel Shanghai People’s Square

$$ ValuePeople’s SquareCentral metro hubHeritage building

A dependable value hotel from the local Jinjiang group in a characterful older building right by People’s Square, the city’s central metro interchange. Rooms are compact but clean and comfortable, and almost every sight is a quick train ride away. Great for practical travellers who prize a central transport hub and honest prices over frills.

Family-Friendly

With children along, pools, space and easy access to the big attractions matter most. These hotels pair family rooms and kids’ facilities with locations that make Shanghai — and a Disney day — simple with little ones in tow.

Shanghai Disneyland Hotel

$$$ ResortDisney ResortKids’ paradiseIndoor pool

The flagship hotel of the Shanghai Disney Resort, an Art Nouveau fantasy wrapped around gardens, a large indoor pool and character dining, with a shuttle and easy access to the parks. Rooms are generous and family-ready, and the whole place is built around delighting children. The obvious choice for families making Disney the centrepiece of the trip.

Kerry Hotel Pudong Shanghai

$$$ UpscalePudongBig kids’ clubHuge pool

A large, resort-like hotel near the Kerry Parkside mall in Pudong, with one of the city’s best hotel kids’ clubs and adventure zones, a big indoor pool and superb restaurants. There’s space to spread out and plenty to keep children busy on a rainy day. Ideal for families who want room, a proper pool and dining variety under one roof.

Adults-Only & Romantic Retreats

For a honeymoon, an anniversary or a quiet grown-up escape, these are Shanghai’s most romantic and serene addresses — long on spa time, fine dining and calm. Not formally child-free, but adult and tranquil in mood; confirm any specifics when you book.

Amanyangyun, Shanghai

$$$$ LuxuryMinhangAntique villasSpa retreat

An extraordinary resort on the city’s green edge, built around camphor trees and Ming and Qing dynasty houses transplanted and rebuilt stone by stone, with vast antique villas and a serene Aman spa. It feels like a private estate an hour from the Bund. The ultimate romantic splurge for couples who want privacy, heritage and deep calm.

The PuLi Hotel and Spa

$$$$ LuxuryJing’anUrban resortInfinity pool

A sleek urban resort overlooking Jing’an Park, with serene, minimalist rooms, a long infinity pool, a superb spa and one of the city’s most elegant bars. Central yet hushed, it’s a favourite for couples who want calm and design in the middle of the city. A romantic, restful base within walking distance of the metro and the concession lanes.

Pet-Friendly Stays

A handful of international hotels in Shanghai genuinely welcome pets. These are reliable choices — always confirm the current pet policy, size limits and any cleaning fee directly on the booking page before you arrive.

Grand Hyatt Shanghai

$$$ UpscalePudong / Jin Mao TowerPets welcomeSky-high views

Soaring through the top floors of the landmark Jin Mao Tower, this hotel has jaw-dropping views, a striking atrium and an indoor pool near the clouds. As part of Hyatt’s pet-friendly program it welcomes dogs, and Lujiazui’s riverside promenades are close by for walks. A dramatic, pet-friendly perch in the heart of Pudong’s skyline.
