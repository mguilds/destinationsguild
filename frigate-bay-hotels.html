Where to Stay in Frigate Bay

By the Guild Explorers · Updated November 2026

Frigate Bay is the narrow isthmus ten minutes south-east of Basseterre where most of St Kitts’ resort rooms are. It has two coasts about a quarter of a mile apart: the calm Caribbean side with the big hotel and the casino, and the Atlantic side where the beach bars of the Strip sit directly on the sand. For a first visit to St Kitts this is the easiest place to base yourself.

Where to stay in Frigate Bay — the areas

One isthmus, two seas, and the only real concentration of hotel rooms on the island.

The Caribbean side — South Frigate Bay

The sheltered western shore, where the water is calm enough for ordinary swimming and where the island’s largest resort and its casino sit. This is the conventional beach-holiday end of St Kitts: loungers, pools, a run of restaurants, and a short walk between most of it. If you want to arrive, unpack and not think about logistics again, stay on this side.

The Atlantic side — North Frigate Bay and the Strip

A few hundred yards across the isthmus the Atlantic rolls in properly. The swimming is less forgiving and the beach is wilder, but this is where the Strip is — a line of wooden beach bars and grills on the sand that is the island’s social centre in the evening, locals and visitors together. Many people sleep on the calm side and eat on this one.

Beyond, on the South East Peninsula

The road climbs out of Frigate Bay and runs down a long peninsula of salt ponds and empty bays — Cockleshell, Majors, the view across to Nevis at the end. The best swimming water on St Kitts is down here, along with a handful of beach restaurants and one large luxury resort at Christophe Harbour. There is very little else, which is the attraction. You need a car or a taxi for all of it.

Getting to town and the airport

Basseterre is about ten minutes by road and Robert L. Bradshaw International is a similar distance, which is one of the quiet advantages of this stretch: you are close to the ferry for Nevis, close to the railway, and close to your flight home without being in the town.

What a room costs here

This is the part of St Kitts that prices like a resort, and it follows the standard Caribbean curve closely: high from mid-December to mid-April, low from July to early November, with shoulder rates in late spring and in the second half of November.

The peak within the peak is Christmas and New Year, when Sugar Mas runs on the island and the dry season is at its most reliable. The sharpest value is August and September, which are also the statistical heart of the hurricane season — a trade the Guild thinks is worth making only with cancellable rates and weather insurance.

Frigate Bay and the peninsula beyond it cover a wide span of price. The large branded resorts sit at the top, the smaller independent places on the isthmus at the middle, and the luxury resort out at Christophe Harbour is in a bracket of its own. Compare across the whole stretch rather than fixing on one property: the distances between them are small.

St. Kitts Marriott Beach Resort, Casino & Spa

The biggest hotel on the island and the most thoroughly documented on its own site. Marriott gives the address as 858 Frigate Bay Road and names eight dining outlets — the ARWEE Great Room Lobby Bar and ARWEE Sushi Bar, Calypso Cafe and Calypso Restaurant, Bohemia Beach Pool Bar and Grille, FIRE Steak and Seafood, La Cucina and the Pizza Shack. There is a main pool with a swim-up bar plus north and south pools and a whirlpool, the Emerald Mist Spa, and the Royal Beach Casino, which the page calls one of the largest and most modern in the Caribbean. Palapas on the beach are first come, first served and free; private cabanas are rentable for the day at US$35, which is Marriott’s own published figure. It does not publish a room count and it is not all-inclusive.

Travelling in your later years

This is the easiest stretch of St Kitts for anyone who would rather not deal with steps and hills. The isthmus is flat, the large resort has level ground between the rooms, the pools and the sand, and the distance from a room to a lounger is short in a way it is not at the plantation inns on Nevis. If mobility is a consideration and you want one base for a week, this is the sensible one.

The two coasts are different underfoot as well as in the water. The Caribbean side is calm and shelves gently; the Atlantic side has surf and a steeper drop at the water’s edge, which is worth knowing before you walk in. The Strip’s bars are on the sand, which means soft ground and occasional wooden steps rather than a level pavement.

For excursions, the scenic railway is much the gentler of the two famous outings — you are seated for about two hours — while Brimstone Hill is a steep fortress with uneven stone at the top. Taxis here are plentiful and used to waiting; agreeing a return time with a driver is normal practice and removes most of the difficulty from a day out.

Questions travellers ask about staying in Frigate Bay

Which side of Frigate Bay should I stay on?

The Caribbean side, for almost everybody. The water is calm enough for ordinary swimming, that is where the large resorts and the casino are, and it is a short walk across the isthmus to the Atlantic side in the evening when the beach bars on the Strip get going. Staying on the Atlantic side puts you on a wilder beach with less swimming and fewer rooms. Most people sleep on one side and eat on the other.

Is Frigate Bay walkable, or do I need a car?

Frigate Bay itself is walkable — the isthmus is small and flat and you can cross it in a few minutes. For everything else you want wheels. Basseterre is about ten minutes by taxi, the airport a similar distance, and the South East Peninsula beaches are a proper drive beyond the isthmus. Taxis are easy to arrange through any hotel; a hire car makes sense if you intend to explore the peninsula and the north of the island.

Is the Park Hyatt at Frigate Bay?

No, and we would rather say so than let the name of this page imply it. The Park Hyatt is at Christophe Harbour, out on the South East Peninsula, a drive beyond the Frigate Bay isthmus. It is on this page because it is the luxury option on this side of the island and because anyone comparing Frigate Bay hotels will want it in the comparison — but if you are picturing a short walk to the Strip, that is not where it is.

Is there a casino on St Kitts?

Yes, at the St Kitts Marriott Beach Resort at Frigate Bay. Marriott’s own page calls the Royal Beach Casino one of the largest and most modern in the Caribbean. It is the main one on the island, and its presence is part of why Frigate Bay feels busier in the evening than anywhere else on St Kitts outside Basseterre.

How far is the airport?

About ten minutes by road. Robert L. Bradshaw International sits just outside Basseterre, on the same side of the island as Frigate Bay, so arrivals and departures here are unusually painless by Caribbean standards. The port authority gives the runway as 2,321 metres and the airport’s 2024 traffic as 317,618 passengers.

Are the beach bars on the Strip worth it?

They are the reason a lot of people remember St Kitts fondly. The Strip is a line of wooden bars and grills directly on the Atlantic sand on the north side of the isthmus, busiest from late afternoon, and it is one of the few places in the eastern Caribbean where visitors and residents genuinely drink in the same spots. It is sand underfoot and it is informal; dress accordingly and bring cash as well as a card.
