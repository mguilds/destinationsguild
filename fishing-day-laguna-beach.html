A Fishing Day from Laguna Beach — Where You Can, Where You Cannot

You cannot fish most of Laguna’s own shore. But two working sportfishing harbours lie about twenty minutes either side of it, with half-day boats, bass most days, and room for a child at the rail.

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Checked against the California Department of Fish and Wildlife and the City of Laguna Beach, 4 November 2026

This guide began with one real plan: a fishing day with a grandchild out of Laguna. The first thing we found out is the thing most visitors do not know. Nearly all of Laguna Beach’s shoreline is a protected marine area, and you may not take a fish from it, or a shell, or a crab from a tide pool.

That does not spoil the day. Laguna sits between Newport Beach and Dana Point, and both have sportfishing landings with half-day boats that leave most days of the year. So you sleep in Laguna if you like, drive twenty minutes, and fish from a boat or a pier. Here is where you can go, what it costs, and how it works with a child. Where these facts come from. The rules on this page were read on 4 November 2026 from the California Department of Fish and Wildlife (CDFW): its 2026 Ocean Sport Fishing Regulations booklet, its Marine Protected Area pages and its regional summaries updated 2 November 2026; and from the City of Laguna Beach. Fares, times and fish counts were read from each landing’s own website the same day. Rules and fares change — check before you go.

Where you can fish, and where you cannot

Reading the coast from north to south. Fishing inside the two middle rows is against the law.

StretchProtected areaFishing?

Abalone Point south to Goff IslandLaguna Beach State Marine ReserveNo. All take is prohibited.
Goff Island south to Table RockLaguna Beach No-Take State Marine Conservation AreaNo. All take is prohibited.
South of the no-take area, to Dana PointDana Point State Marine Conservation AreaYes, for finfish by hook and line. Nothing may be taken from the tide pools.

What that means on the beach

Do not bring a rod to Main Beach, Heisler Park or Victoria Beach. Swimming, surfing, paddling and looking at the tide pools are all welcome; fishing and collecting are not. Leave every shell, rock and animal where you find it. The protection goes out to sea as well as along the sand, so it also applies to a boat offshore of the town.

Confirmed with CDFW and the City of Laguna Beach, 4 November 2026 — CDFW describes the reserve as running from Abalone Point, “Laguna’s northernmost city limit, south to Goff Island”, and the no-take area as extending “south from Goff Island to Table Rock”. The regulations for both read: “It is unlawful to injure, damage, take, or possess any living, geological, or cultural marine resource.” The City’s Marine Safety page says: “The areas of Laguna Beach north of Abalone Point and south of Seacliff Drive are open for fishing.” CDFW names Table Rock and the City names Seacliff Drive; if you are near that line, go by the CDFW map. To report a violation, the City gives (949) 497-0310.

The half-day boats — Newport or Dana Point

A half-day is about five to six hours. Real landings, each with its own website, opened on 4 November 2026. No ratings, no paid placements.

Davey’s Locker — Balboa Pavilion, Newport Beach

Half-day boats, three-quarter days, overnights, twilight trips and whale watching. Listed half-day departures run from early morning to evening. Half-day fares were listed at $65–$80 for adults and $55–$65 for juniors and seniors; rod rental $22. A day licence can be added to the booking. It says no refunds within 48 hours of a half-day departure. 400 Main Street; (949) 673-1434. daveyslocker.com

Newport Landing — Newport Beach

Half-day, three-quarter day, overnight and twilight trips, and charters. Half-day fares were listed at $50–$75 for adults and $40–$60 for juniors and seniors; rod rental $22. (949) 675-0550. newportlanding.com

Dana Wharf Sportfishing — Dana Point Harbor

Half-day, twilight (spring and summer), three-quarter day and overnight trips. Half-day fares were listed at $79.50–$90.10 for adults and $63.60–$74.20 for children under 14 with an adult, before any fuel surcharge. Rods can be rented at the office. You can buy a one-day licence before you sail, and you are asked to arrive 30 minutes ahead. (888) 224-0603. danawharf.com

Which one?

There is little to choose on the fishing: all three work the same stretch of coast. Pick by what suits the day. Dana Point has the simpler parking and a child’s fare set out plainly. Newport puts you beside the Balboa Pier and the Fun Zone for the afternoon. Book ahead for weekends and school holidays, and ask the landing which boat is sailing: fares vary by boat and by day, and a limited-load boat costs more but has more room at the rail.

Parking. Dana Point Harbor’s structure at 799 Harbor Way gives the first four hours free, then $6 an hour to a $24 daily maximum; Dana Wharf customers must use the third level. Davey’s Locker points you to the Balboa Pier lot on Palm Street or the Newport Landing structure at East Bay Avenue and Adams Street, and advises arriving early.

Taking a child

The best fishing day for a youngster is one that ends while they still want more.

What the landings say, and what we would add

Age. Neither Davey’s Locker nor Dana Wharf gives a minimum age. Oceanside Sea Center, down the coast, gives the only plain advice we found: no official minimum, but a good day is rare under about seven or eight.

Licence. None under 16. Dana Wharf says children under 16 fish its trips without a licence, and that they carry the same catch limit as an adult.

Seasickness. Dana Wharf’s advice is to take preventative measures beforehand if you are prone to it. Oceanside Sea Center warns it cannot return to the dock to drop off a seasick passenger, so talk to your pharmacist before the trip, not on the morning. A first-timer is often better on a morning boat in calm weather; our guidance.

What to bring. Layers and a windproof jacket, a hat, sunglasses, and non-slip shoes. Davey’s Locker and Dana Wharf both bar outside alcohol and ice chests; Dana Wharf allows a brown-bag lunch and its boats sell food.

Try a pier first. If you do not know how a child will take to it, an hour on a pier costs nothing and you can walk off when it stops being fun. Dana Wharf also lists a kids’ fishing camp and clinic on its website; we have not read their dates.

Piers and shore near Laguna

No boat, no ticket, and on a public pier no licence. Fishing rules still apply.

Balboa Pier and Newport Pier, Newport Beach

The City of Newport Beach lists both as open from 5 a.m. to midnight. They closed for a storm in October 2026 and reopened on the 12th. The photograph below shows anglers after mackerel on Balboa. Check the city’s notices in stormy weather.

Dana Point Harbor

The harbour allows shore fishing from the outermost jetties and the Dana Point Pier without a licence, and forbids fishing along the boardwalk. Everywhere else in the harbour needs a licence and the property owner’s written permission. We could not confirm fishing from Baby Beach, so do not assume it.

Doheny and San Clemente State Beaches

California State Parks lists fishing at Doheny, and names bass, croaker, corbina and barred perch at San Clemente State Beach. A licence is needed from 16 on the sand. The San Clemente Pier closed after a June 2026 swell and we could not confirm it has reopened, so check with the city.

Crystal Cove State Park

North of Abalone Point, hook-and-line fishing for finfish is allowed in the conservation area, with no taking from the tide pools. The park was open with some restrictions on 4 November 2026.

Further up: Huntington Beach

State Parks calls Bolsa Chica a great place for surf fishing, and allows surf fishing at Huntington State Beach as long as no swimmers are nearby. More in our surf section.

What you will catch

Bass are the steady half-day fish here, and many go back.

MonthsWhat Dana Wharf says is typical
January–MarchCalico bass, halibut, sculpin, white seabass. Boats may not keep rockfish: the groundfish season is closed.
April–JuneRockfish, calico and sand bass, halibut, white seabass, barracuda; yellowtail and bluefin begin in June.
July–SeptemberSand and calico bass, yellowtail, barracuda, bonito, with tuna and dorado in range of the longer trips.
October–DecemberCalico, halibut and bonito in October; then halibut, and calico or sand bass if the water stays warm.

This very week

Fish counts are posted on the landings’ own pages, and they are the honest way to choose a day. Western Pride, Newport, afternoon half-day, 2 November 2026: 41 anglers, 36 calico bass, 9 sand bass, 5 sheephead, 2 whitefish, 1 blue perch. Clemente, Dana Point, half-day, 3 November: 40 anglers, 11 bonito, 5 sheephead, 4 sand bass, 2 yellowtail and more, with 280 bass released. Tuna were within reach of day boats that week, too; Dana Wharf’s own list does not expect that in November, so do not count on it.

Confirmed with CDFW, 4 November 2026 — 2026 Ocean regulations: kelp, barred sand and spotted sand bass “Five, in any combination”, with a maximum of 4 barred sand bass and a 14-inch minimum. Southern groundfish, 1 October to 31 December: boats may take shelf and slope rockfish and lingcod only, seaward of the 50-fathom line; nearshore rockfish, cabezon and greenlings are closed. 1 January to 31 March: closed. Anglers on shore, piers and jetties are exempt from those seasons, but not from the zero limit on cowcod, yelloweye, quillback and bronzespotted rockfish. Crew will measure your bass for you; take their word for what goes back. Counts from Davey’s Locker and Dana Wharf.

The licence, plainly

Full detail is in our California fishing guide.

For a day out

Under 16: none. On a public pier: none, at any age. On a boat or a beach, 16 and over: yes. A one-day licence is the simple answer for a visitor, and it needs no extra ocean stamp. Davey’s Locker, Dana Wharf and others sell or arrange it, or buy it from CDFW online first.

Confirmed with CDFW, 4 November 2026 — 2026 fees: one-day $21.09, two-day $32.40, for residents and visitors alike. The Ocean Enhancement Validation is required south of Point Arguello but “not required when fishing under the authority of a One or Two-Day Sport Fishing License.” Lobster and some other fish need report cards even on a pier.

A day on Catalina

The ferry leaves from the same harbour as the Dana Point boats.

Dana Point to Avalon

Catalina Express runs from Dana Point in about an hour and a half. Between 20 October 2026 and 31 March 2027 the morning boat leaves at 9:30; the 5:00 p.m. return runs on most days but not all, so check the timetable before you plan it as a day trip. Round-trip fares were listed at $111 for adults and $89.50 for children aged 2 to 11. Avalon’s Green Pleasure Pier needs no licence, say the island’s visitor pages.

Do not fish at Casino Point, which is no-take, or at Lover’s Cove, where hook and line is allowed only from the Cabrillo Mole. And leave the bright orange garibaldi alone.

Near the boats, or near the beach

Laguna Beach is the prettiest base and is equally close to both harbours. Newport Beach puts you a walk from Davey’s Locker. Dana Point puts you beside the boats and the Catalina ferry. An early boat is easier if you sleep next to it.

Three or four of you? Take a house, not two hotel rooms. A condo in Dana Point or Newport gives you a kitchen for the early breakfast, a freezer for what you keep, and a place to rinse the gear. Our vacation homes guide has the questions to ask before you pay.

Questions

Can you fish in Laguna Beach?

Mostly no. From Abalone Point south to Table Rock, the Laguna Beach State Marine Reserve and the No-Take State Marine Conservation Area bar all fishing and all take of marine life. The City of Laguna Beach says the parts of town north of Abalone Point and south of Seacliff Drive are open for fishing.

Where is the nearest half-day fishing boat to Laguna Beach?

Newport Beach and Dana Point. Davey’s Locker and Newport Landing sail from Newport Harbor, and Dana Wharf Sportfishing from Dana Point Harbor. Each is roughly eight or nine miles from Main Beach, about twenty minutes without traffic by our map estimate.

Does my grandchild need a fishing licence?

Not under 16. California requires a sport fishing licence from age 16. Dana Wharf confirms children under 16 can fish its trips without one, under the same catch limits as adults. An adult needs a licence on a boat; a one-day licence was $21.09 in 2026.

How old does a child have to be to go on a half-day boat?

Neither Davey’s Locker nor Dana Wharf states a minimum age. Oceanside Sea Center, further south, says it has no official minimum but has rarely seen a good day with children under seven or eight. Use your own judgement, and start with a pier if you are unsure.

Do you need a licence to fish Balboa Pier or the Dana Point pier?

No licence is needed to fish from a public pier in ocean or bay waters, though every other rule still applies. Jetties and breakwaters are not public piers under California’s definition. Dana Point Harbor says shore fishing is allowed from its outermost jetties and the Dana Point Pier without a licence; if in doubt there, carry one.

What will we catch on a half-day boat in November?

Mostly bass. On 2 November 2026 the Western Pride out of Newport reported 36 calico and 9 sand bass among 41 anglers; on 3 November a Dana Wharf half-day boat reported 280 bass released. Many bass are under the 14-inch minimum and go back. Boats may fish only the deep rockfish in October to December.

What we could not check, and will not pretend to have

The part of a fishing page most sites leave out.

Four honest gaps

Seacliff Drive and Table Rock. The City and CDFW use different landmarks for the southern edge of the closed water, and we have not walked the line. Use CDFW’s map.

Fares. Landings publish ranges that change by boat and day, and Dana Wharf’s page shows two unlabelled sets. Confirm yours when you book.

Davey’s Locker’s junior age. We could not find it stated. Ask when you book.

Drive times. About 9 miles to Davey’s Locker and 8 to Dana Wharf from Main Beach are our own map estimates without traffic. Pacific Coast Highway in summer can take much longer, so leave early.
