Where to Stay in Savannah — the Historic District, River Street, the Victorian District & Tybee

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated November 2026

Savannah is a city made for walking, and where you sleep decides how much of it you see on foot. The Historic Landmark District, laid out around its leafy park squares, holds most of what visitors come for. River Street runs along the water at its northern edge, the Victorian District and Forsyth Park lie beyond the squares, and Tybee Island, the city’s beach, is a drive away. This page sorts the areas and names the hotels and inns we could confirm today.

Choosing Your Area in Savannah

Four places to base yourself, each suited to a different trip.

The Historic Landmark District

Visit Savannah, the city’s official visitors bureau, invites you to “stroll through 22 park squares and explore museums, monuments, restored 18th-century homes” here. This is the heart of the city: oak-shaded squares, old houses, small museums and restaurants, all close together.

Stay here for a first visit. One of our listed inns puts it plainly on its own website: you can spend several days exploring on foot, but you will need a vehicle for Tybee Island and Wormsloe Historic Site.

River Street and the waterfront

The bureau calls River Street “a popular hub for locals and visitors alike”. It runs along the Savannah River, and the newer Eastern Wharf area extends the riverfront promenade with restaurants and rooftops.

Good if you want to be by the water and in the thick of things.

The Victorian District and Forsyth Park

Beyond the squares. The bureau describes a 50-block neighbourhood known as Savannah’s first suburb, featuring Forsyth Park. Starland, close by, is the place to “shop for vintage clothing, vinyl records and original art” and find quirky cafés.

Choose it for a quieter, more residential feel, with the park for morning walks.

Tybee Island, Southside and beyond

Tybee Island is the city’s beach; the bureau says it has five beaches and plenty of fresh seafood. For a road trip or an early flight, the bureau notes that the Southside district is convenient to the airport, a quick ride from downtown, and offers a range of affordable hotels.

You will want a car for either.

The Marshall House

Historic DistrictHistoricBreakfast included

At 123 East Broughton Street. Its own website calls it a Savannah landmark since 1851 and describes 3 suites and 65 guest rooms with original floors and doors and tall ceilings. Every stay includes a full breakfast each morning and wine and hors d’oeuvres in the evening, its site says. Its history page tells how the building served as a hospital during the yellow fever epidemics and as a Union hospital at the end of the Civil War.

Getting There & When to Go

The handful of Savannah details that change how a trip goes.

Getting to Savannah

The airport is Savannah/Hilton Head International (SAV), which Visit Savannah says is 15 miles from the Historic District. By car, the bureau notes the city is reached along the East Coast on Interstate 95 or from the west on Interstate 16. Amtrak’s Palmetto and Silver Service lines stop here; the bureau puts the station about a 10–15-minute drive from the Historic District.

Getting around

Inside the Historic District you will walk: the squares are close together. You will want a car or a tour for Tybee Island, Wormsloe and anywhere outside downtown. If you bring a car, check your hotel’s own parking page before you arrive.

When to go

The bureau points to the holiday season for mild weather, festive charm and fewer crowds.

Savannah is on the Atlantic coast. The US National Hurricane Center gives the Atlantic hurricane season as 1 June to 30 November, with the peak on 10 September. If you travel then, choose a rate you can change.

Savannah Hotels FAQ

The questions travellers ask most before booking a room here.

Where is the best area to stay in Savannah?

The Historic Landmark District for a first visit, so you can walk to the squares, museums and restaurants. River Street for the waterfront. The Victorian District near Forsyth Park for a quieter stay. Tybee Island if you want the beach.

How many squares are in Savannah?

The official visitors bureau, Visit Savannah, invites visitors to stroll through 22 park squares in the Historic Landmark District.

Do I need a car in Savannah?

Not in the Historic District, which is easy to explore on foot. You will need a car or a tour for Tybee Island, Wormsloe Historic Site and other places outside downtown.

How far is the airport from downtown Savannah?

Savannah/Hilton Head International Airport (SAV) is 15 miles from the Historic District, according to Visit Savannah.

When is hurricane season in Savannah?

The US National Hurricane Center gives the Atlantic hurricane season as 1 June to 30 November, with the peak on 10 September. Travellers in those months should book a rate they can change.
