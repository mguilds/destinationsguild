Common questions about where to stay in Cancún

Should I stay in the Hotel Zone or downtown Cancún?

The Hotel Zone is the narrow barrier island where the beach and almost all the resorts are, and it is where most visitors stay. Downtown Cancún, El Centro, is the working city on the mainland: cheaper rooms, local restaurants and markets, but no beach of its own and a bus or taxi ride to the sand. If the beach is the reason you are coming, stay in the Hotel Zone.

Which stretch of the Hotel Zone should I pick?

Around Punta Cancún, where the island turns the corner, the beaches face north into the sheltered bay and the water is generally calmer, which suits families and swimmers. The long eastern stretch running south has the widest sand and a bigger surf. The far south end near Punta Nizuc is the quietest and closest to the airport, but you will rely on taxis to reach the restaurants and nightlife further north.

Do I need an all-inclusive resort in Cancún?

Not necessarily. All-inclusive works well if you plan to stay on the property and want the cost settled in advance. The Hotel Zone also has plenty of independent restaurants, and several hotels sell room-only or bed-and-breakfast rates that cost considerably less if you intend to explore, take day trips to Tulum or Chichen Itza, or eat downtown.

What about sargassum seaweed?

Sargassum seaweed runs on Mexico’s Caribbean-facing beaches from about April to October, with June and July the heaviest months and November to March normally clear. It varies a great deal from year to year and week to week. The north-facing beaches around Punta Cancún usually get less of it than the long east-facing stretch, and the larger resorts rake their sand each morning. It is worth checking current conditions close to your travel dates rather than relying on any one season’s pattern.
