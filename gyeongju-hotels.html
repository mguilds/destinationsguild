Gyeongju’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Gyeongju — Best Areas & Hotels

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Gyeongju is Korea’s open-air museum — the thousand-year Silla capital of royal tomb mounds and stone Buddhas — and it splits into two very different bases. The lakeside resort zone of Bomun has the big hotels, the lake and the theme parks; the old town around the tombs and Hwangnam-dong is where you’ll find hanok stays and the night markets on foot. We’ve grouped Gyeongju’s best-run stays by the kind of trip you’re after, each with an honest note and a Book Me link that opens that exact property on Trip.com.

Luxury

Gyeongju’s top hotels line the shore of Bomun Lake, a purpose-built resort district a short drive from the ancient sights — big grounds, pools and lake views.

Hilton Gyeongju

$$$$ LuxuryBomun LakeLake viewsIndoor pool & spa

The polished anchor of the Bomun Lake resort, with lake-view rooms, an indoor pool, a spa and the Woljeonggyo bridge and the museums a short drive away. It pairs international five-star comfort with a tranquil lakeside setting and neat Korean design touches in the lobby. A smooth, reliable luxury base for exploring the Silla sights by day and unwinding by the water.

Lahan Select Gyeongju

$$$$ UpscaleBomun LakeBig resortPools & gardens

A large, well-run lakeside resort (long known as the Hyundai) right on Bomun Lake, with generous rooms, indoor and outdoor pools, landscaped gardens and easy access to the lake path, the theme parks and the historic sites. Its scale and facilities make it a comfortable, everything-on-site choice for a Gyeongju stay. Dependable resort comfort in the prime lakeside spot.

Boutique & Hanok

For character, Gyeongju offers something the big cities can’t — a night in a traditional hanok in the old town, or a design-minded hotel near the great temple — putting you close to the history in style.

Gyeongju Hwangnamgwan Hanok Hotel

$$$ HanokHwangnam old townCourtyard roomsWalk to the tombs

A beautifully done modern hanok in the heart of the Hwangnam-dong old town, with tiled-roof buildings, wooden rooms and quiet courtyards, steps from the royal tomb park, Cheomseongdae observatory and the buzzing Hwangnidan-gil café street. It offers the romance of a traditional stay with contemporary comforts and an unbeatable walkable location. The pick for soaking up old Gyeongju on foot.

Kolon Hotel

$$ Mid-rangeNear BulguksaLandmark designGarden pool

A distinctive low-rise hotel nestled below Bulguksa temple and Tohamsan, celebrated for its striking traditional-modern architecture by the noted architect Kim Swoo-geun, with gardens, an outdoor pool and mountain calm. It puts the temple and the Seokguram grotto within easy reach and trades resort gloss for genuine character. A design-lover’s base close to Gyeongju’s greatest monument.

Budget & Value

Gyeongju is kind to a budget: comfortable mid-range hotels near the lake or the theme parks cost far less than a big-city room, leaving more for temple tickets and bike rentals.

Commodore Hotel Gyeongju

$$ ValueBomun LakeHanok-style facadeGood value

A well-priced lakeside hotel with a handsome traditional-style exterior on the Bomun resort strip, offering comfortable rooms, a pool and an easy walk to the lake and its attractions at a friendly rate. It delivers a resort-area location and solid comforts without the top-tier price, making it a sensible value base. Good for travelers who want to be by the lake for less.

Family-friendly

Gyeongju is a natural family trip — bikes around the tombs, boats on the lake and two theme parks — and the resort hotels give children pools and space between the sightseeing.

Hilton Gyeongju

$$$$ LuxuryBomun LakeIndoor poolNear theme parks

Beyond its polish, the Hilton is a comfortable family base on Bomun Lake, with an indoor pool, roomy accommodations and the lake path, boat rides and Gyeongju World theme park all close by. Children have space to unwind after a day among the tombs and temples, and the lakeside setting is safe and scenic. A smooth, all-ages choice on the resort strip.

There’s room to roam, nature on the doorstep and the great monuments a short hop away. A relaxed, good-value base for families who want the temple side of Gyeongju rather than the lake.

Pet-friendly

Gyeongju’s pet scene is small but real — one lakeside property has even reinvented itself around welcoming dogs. Always confirm the size limit, fee and room type when you book.

Kinock Hotel Gyeongju (Pet-Friendly)

$$ ValueBomun LakePets welcomeFormerly The Suites

A lakeside hotel on the Bomun strip (formerly The Suites Hotel Gyeongju) that has re-launched with a strong pet-friendly focus, welcoming dogs with dedicated rooms and facilities alongside comfortable suites and lake access. It’s a rare find for travelers who want to bring the dog along on a Gyeongju trip. Confirm the current pet policy and fees when booking.
