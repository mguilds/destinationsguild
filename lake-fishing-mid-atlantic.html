Lake Fishing in the Mid-Atlantic — Virginia, West Virginia, Maryland and Delaware

Smith Mountain Lake, Lake Anna, Kerr Lake on the Virginia–North Carolina line, Summersville Lake and Deep Creek Lake, with a short word on Delaware’s ponds: what swims in each, the rules from the agencies that set them, the licence a visitor needs, where the boats go in, and where to sleep.

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Checked against Virginia DWR, NC Wildlife, West Virginia DNR, Maryland DNR and Delaware DNREC, 23 November 2026

The Mid-Atlantic is not short of lakes, and most of them are reservoirs built in the last century: long, branching water in wooded hills, with a striped bass fishery that grew up behind the dams. Smith Mountain Lake, near Roanoke, and Lake Anna, between Richmond and Fredericksburg, are Virginia’s two famous striper lakes. Kerr Lake — Buggs Island Lake to Virginians — straddles the North Carolina line and is big enough that both states honour each other’s licences on it. Up in the mountains, Summersville Lake in West Virginia is clear, cliff-lined water with walleye and smallmouth, and Deep Creek Lake in western Maryland is, in the state’s own words, its premier ice-fishing lake.

This guide belongs to The Guild Fishing Library. It is written for families with a spinning rod, boat anglers towing a trailer, and anyone who wants a guide for a day. Every rule, limit and price below was read on 23 November 2026 from the body that sets it, and is dated. Three things to know before you plan: water is low this autumn on Kerr and Lake Anna, and many ramps are closed until it rises; Smith Mountain Lake and Kerr have striped bass rules that change with the season; and Deep Creek closes walleye from 1 March to 15 April. Where we could not confirm something, we say so.

The best months, in plain words

These are southern and mountain lakes. Virginia’s lakes fish all year; Summersville is lowered for winter, and Deep Creek usually freezes.

LakeBest monthsWhy
Smith Mountain LakeMarch to May; October to DecemberOur guidance: spring and late autumn for striped bass and bass in shallower water. From 1 November to 31 May every striper from 30 to 40 inches must be released (a rule).
Lake AnnaMarch to June; September to NovemberOur guidance only. The lake cools the North Anna power station, which the Virginia Department of Wildlife Resources calls “thermally enriched”; no agency page we read gives it a best season.
Kerr Lake (Buggs Island)April to June; autumnOur guidance: spring for crappie and stripers. The striped bass rules change on 1 June and 1 October (a rule, see below). Check the water level: many ramps were closed this autumn.
Summersville LakeMay to OctoberOur guidance: the summer pool, when every ramp is in the water. The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers lowers the lake in late autumn and winter (a fact), and West Virginia DNR’s winter guide sends walleye anglers to 25 to 50 feet of water.
Deep Creek LakeJune to October; the ice seasonBass may be kept from 16 June, and walleye are closed from 1 March to 15 April (rules). Maryland DNR calls the lake the state’s premier ice-fishing water when the weather allows.

“Our guidance” marks advice from the Guild Explorers, not a rule. The seasons and limits that are rules are in each lake below and in the licence section, and are dated.

Smith Mountain Lake — Virginia’s big striper lake

A reservoir on the Roanoke River south-east of Roanoke. Fishing rules: the Virginia Department of Wildlife Resources (DWR). State park: Virginia State Parks.

Smith Mountain Lake, from Moneta to Penhook

What swims there. DWR lists black bass (largemouth and smallmouth), striped bass, crappie, catfish — channel, flathead and white — sunfish and white perch. Muskellunge are stocked, but DWR says the lake produces few of them.

Striped bass. Two a day. From 1 June to 31 October there is no length limit. From 1 November to 31 May every striped bass between 30 and 40 inches must be released at once, unharmed.

Black bass. Five a day, of which only two may be under 14 inches. This rule covers the lake and its tributaries below Niagara Dam.

How people fish it. Our guidance: stripers are usually trolled or fished with live bait from a boat, following the fish as they move with the seasons; bass anglers work the docks and points. A guide is the quickest way to learn the lake.

Boat ramps. DWR runs public landings at Oak Grove, Scruggs and Penhook — but DWR has posted that temporary closures are planned at all three, with work starting on 30 November 2026, so check its Smith Mountain Lake page before you tow. Smith Mountain Lake State Park, at Huddleston on the north shore, has a ramp (a launch fee for day visitors; free for overnight guests), a 112-foot fishing pier into deep water, 20 cabins each with a dock slip, and a 50-site campground open from the first Friday in March to the first Monday in December.

Nearest towns: Moneta, the lakeside village; Bedford, the town to the north; and Roanoke, the nearest city.

Confirmed with the Virginia Department of Wildlife Resources and Virginia State Parks, 23 November 2026 — DWR’s Smith Mountain Lake page and its 2026 creel and length limits, and the state park’s own page.

Lake Anna — warm water, stripers and largemouth

A 13,000-acre reservoir in Louisa and Spotsylvania counties that cools the North Anna nuclear power station. Fishing rules: Virginia DWR. State park: Virginia State Parks.

Lake Anna, from the dam to the upper arms

What swims there. DWR names largemouth bass, striped bass, hybrid striped bass and black crappie as the main fish, along with bluegill, channel catfish, white catfish and white perch.

Keeping a fish. Striped bass and hybrids: four a day in total, none under 20 inches — Virginia’s statewide rule for lakes, which Lake Anna follows; it has no special entry in DWR’s 2026 table. Largemouth bass: DWR’s Lake Anna page gives five a day with no length limit.

How people fish it. Our guidance: the warm water from the power station keeps fish active later into the year than on other Virginia lakes; stripers and hybrids are chased with live bait and by watching for birds over breaking fish, and largemouth are fished around docks and grass.

Boat ramps. DWR says its own ramp off Route 522 has been closed since it lost the lease, so launching is at private marinas or at Lake Anna State Park. The park’s ramp opens for anglers as early as 5:30 a.m. on summer weekdays and 6:30 a.m. on weekends and holidays — but on 23 November 2026 the park warned that low water may stop guests using the ramp or the cabin-area docks. The park has 10 two-bedroom cabins, 6 camping cabins, 4 yurts, 2 six-bedroom lodges, 46 campsites (first Friday in March to first Monday in December), and a 2-acre fishing pond made for children and anglers with disabilities.

Nearest towns: Louisa, the county seat to the west, and Fredericksburg, the nearest city, to the north-east. The lakeside villages of Mineral and Bumpass are small; use the vacation homes button for a lake house.

Confirmed with the Virginia Department of Wildlife Resources and Virginia State Parks, 23 November 2026. DWR’s Lake Anna page was last updated in 2022; the 2026 creel table shows no special rule for the lake.

Kerr Lake (Buggs Island Lake) — one lake, two states

John H. Kerr Reservoir: about 50,000 acres and 900 miles of shore on the Virginia–North Carolina line, run by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (Wilmington District). Fishing rules: Virginia DWR and the North Carolina Wildlife Resources Commission.

Kerr Lake, from Clarksville to the dam

What swims there. The Corps calls Kerr “widely known for large-mouth bass and striped bass fishing”.

Which licence. Either a Virginia or a North Carolina licence. The two states honour each other’s licences on Kerr and Lake Gaston, including the tributaries you can reach by boat from the main lake. Virginia’s rules apply in Virginia’s part of the lake and North Carolina’s in North Carolina’s part.

Striped bass (and hybrids). From 1 October to 31 May: two a day, and only fish between 20 and 26 inches may be kept. From 1 June to 30 September: four a day, with no length limit. Virginia and North Carolina publish the same rule.

Crappie. In Virginia’s waters, 25 a day and none under 9 inches. We did not confirm North Carolina’s crappie rule for its side — check with NC Wildlife.

Boat ramps — check before you tow. The Corps runs 16 ramps around the lake and charges a fee at most of them. On 23 November 2026 low water had closed a great many: Occoneechee State Park, at Clarksville, said all its boat ramps and its fuel dock were closed, and North Carolina’s Kerr Lake State Recreation Area said its Kimball Point ramp and the NC Wildlife ramp at Hibernia were the only ones open on the North Carolina side.

Camping and cabins. The Corps runs four campgrounds (Buffalo Park, Longwood, North Bend and Rudds Creek), North Carolina’s state recreation area seven, and Virginia two; the Corps has cancelled the 2025–26 winter camping season. Occoneechee State Park has 11 cabins, 2 six-bedroom lodges and 3 yurts.

Nearest towns: Clarksville, the only town on the lake itself; South Hill, on Interstate 85 in Virginia; and Henderson, North Carolina, near the state recreation area.

Confirmed with the Virginia Department of Wildlife Resources (reciprocal licences, the Buggs Island page and the 2026 creel table), the North Carolina Wildlife Resources Commission (rule 15A NCAC 10C .0314), the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Virginia State Parks and North Carolina State Parks, 23 November 2026.

Summersville Lake — clear water under the cliffs

A reservoir on the Gauley River in Nicholas County, run by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers. Fishing rules: the West Virginia Division of Natural Resources (DNR).

Summersville Lake, from the dam to Persinger Creek

What swims there. The Corps lists walleye, perch, crappie, bluegill, channel catfish, smallmouth and largemouth bass; West Virginia State Parks adds muskellunge.

Walleye — a special rule. From Summersville Dam up to the mouth of Persinger Creek, every walleye from 20 to 30 inches must be released at once. You may keep eight a day, and only one of them may be over 30 inches.

Bass. West Virginia’s statewide limit: six black bass a day in any combination, with no statewide minimum size.

How people fish it. DNR’s 2026 winter guide sends walleye anglers to 25 to 50 feet of water. Our guidance: in summer, smallmouth along the rock walls and points are the classic Summersville trip, and a pontoon guide makes it easy for a family.

Boat ramps and camping. The Corps lists ramps at Battle Run, Salmon Run and Long Point, and Summersville Lake Marina at Long Point. Battle Run Campground has 117 campsites. At summer pool the lake covers about 2,790 acres; in late autumn and winter the Corps lowers it to elevation 1,575, and DNR advises checking the level before you launch — some ramps sit high and dry in winter.

Nearest towns: Summersville, beside the dam, and Fayetteville, to the south at the New River Gorge.

Confirmed with the West Virginia Division of Natural Resources (its 2026 fishing regulations summary and winter fishing guide), the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers and West Virginia State Parks, 23 November 2026. The Corps’ Huntington District page for the lake would not open for us; we used the Corps’ division page.

Deep Creek Lake — Maryland’s mountain lake

A mountain reservoir in Garrett County, in Maryland’s far west. Fishing rules and the lake itself: the Maryland Department of Natural Resources (DNR).

Deep Creek Lake, from McHenry to the state park

What swims there. DNR lists largemouth and smallmouth bass, walleye, northern pike, chain pickerel, yellow perch, bluegill, pumpkinseed and crappie.

Walleye. Closed from 1 March to 15 April; open the rest of the year. DNR’s Deep Creek page gives five a day and ten in possession, a 15-inch minimum, and protects fish between 18 and 21 inches.

Bass. Maryland closes bass to keeping from 1 March to 15 June in its freshwater lakes and rivers; catch-and-return fishing for largemouth is allowed during the closure. From 16 June most waters allow five a day with a 12-inch minimum.

Ice fishing. DNR calls Deep Creek Maryland’s premier ice-fishing lake when the weather allows — and says plainly that it does not monitor ice thickness. That is your job.

Boats. The main public launch is at Deep Creek Lake State Park, open 24 hours, weather permitting: a $10 launch fee for Maryland residents and $12 for others, paid at an honour box (or show a lake-use permit or Maryland park pass). Boats may be no longer than 26 feet (pontoons 30); houseboats are not allowed; keep to no-wake speed within 100 feet of shore, docks and other boats; and anyone born on or after 1 July 1972 needs a boating safety certificate. The park closes at sunset, but night anglers may park at the Ranger Station lot or the ramp.

Camping. The state park’s Meadow Mountain campground is open from 15 April to 15 December, with 82 basic and 23 electric sites, two mini cabins and a yurt.

Nearest towns: McHenry, on the lake, and Oakland, the county seat, to the south.

Confirmed with the Maryland Department of Natural Resources, 23 November 2026 — its Deep Creek Lake fishing, state park, boating and Natural Resources Management Area pages and its 2026 bass rule summary.

Delaware — ponds, not big lakes

Delaware has no large reservoir like its neighbours. Its freshwater fishing is in millponds and state-park ponds, managed by DNREC’s Division of Fish and Wildlife.

Lums Pond and Trap Pond

Lums Pond State Park, near Bear. Delaware’s largest freshwater pond, with a boat ramp, a fishing pier, and kayaks, canoes and pedal boats to rent. Entry was $10 a day for an out-of-state car and $5 for a Delaware one when we checked.

Trap Pond State Park, near Laurel. A pond where Delaware State Parks lists pickerel, crappie, bluegill, catfish, American eel and largemouth bass. Its page says the boat ramp is closed because the pond was lowered after a spillway failure; we could not confirm whether it has reopened.

The rule on every public pond. Boats must run at no-wake speed on all of Delaware’s freshwater public ponds. Delaware’s bass limits are in its fishing guide, which we could not read today — check with DNREC.

Nearest town: Bear, for Lums Pond.

Confirmed with Delaware DNREC and Delaware State Parks, 23 November 2026.

The licence and the limits, plainly

Each state sells its own licence. On Kerr Lake alone, Virginia and North Carolina honour each other’s.

Virginia

For a visitor. Nonresident freshwater licence $72; five consecutive days $36; one day $20.

For a Virginia resident. One-year freshwater licence $28; five days $19.

Children. No licence under 16, resident or not.

Boats. A boat registered in another state may be used in Virginia for up to 90 days in a row on that registration. When you leave any water, Virginia law says all water plants must be removed from the boat, trailer and gear.

Confirmed with the Virginia Department of Wildlife Resources, 23 November 2026 — its licence page (updated 1 October 2026), reciprocal-licence and general-regulation pages. Virginia raised most resident fees by $5 from 1 October 2026; check the price when you buy.

North Carolina (Kerr Lake)

Licence. A North Carolina licence is honoured on the whole of Kerr Lake, as a Virginia one is. Nobody under 16 needs a fishing licence in North Carolina. We could not confirm North Carolina’s current nonresident prices on its own site today — check with the North Carolina Wildlife Resources Commission.

Confirmed with the North Carolina Wildlife Resources Commission, 23 November 2026.

West Virginia

For a visitor. Nonresident fishing licence $37; one day $3; a trout stamp ($16) if you fish for trout. The 2026 summary also lists a $13 nonresident Conservation/Law Enforcement stamp — ask DNR which stamps your licence needs.

For a West Virginia resident. Fishing licence $19; five days $8; trout stamp $10; conservation stamp $5.

Children. No licence under 15, resident or not.

Confirmed with the West Virginia Division of Natural Resources, 23 November 2026 — its 2026 fishing regulations summary.

Maryland

For a visitor. Nonresident nontidal (freshwater) licence $55, or your home state’s fee for the same licence if that is higher; seven days $45 and three days $35 at Maryland’s June 2025 prices, which can also be higher depending on your home state. A trout stamp ($30 for nonresidents) is needed to catch or keep trout.

For a Maryland resident. Nontidal licence $32; seven days $16; trout stamp $20.

Children. No licence under 16.

Confirmed with the Maryland Department of Natural Resources, 23 November 2026 — its licence page and its fee announcement of 29 April 2025.

Delaware

Licence. DNREC’s fee page lists $20 for a nonresident licence and $12.50 for seven days; residents aged 16 to 64 pay $8.50. Residents 16 to 64 and nonresidents 16 and over need one. Delaware has no reciprocity with any other state.

Confirmed with Delaware DNREC, 23 November 2026. The fee page was last updated in 2022; check the price when you buy.

The towns that serve each lake

Questions

Which licence do I need to fish Kerr Lake (Buggs Island)?

Either a Virginia or a North Carolina fishing licence. The two states honour each other's licences on Kerr and Lake Gaston, including the tributaries you can reach by boat from the main lake. Virginia's rules apply in the Virginia part of the lake and North Carolina's in the North Carolina part. Checked with the Virginia Department of Wildlife Resources on 23 November 2026.

What does a Virginia fishing licence cost a visitor?

On the Virginia Department of Wildlife Resources licence page on 23 November 2026: a nonresident freshwater licence $72, a nonresident five-day freshwater licence $36 and a one-day freshwater licence $20. Residents pay $28 a year. Nobody under 16 needs a licence. The page was updated on 1 October 2026, the day Virginia's new fees began, so check the price when you buy.

Can I keep a big striped bass on Smith Mountain Lake?

Two striped bass a day. From 1 June to 31 October there is no length limit. From 1 November to 31 May every striper between 30 and 40 inches must be released at once. Checked with the Virginia Department of Wildlife Resources on 23 November 2026.

When is walleye season on Deep Creek Lake?

Walleye may be kept all year except from 1 March to 15 April, when the season is closed on Deep Creek Lake. Maryland's Deep Creek page gives a limit of five a day and ten in possession, with a size rule that protects fish between 18 and 21 inches. Checked with the Maryland Department of Natural Resources on 23 November 2026.

What does a West Virginia fishing licence cost a visitor?

West Virginia's 2026 regulations summary lists a nonresident fishing licence at $37 and a one-day licence at $3. A nonresident who fishes for trout also needs a $16 trout stamp. Residents and nonresidents under 15 need no licence. Checked with the West Virginia Division of Natural Resources on 23 November 2026.

What we could not check today, and will not pretend to have

The part of a fishing page most sites leave out.

Seven honest gaps

Low water and closed ramps. On 23 November 2026 low water had closed ramps on Kerr and threatened the ramp at Lake Anna State Park, and DWR planned work at its three Smith Mountain Lake landings from 30 November. Ramps reopen as the water rises; check the park, Corps or DWR page the day before you tow.

North Carolina’s prices. We could not read North Carolina’s current nonresident licence prices on its own site, so we print none. Check with NC Wildlife.

Where the Kerr striper rules end upstream. DWR’s Buggs Island page and its 2026 creel table describe the upper boundary differently (Leesville Dam on one, Difficult Creek on the other). If you fish far up the Staunton River, ask DWR.

Delaware’s bass limits and fees. Delaware’s bass limits are only in its fishing guide, which we could not read, and its fee page dates from 2022. Whether the Trap Pond ramp has reopened we could not confirm.

Best months. None of these agencies publishes a month-by-month calendar. Where the months table says “our guidance”, it is ours.

Businesses we could not list. We found marinas and guides on Kerr Lake with working websites, but none showing anything dated 2026, so we list none for Kerr; Summersville Lake Marina’s own site would not open for us. That is not a judgement on them.

Small towns and hotel lists. Moneta, Clarksville and McHenry are small, so we added bigger neighbours. Our hotel buttons are pinned to town numbers checked two ways with our own resolver; we do not open Trip.com’s hotel-list pages to count the hotels, so small towns may show few.
