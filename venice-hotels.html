Venice’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Venice — Best Areas & Hotels

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated July 2026

Venice is a city of six sestieri, and the one you pick shapes the whole trip more than the hotel does. We've mapped the places to stay by area and style — from Grand Canal palazzi to quiet, better-value Cannaregio — so you can jump straight to your kind of visit. Every pick includes an honest note and a Book Me link that opens that exact hotel on Trip.com; just choose your dates.

San Marco — Central, On the Action

Best for first-timers who want St Mark's Square, the big sights, and the gondola postcard within a short walk — and don't mind crowds or higher prices.

Baglioni Hotel Luna

$$$$ LuxuryNear St Mark'sHistoricClassic Venetian

A short stroll from Piazza San Marco and the waterfront, the Luna traces its history back centuries and wears it in the Murano chandeliers and frescoed ballroom. It's grand in the old-world Venetian manner rather than the minimalist one — a fitting base if you want to be steps from the square without being on it.

Hotel Concordia

$$$ UpscaleOn St Mark's SquareCentralSquare views

The Concordia's claim to fame is genuine: it is the only hotel with rooms that look directly onto Piazza San Marco and the basilica. You pay for the address and rooms run classic-cozy rather than large, but for waking up over the most famous square in Italy there's simply nothing closer.

Hotel Flora

$$ Mid-rangeGarden courtyardFamily-runNear St Mark's

Tucked down a lane off the smart Calle Larga XXII Marzo, the family-run Flora is beloved for its planted courtyard garden — a rare pocket of green and quiet a few minutes from the square. Rooms vary in size in the way old Venetian buildings do, but the warmth and the location are the enduring draw.

On the Grand Canal — Luxury & Views

Best for a special-occasion splurge, when the water view from your window is the whole point.

The Gritti Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel

$$$$ LuxuryGrand CanalHistoric palazzoIconic terrace

A 15th-century palazzo on the Grand Canal near the Salute, the Gritti is Venetian grand-hotel tradition at its most polished, from the antique-filled salons to the fabled canal-side terrace. It has hosted writers and royalty for generations, and a Bellini on that terrace at dusk remains one of the city's great rituals.

Aman Venice

$$$$ Ultra-luxuryGrand CanalPalazzo PapadopoliPrivate garden

Set inside the 16th-century Palazzo Papadopoli, Aman Venice is hushed, discreet, and extraordinary — frescoed piano nobile ceilings, just two dozen rooms, and one of the very few private gardens in the historic center. It is the choice when you want the grandeur of a Grand Canal palazzo with almost none of the crowd.

Book Me on Trip.com ›

Ca’ Sagredo Hotel

$$$$ LuxuryGrand CanalFrescoed palazzoNear Rialto

A former noble residence turned hotel-museum on the Grand Canal in Cannaregio, Ca’ Sagredo is hung with original frescoes and a monumental staircase you'll want to photograph. Its water-facing rooms and terrace look straight across to the Rialto market bustle — grand history at a notch below the very top prices.

Cannaregio — Quieter & Better Value

Best for repeat visitors and anyone who wants real neighborhood life, local bacari wine bars, and lower prices — while staying an easy walk from the sights.

Palazzo Abadessa

$$$ UpscaleHistoric palazzoWalled gardenRomantic

A 16th-century palazzo run as an intimate residence, the Abadessa is famous for its large private garden — something almost unheard of in Venice — and for salon-style rooms dressed with antiques and silk. Down a quiet Cannaregio lane yet close to the Ca’ d'Oro, it feels like staying with a well-connected Venetian family.

Hotel Antico Doge

$$ Mid-rangeNear RialtoVenetian styleGood value

Once home to a Venetian doge, this small hotel sits just over the bridge from the Rialto in the quieter reaches of Cannaregio. Rooms lean into damask-and-gilt Venetian décor, and the central-yet-calm position makes it a dependable mid-range base for exploring on foot.

Hotel Ai Mori d’Oriente

$$ Mid-rangeCanal-sideNear the GhettoQuiet

On a peaceful fondamenta near the historic Jewish Ghetto, Ai Mori d’Oriente pairs an Orientalist-tinged Venetian look with a genuinely local, unhurried stretch of Cannaregio. It's a short walk to the vaporetto and station yet a world away from the San Marco crush — solid value for the setting.

Dorsoduro — Arty & Canal-Calm

Best for art lovers and slow travelers — home to the Accademia and Guggenheim, student cafés on Campo Santa Margherita, and a calmer, more residential pace.

Ca’ Maria Adele

$$$$ Luxury boutiqueNear the SaluteRomanticDesign rooms

A jewel-box boutique of a dozen rooms beside the Salute church, Ca’ Maria Adele is one of the city's most romantic small hotels, known for its theatrical themed rooms and dawn-quiet corner of Dorsoduro. Intimate and design-forward, it's a honeymoon favorite that trades ballroom grandeur for atmosphere.

Pensione Accademia Villa Maravege

$$$ UpscaleGarden villaNear the AccademiaClassic

A 17th-century villa with two gardens where the side canals meet, the Pensione Accademia is a long-running Venice institution — the sort of leafy, book-lined retreat guests return to for decades. Rooms are traditional rather than flashy; the draw is sipping a drink in the garden as boats slip past.

Ca’ Pisani Hotel

$$$ UpscaleDesign hotelArt DecoNear the Accademia

A rare modernist note in a Gothic city, Ca’ Pisani is a 1930s-inspired Art Deco design hotel steps from the Accademia galleries. Clean lines, vintage pieces, and a small rooftop terrace make it the pick for travelers who want contemporary style without leaving the quiet of Dorsoduro.

Near the Train Station — Convenient & Value

Best for short stays, early trains, and anyone hauling luggage — step off at Santa Lucia and you're checked in before the water taxi would have arrived.

Hotel Antiche Figure

$$ Mid-rangeGrand Canal viewOpposite the stationGreat location

Directly across the Grand Canal from Santa Lucia station, Antiche Figure offers something unusual at this price: real canal views in a restored 15th-century building. The convenience is unbeatable for arrivals and departures, and the classic Venetian rooms punch above the three-star bracket.

Hotel Carlton on the Grand Canal

$$$ UpscaleGrand CanalRooftop terraceNear the station

A larger, full-service four-star a couple of minutes from the station and its own vaporetto stop, the Carlton pairs a Grand Canal frontage with a rooftop terrace and generous breakfast. A practical, comfortable choice when you want space and amenities close to your arrival point.

Hotel Amadeus

$$ Mid-rangeGardenNear the stationReliable

A dependable four-star a short walk from Santa Lucia along the Lista di Spagna, the Amadeus adds a quiet internal garden to its easy-access location. Rooms are traditional and well kept — an unfussy, comfortable base for travelers who value convenience over character.

Budget & Guesthouses

Best for travelers who'd rather spend on cicchetti and spritz than the room — honest, well-located stays that keep Venice affordable.

Generator Venice

$ BudgetDesign hostelGiudeccaLagoon views

On Giudecca island facing San Marco across the water, Generator is a stylish hostel with both dorms and private rooms, plus a bar and lagoon views most budget stays can only dream of. The vaporetto ride to the center is part of the appeal — a sociable, design-minded base for younger and solo travelers.

Book Me on Trip.com ›

Hotel Al Piave

$$ ValueFamily-runCastelloNear St Mark's

A warm, family-run favorite in Castello a few minutes east of St Mark's, Al Piave has earned a loyal following for its genuine hospitality and family rooms — a rarity in Venice. It's simple and central, the kind of honest small hotel guidebooks have quietly recommended for years.

3749 Ponte Chiodo

$$ ValueGuesthouseCannaregioCanal-side

A tiny, much-loved guesthouse in Cannaregio beside one of the last bridges in Venice without a railing, Ponte Chiodo charms guests with its little canal-side garden and personal, host-run feel. Just a handful of rooms, so it books up — but few places at this price feel so authentically Venetian.
