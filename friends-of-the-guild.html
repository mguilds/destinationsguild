Friends of the Guild

The hotels, management companies, associations and independent travel businesses who back the Guild Travel Library — and who we are glad to send travellers to.

🤝

What this page is

The Guild Travel Library is free to read and always will be. It exists because working people in this industry — hoteliers, operators, associations — think a properly researched, honestly written travel library is worth having.

This page is where we name them. Every entry is a real business with a real website, listed because we would be happy to recommend it to a traveller. Nothing here is bought, and nothing here is sold.

Founding Friends

The Library opened its doors in 2026. These are the businesses who stood behind it first — and the list is open.

How it works

  1. You tell us about your business. Name, website, and a sentence on what you do. The form is below and it takes a minute.
  2. We read it, and we check the site. We only list businesses we can stand behind. If we are not sure, we will write and ask rather than guess.
  3. You go on the page, with a link to your website and a line in your own words.
Do you have to link back to us? No. Most friends of the Guild do put a link to destinationsguild.com on their own resources or partners page, and we are genuinely grateful when they do — it is the single most useful thing anyone can do for a young library. But a listing here is not conditional on it. Both links are freely given.
