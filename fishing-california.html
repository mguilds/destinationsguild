California Fishing — Every Kind of Water, One Licence

Surf, piers, half-day boats, the deep sea, mountain lakes and salmon rivers. Start here: what licence you need, what to fish this month, and the rules that catch visitors out.

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Checked against the California Department of Fish and Wildlife, 4 November 2026

No other state offers so many kinds of fishing. In one week here you could catch grunion by hand on a beach at midnight, sail for tuna out of San Diego, drop a line off a free pier, troll for mackinaw on Lake Tahoe and cast to wild trout under Mount Shasta. All of it runs on one state licence, and if you are under 16, or on a public pier, you do not even need that.

This is the front door to the California shelf of the Guild’s fishing library. Below you will find six guides, a picker for what to fish in any month, and the licence and rules in plain words. Everything that is a rule or a price was read from the California Department of Fish and Wildlife on 4 November 2026, and is dated. Where we could not confirm something, we say so.

South or north?

Southern California, below Point Conception, is the place for a warm, easy day: calm seas by Pacific standards, many boats, bass, yellowtail and summer tuna, and a pier in most beach towns.

Northern California is colder and wilder: rockfish, Dungeness crab, salmon when allowed, albacore far offshore, the great reservoirs and the best rivers in the state. Its coast is dangerous from the rocks. Please read our safety section.

The licence, plainly

One licence covers salt and fresh water. All prices are CDFW’s 2026 fees.

Who needs one

Under 16: no licence. 16 and over: yes, except on a public pier in ocean or bay waters. Residents are people who have lived continuously in California for six months or more; visitors pay the higher annual fee, but the one-day and two-day prices are the same for all.

Confirmed with CDFW, 4 November 2026 — 2026 regulations: “Any person who is 16 years of age or older must have a sport fishing license to take any kind of fish, mollusk, invertebrate, amphibian or crustacean in California, except for persons angling from a public pier in ocean or bay waters.” CDFW’s licensing pages list licences as valid 365 days from the date of purchase. Most fees include agent and application charges. Buy online from CDFW, by telephone on (800) 565-1458, or from a licensed agent. CDFW’s own offices do not take cash; agents do.

Shared waters

Colorado River. On the California-Arizona stretch, either state’s licence is good from either bank or a boat. See our Arizona lakes guide for Havasu and Mohave. Lake Tahoe and Topaz Lake. Either a California licence or a Nevada licence, and Nevada also requires a trout stamp.

The rules that catch visitors out

Eight things worth knowing before you cast. As of 4 November 2026.

Salt water

Marine Protected Areas. You may not fish a State Marine Reserve, from shore or boat, and some conservation areas are no-take too. Laguna Beach is the classic surprise: nearly the whole town shore is closed. See Laguna and CDFW’s map.

Rockfish have a calendar. Closed to boats statewide from 1 January to 31 March. South of Point Conception, from October to December, boats may take only deep shelf and slope species beyond 50 fathoms. Anglers on shore are exempt.

Ocean salmon. Closed for the rest of 2026. Dates for 2027 are expected in April 2027.

Dungeness crab. Opens 7 November 2026, with traps restricted from Oregon to Point Conception until at least 1 January 2027, and a delay around Cape Mendocino. See the details.

Abalone. Closed until at least 1 April 2036. Do not take one.

Fresh water

Streams in winter. From 16 November to the Friday before the last Saturday in April: open on most general streams, but zero trout and artificial barbless lures only. Lakes stay open. The detail.

Sturgeon. Catch and release only, zero limit, with a report card and one barbless hook. Green sturgeon may not be taken at all.

Your own boat. Golden and quagga mussels have made launching a patchwork: Lake Casitas asks for a 35-day quarantine. Rent at the lake if you can, and call first if you cannot. South · North.

Confirmed with CDFW, 4 November 2026 — 2026 Ocean and Freshwater regulations; regional summaries updated 2 November 2026; crab notice of 30 October 2026. Sturgeon: “Daily limit: Zero fish.” Abalone: “The red abalone season is closed until at least April 1, 2036.” Night fishing is allowed in most waters, with exceptions: read section 3.00 of the Freshwater booklet.

Eating your catch

The state’s health office, OEHHA, publishes advice for most waters. Women aged 18 to 49 and children have the tightest limits.

The short version

Lakes. Mercury is the common worry, and black bass the usual fish. Where a lake has no advice of its own, the statewide advice says women 18 to 49 and children should not eat largemouth bass.

Coast. White croaker from Santa Monica Beach to Seal Beach Pier: do not eat, for all groups. San Francisco Bay and the Delta. Sharks, striped bass and white sturgeon are among the fish to avoid for women and children; no one should eat fish from Lauritzen Channel in Richmond or the Port of Stockton.

Look up your water on the OEHHA advisories page before you keep a fish.

Questions

How much is a California fishing licence in 2026?

An annual licence is $64.54 for residents and $174.14 for non-residents, valid for 365 days from purchase. A one-day licence is $21.09 and a two-day is $32.40 for anyone. A ten-day licence, for non-residents only, is $64.54.

At what age do you need a fishing licence in California?

Sixteen. Anyone under 16 fishes without a licence, but needs a report card for steelhead, for sturgeon, for spiny lobster and for salmon on the Klamath, Trinity and Smith.

Do you need a licence to fish from a pier in California?

Not from a public pier in ocean or bay waters, at any age. Jetties, breakwaters and sea walls do not count as piers. All other rules, such as limits and sizes, still apply.

Do you need a licence on a party boat in California?

Yes, from age 16; we found no exception for party boats. Many landings sell a one-day licence at check-in, but Riptide in Half Moon Bay says licences are not sold on its boat, so ask, or buy online first.

When are California’s Free Fishing Days?

The next are Saturday 3 July and Saturday 4 September 2027. On those days no licence is needed, but all the other rules apply and report cards are still required.

Does an Arizona fishing licence work on the California side of the Colorado River?

Yes. Along the California-Arizona border, California accepts either state’s licence, from either bank or a boat. We could not confirm the same for the stretch shared with Nevada.

Where is the best fishing in California for a beginner?

A half-day boat from San Diego, Newport Beach, Dana Point or Long Beach. The crew finds the fish and baits your hook, the rod is for hire, and a one-day licence is $21.09. A public pier is even simpler, needing no licence at all.

The part of a fishing page most sites leave out.

Four honest gaps

Party boats. CDFW’s only stated licence exception is public piers, so we read that as requiring a licence on any boat from 16. No CDFW sentence says so outright.

The Nevada stretch of the Colorado. California’s booklet names only Arizona. Check before you fish the California-Nevada border.

The short 2027 steelhead card. It goes on sale on 15 November 2026 at a price we could not confirm.

The legal text. CDFW’s booklets say they are not to be used as legal documents, and we could not reach the official code. Rules can also change in season: CDFW In-Season Changes.
