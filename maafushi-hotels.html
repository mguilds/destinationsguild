Where to Stay in Maafushi

Where to Stay in Maafushi — The Guesthouse Island

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated November 2026

Maafushi is a Maldivian fishing village about half an hour south of the airport that happened to be first through the door when guesthouses were allowed on inhabited islands, and it changed who gets to come to this country. It is a working island with a mosque, a school, a football pitch and a few hundred residents, and it also has a bikini beach, two dozen small hotels, a dive centre on every other corner and a boat leaving for somewhere good every morning. Stay at the south end for the beach, in the village for the cheapest rooms and the best food, and near the harbour if your days start early.

Choosing Your Area in Maafushi

The island is a few hundred metres end to end, so this is a question of what you want outside the door.

The south end and the bikini beach

The marked bikini beach and the swimming area are at the southern tip, and the guesthouses nearest it charge a little more for the walk being thirty seconds instead of five minutes. Several have roof terraces that look down the length of the lagoon.

This is the holiday end of the island and the part that feels least like a village.

The village

The middle of the island, where people actually live: sandy lanes, small shops, cafes serving short eats and curries, and guesthouses tucked between houses. Rooms are cheapest here and the food is both better and a quarter of the price of the tourist-facing places.

You are three or four minutes from the beach in any direction. Mornings begin with the call to prayer.

The harbour side

The northern and western shore, where the speedboats and the ferry come in and the dive boats load. Convenient if you are diving every day or leaving early, and the sunsets from this side are the island’s best.

It is the working edge of the island, so expect engines at first light.

Know Before You Book

The things that decide whether a Maafushi week works for you.

The rules are the island’s, not the hotel’s

Swimwear on the bikini beach only; shoulders and knees covered in the village; no alcohol sold anywhere on the island and none to be brought into the country. Guesthouses get around the last one with trips to a floating bar offshore or day passes to a nearby resort.

This is not a resort pretending to be a village. It is a village that lets you stay, and the difference is the whole point of coming.

Getting here, and getting back

Speedboat from the airport, roughly half an hour, arranged by your guesthouse and usually including the terminal pick-up. The public ferry is a great deal cheaper, takes about an hour and a half, and does not sail every day — check the schedule before you build a plan on it.

Speedboats stop running in the evening. If your flight lands late, plan a night near the airport rather than assuming a crossing.

What the excursions are actually like

Half-day snorkelling with turtles, sandbank picnics, dolphin cruises, night fishing, whale-shark trips down to Ari and resort day passes — all sold on every corner, all leaving most mornings, and all cheaper than the same trip booked from a resort. The water is the reason you came, and this is how you get out into it.

Agree the price, the inclusions and the return time in advance. Good operators will tell you the wildlife rules before you ask.

Maafushi Hotel FAQ

The questions travellers ask most before booking a guesthouse on Maafushi.

Is Maafushi a good place to stay in the Maldives?

It is the best-known of the guesthouse islands and the easiest introduction to the local-island Maldives. You get warm sea, a bikini beach, a reef, a full choice of excursions and a bed for a small fraction of a resort rate, half an hour from the airport by speedboat. What you do not get is a private island, silence, or alcohol. If that trade sounds right, Maafushi is the obvious first try.

How do you get to Maafushi from the airport?

By speedboat in about half an hour, or by the much cheaper public ferry, which takes around an hour and a half and does not run every day. Nearly every guesthouse will book the speedboat transfer for you and most include a pick-up at the terminal. Check the day of the week before you count on the ferry, and remember that the last speedboat of the day leaves well before midnight.

Can you wear a bikini on Maafushi?

Only on the marked bikini beach. Maafushi is an inhabited Maldivian island, so swimwear is confined to that stretch of sand and elsewhere you cover shoulders and knees. The bikini beach is a proper piece of beach with loungers and a swimming area, not a token gesture, and the rule is signposted. On an excursion boat or a sandbank trip it no longer applies.

Is there alcohol on Maafushi?

Not on the island. No inhabited island in the Maldives sells alcohol, and you may not bring your own into the country. What guesthouses do instead is run a trip to a floating bar moored offshore, or sell you a day pass to a nearby resort, where the ordinary resort rules apply. It is a workaround rather than an oversight, and it costs what you would expect.
