Mexican Riviera Cruises

The Mexican Riviera is the West Coast’s warm‑water getaway — sailing from Los Angeles, Long Beach or San Diego to Mexico’s Pacific resort towns. Think beaches, tacos and tequila without a passport‑heavy long‑haul.

Best time to cruise

October through April is prime — warm, dry and sunny, and a welcome winter escape for Californians. Summer is hotter and doubles as shoulder‑season value.
