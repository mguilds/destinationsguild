Port Elizabeth’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Port Elizabeth (Gqeberha) — Best Areas & Hotels

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Port Elizabeth — officially renamed Gqeberha — anchors Nelson Mandela Bay with long, safe city beaches and a friendly, easygoing feel, and it’s the gateway to the malaria-free game reserves of the Eastern Cape and the western end of the Garden Route. Almost all visitors stay in Summerstrand, the beachfront strip along Hobie Beach and the Boardwalk. We’ve grouped the city’s most dependable stays by the way you like to travel — luxury, boutique, value, family, adults-only and pet-friendly — each with an honest note and a Book Me link that opens that exact property on Trip.com.

Luxury

Port Elizabeth’s smartest hotels sit on the Summerstrand beachfront, pairing sea views and pools with the city’s best dining and entertainment nearby.

$$$$ LuxurySummerstrandBeside the BoardwalkSpa & pool

The city’s flagship five-star, overlooking Hobie Beach and attached to the lively Boardwalk entertainment complex, with elegant rooms, a spa, a pool and restaurants and a casino on the doorstep. Its beachfront position and full amenities make it the standout luxury address in town. The definitive choice when you want polish, sea views and plenty to do close by.
