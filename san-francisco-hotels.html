Where to Stay in San Francisco

Where to Stay in San Francisco — Union Square, Nob Hill, the Wharf & the Presidio

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated November 2026

San Francisco is a small city of steep hills, and that shapes where you should sleep. The cable cars — which the city’s transit agency says were invented here and named a National Historic Landmark in 1964 — climb from Market Street over Nob Hill to Fisherman’s Wharf, and that line is the spine most first-time visitors follow. Pick your base by what you want outside the door: shops and theatres at Union Square, grand old hotels on Nob Hill, the bay and the Alcatraz ferry at the Wharf, or quiet streets and trails in Pacific Heights and the Presidio. This page sorts the areas and names the hotels we could confirm today.

Choosing Your Area in San Francisco

Four bases, each suited to a different trip.

Union Square and Downtown

The shopping and theatre centre of the city, around Union Square and Powell Street, running down to Market Street and the Financial District. The San Francisco Municipal Transportation Agency (SFMTA) says two of the three cable car lines start at Powell and Market and run to the Fisherman’s Wharf area.

Stay here for a first visit, for transit in every direction, and for the widest choice of hotels at every price. Streets change character block by block, so look at the exact address.

Nob Hill

The hill above Union Square, crossed by the third cable car line, which the SFMTA says runs along California Street from Market Street to Van Ness Avenue. Home to several of the city’s grand historic hotels.

Stay here for views, quiet nights and a classic San Francisco stay. Be ready for the climb if you walk back up.

Fisherman’s Wharf and the waterfront

The northern waterfront, from the piers round to Ghirardelli Square. The National Park Service says the Alcatraz ferry leaves from Pier 33, operated by Alcatraz City Cruises, and that the Muni F Line streetcar runs along The Embarcadero past the ferry terminal and on through Fisherman’s Wharf.

Stay here with children, for the bay views, and for an early Alcatraz boat. Evenings are quieter than downtown.

Pacific Heights, the Marina and the Presidio

The residential northwest of the city, near the Golden Gate. The Presidio is a former army post that is now a national park site managed by the Presidio Trust, with coastal bluffs, trails and views of the bridge.

Stay here for a slower, greener trip and easy access to the Golden Gate Bridge. Allow time to reach downtown by bus or car.

Getting There & When to Go

The handful of San Francisco details that change how a trip goes.

Getting to San Francisco

The main airport is San Francisco International (SFO). SFO’s own website calls BART the fastest way to San Francisco; the BART station is in the International Terminal, and the free AirTrain links every terminal to it. BART says the ride to downtown takes about 30 minutes. SFO also lists Lyft and Uber pick-up on Level 5 of the Domestic Garage, taxis, and complimentary shuttles run by many local hotels — confirm with yours (all read 24 November 2026).

Getting around

Downtown, the waterfront and the cable car lines cover most first visits without a car. The SFMTA runs three cable car lines and publishes fares on its own website; board at the turntables at the end of each line or at the brown-and-white cable car signs. For Alcatraz, the National Park Service says the ferry leaves from Pier 33 and that there are commercial car parks nearby. If you do drive, ask your hotel about parking before you book.

When to go

The National Park Service describes the Golden Gate area’s seasons plainly: summer fog takes over the coast by July, while from mid-to-late September through October the skies are clear and sunny. Winter brings occasional rain. On Alcatraz, it advises a light jacket or sweater whatever the morning looks like, because fog and wind can arrive quickly. Pack layers in any month.

San Francisco Hotels FAQ

The questions travellers ask most before booking a room here.

Where is the best area to stay in San Francisco?

Union Square for a first visit, transit and the widest choice of hotels. Nob Hill for grand historic hotels and views. Fisherman’s Wharf for families, bay views and the Alcatraz ferry. Pacific Heights or the Presidio for a quieter, greener stay near the Golden Gate Bridge.

How do I get from SFO to my hotel?

SFO calls BART the fastest way into San Francisco. Take the free AirTrain to the BART station in the International Terminal; BART says the ride to downtown takes about 30 minutes. Lyft, Uber, taxis and some hotel shuttles are also available.

Do I need a car in San Francisco?

Not for a first visit centred on downtown, the waterfront and the cable cars. A car helps for day trips out of the city. If you bring one, ask your hotel about parking before you book.

Where do Alcatraz ferries leave from?

The National Park Service says the Alcatraz ferry leaves from Pier 33, run by Alcatraz City Cruises, and that the Muni F Line streetcar runs past the terminal. Hotels at Fisherman’s Wharf are the closest.

What is the best time of year to visit San Francisco?

The National Park Service says summer fog dominates the coast from July, while mid-to-late September through October brings clear, sunny skies. Pack layers whenever you go.
