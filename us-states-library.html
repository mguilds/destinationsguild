Bodhi & Nolie

Two ranger-hatted guides who ride shotgun through every state — so each book reads like a road trip with friends, not a wall of listings.

Bodhi

The Head · the story & the map

He's driven every back road twice and knows why the town got its name. Bodhi brings the why — the history, the route, and the fact that makes a place click.

Nolie

The Heart · the feel & the secret

Where Bodhi has the map, Nolie has the feel — which overlook at golden hour, where the locals actually eat, and which “tourist trap” is secretly worth it.

“Trust me on this one.”

You'll know them the same way every time — the evergreen flat-brim ranger hat, the gold badge, and the hand-stitched “Hi, I'm ___” nametag. An affectionate tip of the hat to the great park guides — never the official article.
