Where to Stay in Hiroshima — Best Hotels by Type

Do you want a high-rise luxury room with a view over the delta, a design boutique near the arcades, a friendly budget base by the station, or a room that welcomes the family dog? Luxury

Hiroshima’s top hotels are gracious high-rises with wide views over the river delta and, on a clear day, the Seto Inland Sea. Book here when you want polished service, a proper restaurant floor, and a calm room to come back to after a moving day at the Peace Park.

RIHGA Royal Hotel Hiroshima

Long the tallest hotel in the city, the RIHGA Royal rises right beside Hiroshima Castle and a short stroll from the Peace Memorial Park, with high-floor rooms that look out over the whole delta. It is a genuine grand hotel — several restaurants, an indoor pool, and the kind of unhurried service that suits a special trip. A dependable, central choice when you want the best address in town.

Budget & Value

Hiroshima is a kind city for careful budgets: clean, friendly guesthouses and hostels near the station cost a fraction of the towers and put you a tram ride from everything. You trade a lobby for a communal kitchen and good local tips — and spend the savings on Miyajima and okonomiyaki.

A well-run, spotlessly kept hostel a couple of minutes from Hiroshima Station, offering both dorm beds and simple private and family rooms with tatami options. Hiroshima Hotels — Frequently Asked

What is the best area to stay in Hiroshima?

For a first visit, stay between Hiroshima Station and the Peace Memorial Park. The station area is the most convenient for luggage and day trips by Shinkansen, while the central Kamiya-cho and Hondori district puts you within walking distance of the Peace Park, the Atomic Bomb Dome, shopping arcades, and the best restaurants.

Is Hiroshima easy to get around on foot?

Yes. Central Hiroshima is flat and compact, and the covered Hondori arcade and Peace Park are an easy stroll apart. For longer hops the city has a simple, cheap tram network, and the ferry and train to Miyajima are straightforward, so most visitors barely need a taxi.

When is the best time to visit Hiroshima?

Spring, especially late March into April for cherry blossoms, and autumn from October into November for mild weather and color are the most pleasant. Summers are hot and humid, and the August 6 Peace Memorial ceremony draws crowds and books out hotels well in advance, so reserve early if you plan to be there then.
