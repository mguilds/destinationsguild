Have the wonders always been the same?

Not at all — and that’s the surprising part. The ancient list was never truly fixed. Greek and Roman writers each kept their own roll of marvels, and older versions swapped in the Walls of Babylon or the Ishtar Gate in place of one wonder or another. The familiar seven only settled into place well after the classical age.

Then time did the editing. Six of the seven ancient wonders were toppled by earthquakes, burned, or quarried away, until only the Great Pyramid remained. So in 2007 a Swiss foundation invited the whole world to vote for a new list of wonders that were still standing — and more than 100 million people did. That vote gave us the New Seven Wonders on the top shelf.

There are other sevens still being written, too: the Seven Natural Wonders, the New Seven Wonders of Nature, and the engineering wonders of the modern age — room enough for more shelves in this Library as the Guild grows.
