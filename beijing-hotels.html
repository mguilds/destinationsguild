Beijing’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Beijing — Best Areas & Hotels

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Beijing is enormous, and the style of hotel you choose shapes the trip as much as the neighbourhood. Do you want a grand tower near the Forbidden City and Wangfujing, a courtyard hideaway in the old hutong lanes, or a family base with a pool and a kids’ club? We’ve grouped Beijing’s most enduring, well-run hotels by style and character, each with an honest note and a Book Me link that opens that exact property on Trip.com so you can just choose your dates.

Luxury

Beijing’s grand hotels pair five-star polish with a sense of the imperial city — sweeping lobbies, destination restaurants and service that turns a stay into part of the story. Book here when you want the hotel to be a highlight in its own right.

Waldorf Astoria Beijing

$$$$ LuxuryWangfujingNear Forbidden CityHutong villas

A discreet, beautifully finished flagship steps off Wangfujing and a short walk from the Forbidden City, with a hushed spa, an excellent bar and a cluster of restored courtyard villas out back. Impeccable service and a superb location make it Beijing’s benchmark address. Best for travellers who want central luxury with a whisper of old Beijing.

Boutique

For character over scale, Beijing’s boutique hotels tuck you into the old city — grey-brick courtyards, drum-tower views and personal service in the hutong lanes near the Forbidden City. Ideal for independent travellers who want atmosphere and a real sense of place.

The Orchid Beijing

$$$ BoutiqueGulou / Drum TowerCourtyardRooftop terraces

A beloved little courtyard hotel in the hutong beside the Drum Tower, run with warmth and design flair, with leafy rooftop terraces and one of the neighbourhood’s best casual restaurants. Rooms are calm and contemporary within the old grey-brick shell. Perfect for travellers who want to live in the lanes of old Beijing, not above a highway.

$$$ BoutiqueBeside the Forbidden CityDesign-ledRooftop bar

A sleek design hotel almost against the moat of the Forbidden City, whose rooftop bar and hot tub look straight across the palace walls — a remarkable spot for a sundowner. Rooms are compact but stylish, and the location is unbeatable for early-morning sightseeing. Best for couples and design lovers who want to be right at the heart of imperial Beijing.

Budget & Value

Beijing rewards a careful budget: a well-placed mid-range hotel near Wangfujing or the Forbidden City costs a fraction of the grand towers and puts the sights on your doorstep. You trade marble lobbies for location and reliability — and spend the savings on Peking duck and the Wall.

Novotel Beijing Peace

$$ Mid-rangeWangfujingWalk to Forbidden CityReliable

A dependable, well-run four-star just off the Wangfujing shopping street, within easy walking distance of the Forbidden City and a subway stop for everywhere else. Rooms are comfortable and modern, with helpful English-speaking staff and good breakfasts. A sensible, central value pick for first-timers who want to walk to the sights and save on the room.

Family-Friendly

With kids in tow, space, pools and easy dining matter more than a fashionable address. These hotels combine family rooms, indoor pools and play areas with locations that make Beijing’s big sights — and a Great Wall day trip — manageable with children.

Kerry Hotel Beijing

$$$ UpscaleCBDHuge kids’ adventure zonePool

A firm family favourite thanks to one of the largest hotel play areas in the city — an indoor-outdoor adventure zone with slides, climbing frames and games — plus a big pool and generous rooms. Parents get a lively bar and excellent restaurants once the children are worn out. Best for families who want the kids entertained without leaving the building.

Adults-Only & Romantic Retreats

For a grown-up trip — a honeymoon, an anniversary or simply a quiet escape — these are Beijing’s most romantic, serene addresses, long on spa time, fine dining and calm. Not formally child-free, but tranquil and adult in mood; confirm any specifics when you book.

Aman Summer Palace, Beijing

$$$$ LuxuryBeside the Summer PalaceSereneSpa

A hushed, courtyard-style retreat in restored pavilions right beside the historic Summer Palace, with its own gate into the imperial gardens, a beautiful spa and deep, meditative calm. It feels a world away from the city yet is genuinely central to a great sight. The definitive romantic splurge for couples who want peace, privacy and heritage.

Pet-Friendly Stays

Travelling with a dog is still uncommon in China, but a few international hotels genuinely welcome pets. These are reliable choices — always confirm the current pet policy, size limits and any cleaning fee directly on the booking page before you arrive.

Grand Hyatt Beijing

$$$ UpscaleWangfujingPets welcomeLagoon pool

A large, polished hotel above the Oriental Plaza mall on Wangfujing, moments from the Forbidden City, known for its dramatic tropical-lagoon pool under a painted-sky ceiling. As part of Hyatt’s pet-friendly program it welcomes dogs, and the central location makes walks easy. A comfortable, dog-friendly base right in the middle of the action.
