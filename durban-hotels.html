Where to Stay in Durban — Best Hotels by Type

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Durban wears its warmth openly — a subtropical, Indian-Ocean city where the beaches are wide, the curries are famous and the sea stays swimmable most of the year. Whether you want a five-star suite above Umhlanga Rocks, a classic room on the Golden Mile, or a leafy boutique bolt-hole on the Berea ridge, we’ve grouped Durban’s most enduring, well-run hotels by the kind of stay you’re after. Durban hotels — quick questions

What is the best area to stay in Durban?

For upscale calm and a cleaner beach, base yourself in Umhlanga Rocks just north of the city. For the classic Durban beachfront, choose the Golden Mile along Marine Parade and North Beach, where the promenade, uShaka Marine World and the big beach hotels are. For a leafy, residential feel with good restaurants, look to Morningside and Musgrave on the Berea ridge.

Is Durban walkable?

The beachfront promenade along the Golden Mile is flat, paved and very walkable by day, and Umhlanga’s village and lagoon are pleasant on foot too. Beyond those strips, Durban is spread out and hilly, so you will rely on metered taxis or Uber to move between areas, and it is wise to avoid walking alone after dark.

When is the best time to visit Durban?

Durban is subtropical and warm all year. The sweet spots are autumn (April to June) and spring (September to October), when days are warm and dry and the sea is still swimmable. Summer (December to February) is hot, humid and prone to afternoon downpours, but the ocean is at its warmest and the city is at its liveliest.
