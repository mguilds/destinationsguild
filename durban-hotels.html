Durban’s Best Places to Stay

Where to Stay in Durban — Best Areas & Hotels

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Durban is all about the warm Indian Ocean. The Golden Mile beachfront runs along the city with its promenade, piers and surf, while glossy Umhlanga, a short drive north, offers the smartest hotels and a lighthouse beach. We’ve grouped Durban’s most dependable stays by the way you like to travel — luxury, boutique, value, family, adults-only, pet-friendly, beachfront, resort and spa — each with an honest note and a Book Me link that opens that exact property on Trip.com.

Luxury

Durban’s finest hotels cluster in Umhlanga, where colonial-style grandeur and modern towers look out over the warm ocean.

The Oyster Box

$$$$ LuxuryUmhlangaBeside the lighthouseIconic curry buffet

An colonial-era icon beside the Umhlanga lighthouse, the Oyster Box pairs richly detailed rooms with sea-facing pools, a renowned spa and a famous curry buffet. Beachfront, characterful and impeccably run, it is the grande dame of the KwaZulu-Natal coast. The definitive luxury stay when you want history, ceremony and the Indian Ocean at your feet.

The Capital Pearls

$$$$ LuxuryUmhlangaBeachfrontRooftop pool & spa

A sleek modern all-suite tower on the Umhlanga beachfront, with spacious contemporary suites, a rooftop pool and spa, and restaurants at the promenade below. Its polished, apartment-style rooms and prime seafront position make it a favourite for longer, self-sufficient stays. Best for travellers who want space, views and a beach on the doorstep in Umhlanga.
