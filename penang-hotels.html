Where to Stay in Penang

Where to Stay in Penang — Best Hotels & Areas

Curated by Our Guild Explorers · Updated August 2026

Penang gives you two very different stays in one island. George Town is a UNESCO-listed maze of clan houses, street art and legendary hawker food; the Batu Ferringhi strip on the north coast is where the resorts and the beach are. Pick heritage character or sand and sea — or split your trip between the two. Below are Penang’s most enduring hotels by style, each with an honest note and a Book Me link straight to Trip.com.

Luxury

Penang’s finest range from a seafront colonial legend in George Town to the grand resort names of Batu Ferringhi.

Eastern & Oriental Hotel

$$$$ LuxuryGeorge TownSeafront1885 heritage

The island’s legendary colonial hotel, founded by the Sarkies brothers in 1885, with a breezy seafront lawn, grand suites and a genuine sense of history on the edge of George Town. Beautifully restored and impeccably run, it is Penang’s signature splurge. Choose the heritage wing for the full period romance.

Boutique

George Town’s restored shophouses and clan mansions make it one of Asia’s great boutique-hotel cities.

Seven Terraces

$$$ BoutiqueGeorge TownPeranakan antiquesHeritage

A jewel-box boutique hotel in a row of restored Anglo-Chinese terrace houses, filled with Peranakan antiques and opening onto a serene courtyard pool. Suites are large and richly atmospheric, and the location among the temples and cafes is unbeatable. A romantic choice for lovers of heritage and design.
